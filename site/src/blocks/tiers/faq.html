Questions about plans and prices

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Send us a message through our contact page.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. You can cancel at any time in the billing settings of your account, and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, as set out in the Terms and Conditions.

How does the yearly plan work?

You pay once a year and get twelve months for the price of ten. Community, for example, costs €240 a year instead of €24 every month.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. You can move to another plan as your goals change. If you need help choosing, write to us through the contact page.

In which currency do I pay?

Prices are shown in your currency where we can, in euros, US dollars, Canadian dollars, Australian dollars or Moroccan dirhams, and you pay in the currency shown on the page.

What if my language is not open yet?

You can join its waitlist for free and pay nothing today. We email you as soon as classes in your language open, and English and German classes are already open.
