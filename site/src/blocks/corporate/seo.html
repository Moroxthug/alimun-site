Corporate language training online

Alimun teaches languages to teams in live online classes with a teacher. Here is how training for your company works.

A private group for your company

Your employees learn together in a private live class with a teacher, on fixed days at the same time each week, and managers can have one-to-one classes. Classes run in the browser, so people join from the office, from home or from another country.

Lessons about the work they do

The teacher builds lessons, notes and homework around your field and the situations your team meets at work, such as meetings, calls, emails and presentations.

Language training for relocation

Employees you relocate can start before they move and keep going after they arrive, so they settle in and work in the new language sooner. English and German classes are open today, and French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese are coming.

Practice for work and visa certificates

Many visas and work permits ask for a language certificate. Employees can practice tasks in the format of IELTS, B2 First and C1 Advanced for English, and Goethe B1, Goethe B2 and telc B1 for German. It is practice in the exam format, not official exam material.

Practice between classes

Between lessons, employees keep practicing with exercises at their level that are corrected instantly and flashcards with the vocabulary from class, and their teacher returns their homework corrected.
