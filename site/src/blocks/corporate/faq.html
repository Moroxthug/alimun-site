Questions about corporate training

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Send us a message through our contact page.

How much does corporate training cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your team and what you need. Contact us and we will put together a quote.

Can the classes be only for our employees?

Yes. Your employees learn in a private group for your company, and managers can also have one-to-one classes.

Which languages can our team learn?

English and German classes are open today. French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese are coming soon.

Can employees prepare for a language certificate?

They can practice tasks in the format of IELTS, B2 First and C1 Advanced for English, and Goethe B1, Goethe B2 and telc B1 for German. This is practice in the exam format, not official exam material: the exam itself is taken with the official provider.

What do employees need to join?

A computer or other device with a camera and a microphone. Classes run in the browser, so there is nothing to install.

How do we get started?

Write to us through the contact page with the size of your team, the languages and what you need, and we will reply by email with a quote.
