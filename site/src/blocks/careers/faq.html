Questions about teaching on Alimun

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Send us a message through our contact page.

Who can apply?

Language teachers. In the application you tell us the language you teach, your years of experience, your specialty and a short bio that your students will see.

Which languages can I teach?

You can apply to teach English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Mandarin, Japanese or Arabic. English and German classes are already running, and the other languages have a waitlist of learners.

How am I paid?

For each class you teach: a base rate per class plus an amount for every learner in the group. Your teacher dashboard shows your earnings and your payout history.

Do I choose my own hours?

Yes. You set your weekly availability in your teacher dashboard and teach online from wherever you are.

What does Alimun take care of?

The video classroom, the class schedule and student payments. Homework grades and feedback are drafted for you to check and send, so your time goes into live conversation with your group.

How long does the review take?

We reply by email within 48 hours of your application.
