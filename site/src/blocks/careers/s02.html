Why teach on Alimun?

Teach a group

Teach a structured group of about 6 to 35 learners instead of repeating the same private lesson again and again, and let the group practise together.

Grading help

Homework grades and feedback are drafted for you to check and send, so your time goes into live conversation.

Paid for every class

You are paid for each class you teach: a base rate per class plus an amount for every learner in the group, so a larger group earns more. Your teacher dashboard shows your earnings and payouts.

Flexible hours

Set your weekly availability in your teacher dashboard and teach from wherever you are. All you need is a computer with a camera and a microphone.
