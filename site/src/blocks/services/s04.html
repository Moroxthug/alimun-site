How it works

Start today
01/ 06
  2. 01

    Find your level

    A short placement test sets your level, from A1 to C1, and your exercises and classes match it.

  3. 02

    Learn live

    Video classes with your teacher and the same group, on fixed days and at a fixed time every week.

  4. 03

    Keep the class with you

    After each class your teacher shares the session notes, the new vocabulary and your homework.

  5. 04

    Hand in your assignments

    Your teacher sets homework with a due date. You submit it in your dashboard and get it back graded: a score, what went well, what to work on next and an example correction.

  6. 05

    Practise whenever you want

    A practice library at your level, with instant feedback, plus flashcards for the words from your lessons.

    • Multiple choice
    • Fill in the blank
    • Word matching
    • Listening
    • Speaking
    • Essay
  7. 06

    Tests and exam preparation

    Quizzes and tests are marked the moment you finish. Working towards a certificate? Practise tasks in the format of:

    • B2 First
    • C1 Advanced
    • IELTS
    • Goethe B1
    • Goethe B2
    • telc B1

    Not official exam material. Alimun is not affiliated with the exam boards.
