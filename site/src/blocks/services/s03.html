AI Features

Every tier includes the same core toolkit: AI essay and homework grading, instant test feedback, interactive speaking and grammar exercises, and a progress dashboard that shows exactly what to practice next.

Essay Grading

Essay Grading

Submit a written answer and the AI scores it out of 100, with feedback on what worked, one or two specific corrections and one tip.

Test & Quiz Grading

Test & Quiz Grading

Multiple-choice, fill-in and word-matching exercises are marked as soon as you answer, with the correct answer and a short explanation of the rule.

Homework Review

Homework Review

Your teacher grades your homework with help from AI (score, strengths, what to work on next, an example correction) and sends the feedback to your dashboard.

Speaking Practice

Speaking Practice

Read the sentence aloud. Speech recognition writes down what you said and shows how many of the expected words it heard.

Error Correction

Error Correction

Chat with the AI tutor in the language you’re learning. Each message is checked for grammar, vocabulary and spelling, and you see the corrected sentence with a short explanation.

Vocabulary Review

Vocabulary Review

Words from your teacher’s lesson notes become flashcards. Words you know come back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days; words you miss come back the next day.

Exam Prep

Exam Prep

Practice tasks in the format of B2 First, C1 Advanced and IELTS (English) and Goethe B1, Goethe B2 and telc B1 (German). Not official exam material; Alimun is not affiliated with the exam boards.

Placement Test

Placement Test

Eight AI-generated questions from A1 up to C1. Your result sets your level, so your exercises and classes match it.
