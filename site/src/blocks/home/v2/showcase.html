Everything you need between classes

Your dashboard brings together your live classes, your course book, practice, mock tests, flashcards, a listening and reading library, homework and your progress.

Live classes in your dashboard

Your next class with a Join button that opens 15 minutes before it starts, a calendar with your classes and homework due dates, and your teacher's notes after every class.

A course book that teaches, then checks

Each lesson is a few pages turned like a book: it explains, then checks with exercises, and every unit ends with a unit test. Explanations come in your own language.

Practice rounds that never repeat the same way

Short rounds built from the units you have reached. A gap exercise comes back as a word bank, matching as memory cards or a crossword, and the answers you got wrong come back first.

Mock tests in the real exam format

B2 First, C1 Advanced and IELTS for English, Goethe and telc for German, with a timer for each paper. When you finish, every answer shows with its explanation and every task with a model answer.

Flashcards that come back at the right time

The words of your course book, your classes and your tutor sessions, reviewed after one day, three days, a week, two weeks and a month. A word you miss comes back sooner.

A library to listen and read

Dialogues, interviews, talks, articles and stories for your level. The line being spoken is marked as you listen, and any word in a text shows its meaning when you tap it.

Homework, feedback and your progress

Write with a word count or record your answer, and your teacher returns it marked with feedback. Your progress is counted only from what you have done, skill by skill.
