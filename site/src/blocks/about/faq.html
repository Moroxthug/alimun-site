Questions about Alimun

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Send us a message through our contact page.

Is Alimun a school or an app?

Alimun is an online language school. Every course is built around live classes with a teacher, and the exercises and flashcards on the platform are there for practice between classes.

Who teaches the classes?

Experienced language teachers lead every live class, plan what you learn and correct your homework.

Which languages can I learn?

English and German classes are open today. Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Mandarin, Japanese and Arabic have a free waitlist, and we email you as soon as your language opens.

How do I find my level?

A short placement test with questions from A1 up to C1 sets your level, so you join a group that matches it and do not repeat what you already know.

Why do group classes cost less than private lessons?

One teacher leads a group, so the cost of each class is shared. You choose how much attention you want, from groups of about 35 people to private one-to-one classes.

Can I teach at Alimun?

We are building our teaching team. If you are a language teacher, see how teaching at Alimun works and apply on our careers page.
