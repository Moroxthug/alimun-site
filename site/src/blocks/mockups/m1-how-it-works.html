LET'S LEARN TOGETHER.
FOR LESS.

Alimun is a live online language school. You learn in a small group with a teacher, at a fixed time each week, and practise between classes with notes and AI feedback.

  • 01

    Take the free level test. It places you from beginner (A1) to advanced (C2).

  • 02

    Join a class at your level. Live 60-minute sessions with your teacher, on the same days and time every week.

  • 03

    Practise between classes. Session notes after every class and AI feedback on your homework.

Classes are open in English and German. Want another language? Get an email when it opens.
