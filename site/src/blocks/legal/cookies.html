Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Kleinefy Solutions Inc., doing business as Alimun ("Alimun", "we", "us" or "our"), uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser local storage on alimun.com and our dashboards, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Local storage is a similar technology that lets a website keep information in your browser. Both can be set by the website you visit ("first-party") or by other companies whose services the website uses ("third-party"). In this policy, "cookies" covers all of these technologies.

2. How We Use Cookies

Strictly Necessary

These keep you signed in, remember your cookie choices and language, protect against fraud and make the site work. They do not require consent and cannot be switched off, because the Service cannot work without them.

Analytics

These help us understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are visited and where errors happen, so we can improve it. We use them only if you accept analytics cookies.

Marketing

These help us measure our campaigns and show relevant messages about Alimun on other sites. We use them only if you accept marketing cookies. We do not currently set marketing cookies; if we start, we will list them below before doing so.

When you first visit, our cookie banner lets you accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies or choose by category. Non-essential cookies are not set until you agree. You can change your choices at any time with the Manage Cookies link in the footer of any page, or with the button below:

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the Service, such as signing in, will not work. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a refusal of marketing cookies.

We may update this Cookie Policy when we change the cookies we use or for legal reasons. We will post the new version with a new date and, where the change requires it, ask for your consent again.
