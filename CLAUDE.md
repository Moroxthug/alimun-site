## Must-follow
- **Root HTML is the source; locale folders are generated.** After editing any root `*.html`, run `npm run build` and commit the regenerated `ar de es fr it ma pt zh` copies. Locale-only hand-made pages are in `build.js`'s skip list.
- **Translations.** Edit only `js/translations.js`. `npm run build` splits it into `js/i18n/<lang>.js` (public text) and `js/i18n/<lang>.app.js` (dashboard-only text); commit those generated files. Pages load English plus their folder's language; `js/i18n.js` fetches another active language on demand.
- **Functions.** The owner moved Vercel to Pro (23 Oct 2026), so the 12-function Hobby cap no longer applies. A new file under `api/` is fine when a feature is clearly separate; otherwise keep adding `?action=` to the existing handler. Do not split working handlers just because you can.
- **Roles.** Never trust `user_metadata` for authorisation. Admin = `app_metadata.role === 'admin'` (client gate and server check) and `public.is_admin()` in SQL. Every API action verifies the Supabase JWT server-side.
- **Escaping.** Anything from the database, the user or the AI that goes into `innerHTML` passes through `escapeHtml`; URLs in `href/src` must be https and validated.
- **Database changes** go in `supabase/migrations/YYYYMMDDHHMM_<name>.sql`, idempotent, ending with a verify query. Show the plan before applying to production. Never delete production data without the owner's explicit yes. If MCP write tools are blocked, the owner runs the file in Supabase → SQL Editor.
- **Secrets** never in client code or commits. Only the publishable Supabase key and `pk_live_` Stripe key may appear in the browser.
- Line endings: the repo is checked out with CRLF on Windows; do not mass-convert files.

## Design and copy rules
- Only elements that exist on the current homepage: Coolvetica Condensed + Satoshi (`fonts/`), ink `#080808`, lime `#ceff65`, white, bone `#eeeeec` / `#f0efe9`, existing radii, images, logos and copy. No new colours, fonts, icon sets, illustrations, gradients or decorative elements without owner approval.
- **No black backgrounds (owner, 26 Oct 2026).** Ink `#080808` is for text, lines and small details only, never a background colour of a section, card or panel on new or redesigned public pages. Backgrounds are white or bone, with lime for markers, buttons and the occasional answer card. Photos and videos in large rounded frames carry the visual weight, as on the homepage, About and course pages. Flag existing black blocks (for example the course pages' certificate panel and the 1 on 1 price card) to the owner rather than changing them unasked.
- **No AI branding for Alimun as a whole (owner, 24 Oct 2026; revised 11 Nov 2026).** Alimun is not marketed as an AI product: the brand, taglines, homepage hero and the classes are about teachers and learning ("your teacher corrects your homework", "exercises with instant feedback"). Features that really use AI (the tutor, speaking practice, pronunciation checks, homework grades drafted for teachers) may be called AI where they are described, on public pages too, and must say so where the EU AI Act requires telling people they are talking to an AI. Never add AI wording to anything that is not AI. The legal pages keep their AI disclosures.
- **Dashboards and drawings (owner, 24 Oct 2026):** see `docs/DASHBOARD_PLAN.md`. The dashboards, exercises and learning material may use the approved drawing cast (Alimun book character, Alimun zoo, language buddies, fantasy and everyday friends, recoloured Open Doodles and Open Peeps, thought and speech bubbles) and its palette: forest #1a443c, gold #d5af33, coral #ff9f8a, mint #9fd6c7, sunflower #ffd166 plus soft tints and natural skin and hair tones, alongside the site colours. One loader everywhere (the owner's Alimun loader). Each dashboard step is confirmed by the owner before the next.
- **Tone in emails and replies (owner, 15 Nov 2026):** match the person's tone and Alimun's friendly voice. Open with "Hello" and their first name ("Hello Sylvie,"), never "Dear Madame/Sir" or other formal titles, and sign off as the Alimun team. Write in the language they wrote in.
- No emojis. No eyebrow/kicker labels above headings. No invented stats, testimonials, badges, logos or counters; numbers come from the database or are removed. Missing copy is left out, not invented, and never shown as a placeholder: no `[COPY NEEDED]` markers on pages (owner, 7 Nov 2026: "do not use copy needed, just leave empty for now"); list what is missing in `docs/PROGRESS.md` instead.
- **Banned (owner, 22 Oct 2026):** tags/chips/pills/badges (small outlined labels holding one word or name), numbered lists and numbered steps (01, 02, 03, "1 2 3 4", step counters like "04 / 06"), checklists and bullet lists of short items. Say it in sentences instead (e.g. "practice in the format of B2 First, C1 Advanced and IELTS"). This applies to new and redesigned sections; flag any that remain in existing blocks to the owner.

## Layout quality (owner, 19 Nov 2026: "this design is bullshit … hardcode something about the design")
Shipped broken once: course contracts squeezed into an 84 px column, a practice classroom that was mostly an empty dark box, a one-tab tab bar, cards whose text stopped at half their width. These are hard rules for every new or changed screen:
- **Look at it at the owner's size.** Screenshot every new or changed screen at 1600 px wide and at 375 px (phone), with sample data and with the empty state, and look at the picture before claiming done. Reading the DOM is not looking.
- **No squeezed text.** No paragraph or line narrower than 220 px on a desktop or 150 px on a phone. Do not reuse a component whose layout assumes content you do not have (`.tt-row` keeps an 84 px first column for a time: rows without a time use `.tt-row--plain`).
- **No half-empty cards.** On a wide screen a full-width card's content uses the width: put media beside text (two columns from 1100 px) or make the card narrower. Do not cap text at 760 px inside a 1400 px card.
- **No big empty areas.** Never leave a large box with only a waiting message or a drawing when something useful can be shown: open the useful thing by default (the practice classroom opens its board).
- **No controls with one option:** a tab bar with one tab, a select with one choice, a toggle that does nothing. Use a heading.
- **Same building blocks as the rest of the dashboard:** `view__head`, the 12-column `.grid` of `.card`s, `.stage` with a cast drawing, existing button and form classes. A one-off layout needs a reason.
- **Automatic check:** `npx playwright test tests/smoke/layout.spec.js` opens every teacher, learner and admin page with sample data at 1600 and 375 px and fails on sideways scrolling, squeezed text (narrow and wrapped onto three lines) and one-tab tab bars. Add every new route to its lists. It must pass before pushing.
