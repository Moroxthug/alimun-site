Everything you need between classes

Your dashboard brings together your live classes, your course book, practice, mock tests, flashcards, a listening and reading library, homework and your progress.

Live classes in your dashboard

Your next class with a Join button that opens 15 minutes before it starts, a calendar with your classes and homework due dates, and your teacher's notes after every class.

A course book that teaches, then checks

Each lesson is a few pages turned like a book: it explains, then checks with exercises, and every unit ends with a unit test. Explanations come in your own language.

Practice rounds that never repeat the same way

Short rounds built from the units you have reached. A gap exercise comes back as a word bank, matching as memory cards or a crossword, and the answers you got wrong come back first.

Mock tests in the real exam format

B2 First, C1 Advanced and IELTS for English, Goethe and telc for German, with a timer for each paper. When you finish, every answer shows with its explanation and every task with a model answer.

Flashcards that come back at the right time

The words of your course book, your classes and your tutor sessions, reviewed after one day, three days, a week, two weeks and a month. A word you miss comes back sooner.

A library to listen and read

Dialogues, interviews, talks, articles and stories for your level. The line being spoken is marked as you listen, and any word in a text shows its meaning when you tap it.

Homework, feedback and your progress

Write with a word count or record your answer, and your teacher returns it marked with feedback. Your progress is counted only from what you have done, skill by skill.

Learn a language online with a live teacher

Alimun is an online language school where every course is built around live classes with a teacher. Here is how it works and what to expect.

Live online classes, not recorded videos

Every class is a live video lesson with a teacher and the same group of learners, on fixed days at the same time each week. You speak from the first lesson, your teacher corrects you as you go, and after each class you get the session notes, the new vocabulary and your homework.

Practice between classes

Between lessons you keep practicing with exercises at your level that are corrected instantly, flashcards with the vocabulary from class and tasks in exam format. Your teacher sets the homework and returns it corrected, with a grade, your strengths and what to improve.

Group classes at a fair price

In a group, the cost of each class is shared, so live teaching costs far less than private tutoring. You choose how much attention you want: groups of about 35, 17 or 6 people, or private one-to-one classes, from €24 a month.

Every level from A1 to C2

Courses follow the Common European Framework of Reference, from beginner to proficient. A short placement test puts you in the right group between A1 and C1, so you do not repeat what you already know. You can also practice tasks in the format of B2 First, C1 Advanced and IELTS for English, and Goethe B1, Goethe B2 and telc B1 for German.

English and German now, more languages soon

English and German classes are open today. French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese are coming, and you can join their waitlist for free to hear when they open. All you need is a computer or other device with a camera and a microphone: classes run in the browser and you can cancel at any time.

FaQs

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Send us a message through our contact page.

How can your classes be so affordable compared to private tutors?

One teacher leads a group, so the cost of each class is shared. Between classes you get instant feedback on your practice, and your teacher grades your homework.

Does a group class really improve my speaking fluency?

Yes. Your teacher guides the conversation, gives everyone time to speak and corrects you live, in groups of about 6 to 35 depending on your tier.

What if I have an unpredictable schedule?

Each class meets on fixed days at a fixed time every week, with the same teacher and group, so choose a class whose schedule fits you. Between classes you can practise whenever you want.

Who are the teachers?

Every teacher applies with a CV, credentials and a 2-minute video, then leads a 30-minute practice session with a small group of active students before teaching.

What do I get with the subscription?

Live classes with your teacher and group on your plan’s schedule, session notes, graded homework, and a practice library with instant feedback, flashcards and exam-format practice.

How do I sign up for membership?

Sign up at alimun.com/signup, take the short placement test and choose your tier. Classes are open in English and German; for other languages, join the free waitlist and we email you when it opens.
