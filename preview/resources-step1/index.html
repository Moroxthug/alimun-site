False friends

Words that look the same and mean something else. Tap a card.

Embarazada ≠ embarrassed

Embarazada means pregnant in Spanish. Embarrassed is avergonzado.

Gift ≠ gift

Das Gift in German is poison. A present is das Geschenk.

Parenti ≠ parents

Parenti in Italian are your relatives. Parents are genitori.

Librairie ≠ library

A librairie in French is a bookshop. A library is a bibliothèque.

Bekommen ≠ become

Bekommen in German means to get or receive. To become is werden.

Puxe ≠ push

Puxe on a Portuguese door means pull. Push is empurre.
