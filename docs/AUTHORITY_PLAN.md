# Authority plan (backlinks and trust)

Written 2026-11-10 from Search Console (Links report) and Ahrefs' free Domain Rating check.

## Where we stand

alimun.com has a Domain Rating of 3.6. Search Console counts 9 external links from 4 sites, all of them listing or directory pages that link to the homepage: launchory.app (3), attracta.com (2), crunchbase.com (2) and saashub.com (2). No external site links to a blog article or a Resources page yet.

For comparison, the sites we meet in results: preply.com 83, italki.com 80, berlitz.com 75, lingoda.com 72. We will not catch them on head terms. What a young site can win is long, specific searches (exam rules, visa language requirements, grammar questions) where the page answering best can still rank. That is why the blog targets those. A few dozen relevant links from real sites would move us from "unknown" to "credible" for those searches.

Search traffic is rising: weekly impressions went from about 130 (19–25 Oct) to 321 (26 Oct–1 Nov) to 641 (2–8 Nov), and clicks from 1 to 8 to 19. 27 of 190 blog and Resources pages were indexed on 5 Nov; most of the rest were "Discovered – currently not indexed", which is typical of a site with little authority. Links are the main thing that changes that.

## What the owner needs to do (accounts in Alimun's name)

These need an account or a signature, so Claude cannot do them. Each one is a real, permanent link or profile.

1. Company profiles that are normal for an education business: LinkedIn company page, Google Business Profile (if eligible as an online business), Trustpilot, Product Hunt launch, AlternativeTo, G2 and Capterra (edtech categories). Use the same name, description and logo everywhere.
2. Ask the first students for an honest review on Trustpilot or Google once they have had classes. Never offer anything in exchange (both platforms forbid it).
3. Journalist request services, answering only questions we can answer with facts (language exams, visas and language levels, learning German for work): Qwoted, Featured.com, Source of Sources. One good quote in a news site is worth more than dozens of directories.
4. Teachers' own profiles: when a teacher has a personal site or LinkedIn, a link to their Alimun teacher page or to an article they reviewed.

## What Claude can prepare (owner sends or approves)

1. Linkable pages that others cite. We already have the CEFR levels poster, the free quizzes and exercises and the fact-checked exam and visa articles. Next: one data page per target country ("language requirements for visas and citizenship in Canada / Germany / Italy / UK", with official sources), which bloggers, forums and relocation sites like to link to.
2. An outreach list with the exact page we would suggest to each site and a short, personal email draft per site, for the owner to send from the Alimun address. Candidates to check first: university international offices and language centres that keep "useful links" pages, settlement and newcomer organisations in Canada that list free CLB practice material, expat sites and forums for Germany, Italian student sites, and Moroccan student and Goethe-preparation communities. No paid links and no link exchanges (Google treats both as spam).
3. Internal links, which we control fully. Today the course pages, the homepage and the Resources pages hardly link to blog articles, so the articles only receive links from other articles. Proposal for owner approval (it touches locked course and resources blocks): a short "Read next" line in sentences on each course page linking two or three matching articles (for example the German course to "German for citizenship", "The integration course" and "German prepositions"), and a link from each Resources exercise or quiz to the article that explains the same topic.

## How we will measure it

Every two weeks: referring domains in Search Console (Links report), Ahrefs free Domain Rating, number of indexed blog and Resources pages (URL Inspection baseline: 27 of 190 on 5 Nov 2026), and weekly impressions and clicks.
