# Class recordings: draft wording for the owner (13 Nov 2026)

Not published. The owner approves (or changes) this before it goes into `terms.html` and `gdpr.html` (root HTML; the
locale copies are generated with `npm run build` and the translations added). Until then the proof clips stay in dry
run (Admin, Automations); an admin's full recording works already, and the classroom already shows the short notice
(`app_cr_rec_note`). This is not legal advice: a lawyer should read it before it is published.

## For the Terms (classes)

**Recordings of group classes.** To check the quality of our classes and keep learners and teachers safe, Alimun
records five short moments (about one minute each) of every group class, and Alimun staff may record a whole class
when a learner, a teacher or Alimun raises a concern. Teachers and learners cannot record classes. Recordings are
seen only by Alimun staff, are never published or used to train AI, and are deleted after 30 days (90 days for a whole
class), unless we need to keep one longer to deal with a complaint or a legal claim. One-to-one lessons are not recorded.

## For the Privacy Policy

**Class recordings and attendance.** What we collect: when you join and leave a live class, from the video service's
records; short video and audio recordings of group classes as described in the Terms. Why: to check that classes take
place as booked and are taught well, to handle complaints, and to pay teachers for the classes they taught. Legal
basis: our legitimate interest in the quality and safety of our classes and in preventing fraud (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR);
you can object at any time by writing to us, and we will consider your situation. Who sees them: Alimun staff only;
each viewing is logged. Processor: Daily.co (video calls and recordings), under its data processing terms; recordings
are stored by Daily, which may store data in the United States under the EU standard contractual clauses or the
EU-US Data Privacy Framework. How long: attendance with the class records; recordings 30 days (90 for a whole class),
longer only for an open complaint or claim.

## Points to check with a lawyer

- Daily's data processing agreement and where recordings are stored (Daily's storage, or our own storage in the EU).
- Learners under 16: if any, a parent's consent may be needed; the sign-up does not ask for age today.
- Teachers: their contract or teacher terms should say the same (they are recorded while working).
- Whether to offer learners who object a class without recordings, or explain that group classes are always recorded.
