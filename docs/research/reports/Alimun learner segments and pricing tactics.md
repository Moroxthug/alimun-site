# Price per class, sell seats truthfully, lead with deadlines

Alimun should sell the same per-class rate card through a short plan builder. The learner picks a language, a goal, a group size, how many classes a week and a real time slot. The price comes from what that class costs Alimun to run, and the page shows both the price per class and the monthly total. At the default setting (a group of up to six, two classes a week, monthly), an English or Spanish class costs **€6.88 (€55 a month)**, French €7.38, Italian €8.63 and German €9.88. Every plan keeps at least about 20-25% contribution once four learners share the hour. Willingness to pay is set by deadlines, not by hobby interest. Nurses and trainees learning German in Morocco, Tunisia, Turkey, India and Latin America, TEF/TCF candidates heading for Canada, and spouses who need Italian B1 for citizenship are the buyers least sensitive to price, and they feed the most useful B2B2C channel: recruiters and employers who already pay for language training. The pricing tactics that work and are also legal all rest on facts Alimun actually holds. They are a seat count read live from the database, a fixed cohort start date, a founding-price lock written into the terms, rewards paid in seats in groups that have room rather than in cash, and an honest pause and downgrade path. Fake timers and invented "almost full" claims are blacklisted in the EU, the UK, Canada and India. One compliance gap needs action before any new pricing goes live. Alimun's current Terms let learners withdraw only by email. The **EU's online two-step withdrawal function (Directive 2023/2673, new CRD Art. 11a) has applied since 19 June 2026**, so Alimun most likely needs to build it now. The evidence is thin in two places: no public source measures willingness to pay, tenure or churn for live-class learners by segment, and no competitor publishes how well its plan builder converts. Every figure below marked [estimate] is reasoning, not data.

## Deadlines and payers, not hobbies, set willingness to pay

Large public surveys mostly describe app users in rich countries, and they put travel and culture first. US adults cite culture (31%), keeping the brain sharp (27%) and travel (27%) ([Duolingo/Talker](https://blog.duolingo.com/never-too-late-to-learn)). Duolingo's 2024 report finds education the top reason for learning English worldwide ([Duolingo 2024](https://blog.duolingo.com/2024-duolingo-language-report/)). Indian learners put career first (55%) ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/71-of-indian-learners-excited-about-emerging-technology-duolingo-report-49381.htm)). The Goethe-Institut says German is wanted worldwide "mainly as a language for work". Demand is growing where Germany is seen as a place to work, train (Ausbildung) or study, and Egypt, India, Colombia and Morocco are named. There are about **15.5 million learners of German**, and **1.1 million Goethe exams** were taken in 2025 ([Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/de/uun/prs/pma/26859595.html)). Two conclusions follow, and both are inferences. The first concerns who pays most and stays longest. The least price-sensitive buyers are, in order, B2B and recruiter payers, exam and citizenship candidates close to their date, and work migrants who already hold a job offer. Lifetime value is highest for German learners climbing several levels, for B2B contracts, and for retiree groups that bond. Single-exam candidates and students spend heavily but leave quickly. The second concerns the "real person" argument. The 97% "practising with a real person matters" and 72% "apps alone are not enough" figures are the strongest public case for live classes over apps ([Workplace Journal](https://workplacejournal.co.uk/2026/07/nine-in-10-workers-would-use-employer-funded-language-learning-study-finds/)). They come from Preply, which sells live tutoring, so quote them as Preply's survey or leave them out.

## Truthful urgency, seat-paid rewards and easy exits beat discount theatre

The behavioural evidence is uneven. Some of it is classic lab work, some is vendor data and some comes from field experiments, and the table rates each tactic on that basis. The rule running through it is simple. Use a tactic when the fact behind it is real and Alimun can prove it from its own database. Drop it when it needs an invented number.

| Tactic | Best evidence | How Alimun should use it |
|---|---|---|
| Good-better-best and anchoring | Adding a third, higher-priced option pushed the middle choice to about 57% (classic, reported second-hand) ([cience](https://www.cience.com/programmable-revenue/issue-12/buyers-reach-for-the-middle/)). Ariely's Economist decoy came from a classroom study, not a field test ([UBC](https://blogs.ubc.ca/onurkurtic/2015/07/10/the-decoy-effect/)) | Show 1, 2 and 3 classes a week side by side, pre-select 2, and show the per-class price falling. Mark the default with layout and a sentence, never a badge |
| Decoys | No 2022-2026 field test of decoys in subscriptions was found | Use only real alternatives. A private hour shown next to the group per-class price is an honest anchor. Never add a plan that exists only to lose |
| Per-class framing | "Pennies-a-day" framing helps at small amounts ([Gourville 1998](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/jconrs/v24y1998i4p395-408.html)) but backfires at larger ones ([Atlas & Bartels 2018](https://home.uchicago.edu/~bartels/papers/Atlas-Bartels-2018-JCR.pdf)) | Show the price per class plus the monthly total. Never "less than €1 a day" |
| Charm endings | Left-digit effect (classic) ([kolenda](https://kolenda.io/tactics/charm-prices)) | Keep the earlier local rounding rules (MAD x9, R$x9,90 and so on) |
| Upsell to the next tier | No attach rates are published for tutoring. Stripe prorates natively | Trigger only on real use. When a learner has booked every class or asks for a make-up, show the next tier's actual price and the per-class difference. Upgrade at once, prorated |
| Add-ons | Duolingo family plans reached 23% of subscribers with better retention ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/02/27/duolingo-duol-q4-2024-earnings-call-transcript/)) | Pairs (partner/family), extra classes, mock exams, conversation club. Every add-on is opt-in and unticked |
| Truthful scarcity | "Only X left" works best for perishable, dated inventory ([ASU](https://news.wpcarey.asu.edu/20200327-%E2%80%98only-2-left-stock-order-now%E2%80%99-does-it-work)). The CMA pursued Emma Sleep and Wowcher over fake timers ([Hausfeld](https://www.hausfeld.com/what-we-think/perspectives-blogs/a-wake-up-call-for-online-retailers-as-cma-tackles-misleading-choice-architecture); [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/en/insights/2024/07/19/cma-issues-update-in-wowcher-dark-patterns-investigation-102jdyy)) | A seat in a six-seat class starting on a date is exactly such inventory. Offer it until a stated calendar date, not "the first N members" |
| Trials | RevenueCat (17,000+ apps): monthly-plan trials of 10-16 days convert at 46.6%, and first renewal reaches 72.0% versus 49.5% without a trial. A/B test 7 days in Band C |
| Prepay discounts | The most common annual discount is 16.7% ([Recurly](https://recurly.com/research/saas-benchmarks-for-subscription-plans)). No annual plan until cohort churn is measured |
| Pause and downgrade | Adding pause saves 9.6% of would-be cancellers (vendor) ([eCommerce Fastlane](https://ecommercefastlane.com/reduce-your-cancellations-by-10-with-pause-subscriptions/)). | Sprint-style attendance rewards | Lingoda refunds 100% (30 classes a month) or 50% (15) of fees for full attendance, under strict rules ([Lingoda Sprint rules](https://www.lingoda.com/downloads/sprint/72/2026-Sprint-72a-Rules-October-Start-EN.pdf)). | Penetration and grandfathering | No 2022-2026 study compares penetration and skimming in edtech | The list prices already sit near the cost floor and under Lingoda. There is no room for a further "intro price" that steps up later. Grandfather founding members for life, and everyone else for six months after any rebase |

The way pricing scales argues for rewards paid in seats. Teachers are paid per scheduled class, so an extra learner in a class that is already running costs Alimun only video, about **€0.21 per participant-hour** ([Daily](https://www.daily.co/pricing/video-sdk/)). Four bonus group classes look worth about €27 to a learner, at the €6.88 Regular per-class price. They cost Alimun under €1 when they are placed in groups with free seats. Cash refunds of the Lingoda kind cost real money and invite gaming. Seat rewards also fill under-filled groups, and fill is the margin lever. The earlier unit economics showed most group products falling to 0-18% contribution with three learners and reaching 36-53% with five.

The evidence also argues against two things. Learners overestimate how much they will attend. DellaVigna and Malmendier found that gym members paying over $70 a month paid more than $17 per visit when a $10 pass was available ([NBER](https://nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w10819/w10819.pdf)). So the 12-class tier will be over-bought and under-used. A one-month rollover and a downgrade suggestion after low attendance are the honest answers, and they also lower legal risk (see the legal section below). Streak mechanics that punish illness are the second thing to avoid. Duolingo ran 600+ streak experiments but published no effect sizes ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://lennysnewsletter.com/p/behind-the-product-duolingo-streaks)).

## A plan builder priced from the teacher-cost floor

### How competitors build plans, and why Alimun needs fewer choices

The closest competitors use two or three choices plus a commitment length. Lingoda asks for language, then group or private, then one of four volume tiers. It shows a per-class price, marks the 12-class tier "POPULAR" and renews every four weeks ([Lingoda](https://www.lingoda.com/en/pricing/)). Cambly's "Build your own plan" asks for plan type, then lessons per week, then plan length, and prices per lesson ([Cambly](https://www.cambly.com/en/subscribe)). The research on choice overload is mixed. A 2010 meta-analysis found an average effect near zero ([Scheibehenne et al.](https://abcwest.cogs.indiana.edu/pmwiki/pdf/scheibehenne.options.2010.pdf)). A 2015 meta-analysis found real overload when choices are complex, preferences uncertain and the goal is to decide fast ([Chernev et al.](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/academics-research/research/detail/2015/when-product-assortment-leads-to-choice-overload-a-conceptual/)). A first-time learner meets all three conditions. So Alimun's builder should have no more choices than competitors' do, and should pre-fill a sensible answer for each.

### The pricing rule

The rule is cost-plus with a market cap. Start from the cost of running a 60-minute class: teacher rate × 1.03 for payouts, plus €0.21 of video per participant. Work out the net revenue each seat must bring in to leave at least **25% contribution at the planned fill**. Planned fill is four learners for groups of up to six, six for exam cohorts of up to eight, and two for pairs. Private lessons carry no empty-seat risk, so their target is 15-20%. Then round to a price that stays under the comparable competitor. In the EU, net revenue = price ÷ 1.2 (VAT) − 2.7% − €0.25 (Stripe, Billing and Tax), which is roughly 0.8063 × price − €0.25.

Applied to French, the rule changes one price from the earlier report. At €55, French Regular earns 22.05 − 16.50 = €5.55 per class (25%), and French Intensive at €76 earns only 19%. The French price therefore rises to **€59 Regular**: net €47.32 ÷ 8 = €5.92 per seat, × 4 = €23.66, − €16.50 = **€7.16, a 30% contribution**. Spanish stays with English because both teachers cost €13.

### How the builder works

The builder reads as one page, and each choice appears in a sentence. It shows no step counter, no numbered steps and no chips, as the site rules require. These are its parts, each with its default:

| Choice | Options | Default | Effect on price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Language | English, Spanish, French, Italian, German | Taken from the page locale, or none | Sets the teacher cost |
| Goal | Everyday speaking; work (business English, German for care, BPO English or French); an exam by a date (Goethe/telc, TEF/TCF, CILS B1 cittadinanza, IELTS, DELE) | Everyday speaking | Exam routes to the exam course (up to 8 learners, 12 classes a month, fixed start dates). The other goals keep the same price |
| Group size | Group of up to six; pair (you plus one person); private | Group of up to six | Main price lever (table below) |
| Classes a week | 1, 2 or 3 (pairs and private: 1 or 2) | 2 | Per-class price falls with volume |
| When | Morning, afternoon, evening or weekend in the learner's time zone, then a list of real groups at their level with seat counts and start dates | Evening | No price effect. There is no off-peak discount, because demand there is a matching problem and constant discounts teach people to wait ([Vibefam](https://vibefam.com/how-fitness-studios-can-monetize-off-peak-hours-without-discounting/)) |
| Payment | Monthly or every three months | Monthly | 10% off three months (5% on Intensive plans and in Band C) |

The price line reads: "€6.88 per class, €55 a month. Most learners start with two classes a week, so that's what we've selected." It is not "from €X". Under *Ving Sverige* a "from" price must be the real lowest available price, and its conditions belong next to it ([IPPT](https://www.ippt.eu/items/ippt20110512-cjeu-konsumentombudsman-v-ving)). The UK ASA also requires a "significant proportion" of the product to be available at the "from" price ([Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2025/11/24/asa-acts-on-misleading-hotel-pricing-102lvpo)). Where Alimun does advertise "from", it should name the true minimum with its condition. For English group classes that is "from €6.00 per class, three classes a week, paid every three months", and "from €5.08 per class on exam courses of up to eight learners". In Canada and Quebec, every displayed total must already include any mandatory fee, and only taxes may be added later ([Competition Bureau](https://www.canada.ca/en/competition-bureau/news/2022/06/important-amendments-to-the-competition-act-come-into-effect.html); [Lavery](https://www.lavery.ca/en/publications/our-publications/1895-retail-sales-and-consumer-law-make-sure-your-prices-are-accurate.html)).

### Running it without empty classes

Learners never configure a class into existence. They join one from a timetable. The "when" choice lists only groups that are open or forming, at the learner's level and in their language, each described in a sentence: "Tuesday and Thursday 19:00 Lisbon time, B1 German, starts 10 November, four of six seats taken." A forming group opens at four bookings. Card holders in a forming group are not charged until their first class runs. If the group has not formed within 21 days, the learner is offered the nearest running group or a refund of the €3 trial.

The rules for under-filled groups follow the language schools in Granada and Ingolstadt and are stated at checkout ([Instituto Mediterráneo Sol](https://www.languagecourse.net/pdf/instituto_mediterraneo_sol_grenade__1748_en_46205.pdf); [Sprachschule Aktiv](https://www.sprachschule-aktiv.de/ingolstadt/en/legal-terms/)). At three learners the class runs normally: an English Regular class earns 3 × €5.51 = €16.54 against €14.23 of cost, about 14%. At two, it runs for 45 minutes at the same price for at most two weeks and is then merged. At one, it becomes a 30-minute private lesson or moves to another group. The learner never pays more, and is told at least 48 hours ahead, because late notice is the main complaint about inlingua Como ([Language International](https://www.languageinternational.com/school/inlingua-como-66077)). Teachers are paid for whatever is scheduled at the 48-hour mark, and 100% for any later change.

Make-ups follow the same logic. A missed class can be made up in any group at the same level with a free seat that week, which costs Alimun video only. That replaces Lingoda's three-day credit forfeiture, which draws complaints ([Trustpilot UK](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/lingoda.com?page=8)).

Unused classes roll over for one month, capped at one month's allowance, stay usable while the subscription is active, and trigger a reminder before they lapse. Germany's BGH struck down clauses forfeiting prepaid balances (telecom credit, BGH III ZR 157/10) ([Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/digitale-welt/mobilfunk-und-festnetz/prepaidrestguthaben-nach-vertragsende-auszahlen-lassen-so-gehts-23828)), so profit should come from full groups, not from classes that expire.

## Five gaps worth entering, and the buyers who already pay

**German for health professionals still abroad** is the clearest gap. Lingoda/AMBOSS courses for doctors are free only with a Bildungsgutschein, which requires residence in Germany. Their KP groups run up to 14-15 learners, and no self-pay price is published ([AMBOSS](https://www.amboss.com/de/partners/lingoda)). Lingoda is hiring B2B health sales staff and selling to German employers ([ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.de/jobs/580785957-sales-development-representative-b2b-health-all-genders-bei-lingoda)). That leaves self-paying candidates, and candidates sent by small agencies in Morocco, Tunisia, Turkey, India and Latin America, poorly served. The existing pipelines have documented quality problems. A 2020 CORRECTIV investigation found a Colombian school promising B2 in six months and an agency moving courses to Bosnia because it was cheaper. Essen University Hospital's nursing director called B2 "quite meagre" ([CORRECTIV](https://correctiv.org/en/?p=71438)). The source is old, so treat it as a pattern to check, not a current measurement. The product is a stable cohort with one named teacher, care vocabulary from B1 onwards, telc Pflege or Goethe B2 mock exams, and a monthly progress report per candidate.

**Exam-date cohorts.** TEF sessions sell out at the Alliances Françaises in Manitoba, Victoria and Edmonton ([AF Manitoba](https://afmanitoba.ca/en/exams/tef); [AF Victoria](https://www.afvictoria.ca/exams/tef); [AF Edmonton](https://www.afedmonton.com/en/exams/tef/)). IIC Los Angeles' CILS B1 citizenship session was fully booked ([IIC LA](https://iiclosangeles.esteri.it/en/?p=100)), and Goethe India keeps formal exam waiting lists ([Goethe](https://goethe.de/resources/files/pdf340/website_exam-v2025.pdf)). Cohorts should therefore be planned backwards from published exam dates. Enrolment should tell learners to book their exam seat at the same time, because exam seats are the real bottleneck. This is also Alimun's most honest form of urgency: the cohort starts on a date, and the exam is on a date.

**Italian B1 for citizenship** is a fresh, legally created deadline for spouses and residents, concentrated in Argentina, Brazil and the EU. Run CILS B1 cittadinanza cohorts timed to the June and December sessions.

**BPO English and Spanish in Morocco.** France's ban on cold-call telemarketing took effect in August 2026. Moroccan estimates of jobs at risk range from 10,000 (the sector body) to 40,000-50,000 (a ministry estimate reported second-hand) ([Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/les-call-centers-face-a-une-loi-francaise-qui-change-la-donne_GW2QNAUUMJFZ7L3EELN65ICF6I/); [Outsource Accelerator](https://news.outsourceaccelerator.com/?p=92767)). If centres move to other languages and markets, English and Spanish training for agents becomes a B2B sale. No source confirms that shift yet, so test it with one or two centres before building for it.

**One teacher, one group, start to finish.** The recurring complaints in the market are rotating teachers at Lingoda, rigid rescheduling, surprise renewals at Preply and Lingoda, tutor no-shows at Preply, and weak support at Cambly ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lingoda.com?page=7); [Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unveil-preplys-service-insights-a-comprehensive-review-analysis-trustpilot-en-us-154722); [Sikayetvar](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/preply-us/preply-teacher-missed-all-lessons-and-i-didnt-get-a-refund); [Trustpilot CA](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/cambly.com)). A fixed weekly group with a named teacher, a renewal reminder and a written progress letter answers every one of them.

Heritage demand among Moroccan families is real. Madrid and Murcia suspended in-person Arabic classes for Moroccan-origin children in 2026 ([Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/?p=282146)). But that demand is for Arabic and Darija, outside Alimun's catalogue.

The B2B2C money already flows. Under German fair-recruitment rules the employer pays placement and recruitment costs, and who pays for language training is agreed case by case ([IHK Halle](https://www.ihk.de/halle/ihr-unternehmen-unsere-serviceangebote/international/aussenwirtschaft-aktuell/auslaendische-fachkraefte/faire-rekrutierung-ueber-vermittler-6215558)). Baden-Württemberg's €3,000-per-nurse language subsidy and Triple Win's €7,900 employer fee bound what one candidate's training can cost. Small agencies already advertise Moroccan language schools and B1-B2 candidates ([hogapage](https://www.hogapage.de/jobs/firma/sami-personalvermittlung)). The channels to approach, in order, are:

1. Small German-care recruiters with source pipelines in Morocco, Tunisia, Turkey and India.
2. German care employers using the Baden-Württemberg subsidy.
3. Moroccan and Egyptian BPOs.
4. Employers in the UK, US, Canada and the EU paying for staff, served through open-group seats invoiced monthly.

Canadian immigration consultants (RCICs), Italian citizenship service firms (patronati) and university international offices are logical partners but were not researched.

One flow built to the strictest of these rules covers every market:

1. An all-in price that shows both the per-class figure and the total.
2. An unticked box asking to start classes early, with the acknowledgement it requires.
3. A "Withdraw from contract" link that stays visible for 14 days after signup and again after the trial converts. Whether conversion restarts the withdrawal period is an open legal question.
4. A trial reminder 7-10 days before conversion, plus a Spain-only confirmation.
5. A cancel button that reaches its confirmation page directly, with no offer on it.
6. An email receipt.
7. Pause and downgrade offered on the plan page and in that receipt.

Whenever a referrer is rewarded, the incentive must be disclosed. Nothing in Alimun's model needs a fake reference price or a fake deadline, so the legal limits cost it almost nothing.

## The revised price list and how it earns money

All prices are per learner, for 60-minute classes, in Band A (EU/EEA including VAT). Band B is 85% of Band A for groups, and Band C 75%. Pairs and private lessons are 85% outside Band A. The local rounding rules are those of the earlier report. Contribution is shown at planned fill, before overheads and acquisition.

The quarterly price for 1 and 2 classes a week is 10% off, which works out as monthly price × 2.7: English €78 and €148, French €84 and €159, Italian €97 and €186, German €113 and €213. At four learners these keep 24-30% contribution. For example, English €148 nets €119.08, which is €4.96 per seat-class. Four seats make €19.85, and after €14.46 of cost that leaves €5.39, or 27%. Intensive plans get only 5% off, because 10% would push French and German below 20%. German at €294 would net €6.58 per seat and only 18%.

### Exam courses (up to eight, open at six, fixed dates, 12 classes a month)

English, Spanish and French €61 (€5.08 per class), Italian €76 (€6.33), German €87 (€7.25). At six learners, contribution is 39% for English, 31% for French, 41% for Italian and 37% for German. The German figure works like this: €87 nets €69.90, which is €5.83 per seat. Six seats make €34.95, and after €22.07 of cost that leaves €12.88. Exam courses take no further discounts.

### Add-ons (opt-in, never pre-ticked)

**Extra group class:** €8 (English, Spanish, French), €10 (Italian), €12 (German). It is placed in a group with a free seat, so nearly all of it is margin.

**Extra private hour:** €26 English or Spanish, €28 French, €30 Italian, €36 German, with 27-33% contribution. German: €36 nets €28.78, and €28.78 − €21.02 = €7.76.

**Mock exam marked by your teacher**, about 1.75 teacher-hours including marking: €39 English or Spanish, €45 French or Italian, €55 German, with 18-25% contribution. German: €55 nets €44.10, and 1.75 × €20.60 = €36.05 of teacher cost, which leaves €8.05.

**Conversation club**, four 45-minute sessions a month in groups of up to ten, opening at five: €19 English, Spanish or French, €23 Italian, €27 German. At six attendees English contributes 51% (€22.60 − €11.14). The club doubles as the bottom of the downgrade ladder and as the retiree product. Its per-session price must not appear in any "from" claim for 60-minute small-group classes.

**Attendance certificate and progress letter:** free, because it is what lets employers reimburse learners.

### Trial, commitment and retention rules

**Trial:** €3 for 14 days, with up to three classes in groups already running, credited to the first month. Local prices are about 29 MAD, R$16,90 and C$4.95. The cost is three seats at €0.21. Never call it "free".

**Commitment:** quarterly as above. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time. In Germany, a quarterly plan rolls into a monthly one after the first term.

**Founding offer**, valid until a calendar date the owner sets, with no member counter. Founding members lock their price for as long as the subscription continues, including pauses of up to two months. In Band A only, 1- and 2-a-week group plans also carry a separate founding price about 10% below list, shown as a price in its own right, not as a crossed-out one: English €26 and €49, French €28 and €53, Italian €32 and €62, German €38 and €71. At four learners these keep 22-31% contribution. English €49 nets €39.26, which is €4.91 per seat; four seats make €19.63, leaving €5.17 (26%). The founding price does not combine with quarterly billing or apply to Intensive plans. Bands B and C get the lock only, because their prices already sit near the floor. A 10% cut on Moroccan English Regular would leave about 10%.

**Referrals:** once the friend has paid a first full month, both sides receive four bonus group classes, booked into groups with free seats. They are worth about €27 at the Regular per-class price and cost Alimun under €1 in video. The limit is ten rewards a year, they are never paid in cash, and referrers are told to disclose the reward when they post.

**Bring a friend into your group:** a friend who takes a free seat in your existing group pays half price for the first month, and you get the four bonus classes. In English that month brings in €27.50 against a marginal cost of €0.21 per class.

**Full-attendance reward:** a learner who attends every class in a month, with one make-up allowed, gets one bonus group class the next month. Exam-course learners who attend all 12 classes get four bonus classes in the month after their exam, which is Alimun's version of Lingoda's "Keep Running" bridge to a Regular plan ([Lingoda Sprint rules](https://www.lingoda.com/downloads/sprint/72/2026-Sprint-72a-Rules-October-Start-EN.pdf)).

**Pause, downgrade and price changes:** pause for one or two months, once in any 12 months. The downgrade ladder runs 12 → 8 → 4 classes → club. Price rises come with 30 days' notice. Founding members are grandfathered for life and everyone else for six months.

### B2B prices (excluding VAT, invoiced, paid by bank transfer)

**Closed company or recruiter cohort of up to eight**, priced per class-hour: €36 English or Spanish, €40 French, €42 Italian, €48 German. The minimum term is three months. Above 60 class-hours a month the price drops 10%. Contribution is 53-58%. German: €48 − (€20.60 + 9 × €0.21 = €22.49) = €25.51. That margin pays for sales effort, reporting and 30-day payment terms. At eight candidates, high-volume German costs €43.20 ÷ 8 = **€5.40 per candidate-hour**, close to Goethe Morocco's online 60 MAD/h (about €5.45) ([Goethe Marokko](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ma/de/m/spr/kur/all.html)). Baden-Württemberg's €3,000 subsidy would therefore buy about 555 hours.

**Repeat guarantee** in place of a pass-rate promise: a candidate with at least 90% attendance who fails the target exam joins the next cohort's final month free, filling a free seat.

**Staff seats in open groups:** consumer list price excluding VAT, with a monthly invoice and attendance reports.

### Learner value

Learner value is the sum of plan contribution and add-ons. An EU English Regular learner contributes €15.17 a month at four per class. At an assumed 10% monthly churn, which is not measured (no churn benchmark for live classes exists), that is about €152 over a lifetime. ## Conclusion

Several of the strongest pricing tactics are cheap for Alimun because it pays teachers per scheduled class. Once a class runs, an empty seat is worth almost nothing, while a filled one is pure margin, so seats are the natural currency for every reward, make-up, trial and referral. A seat feels valuable to the learner and costs Alimun about €0.21. The same structure makes honest urgency easy, because Alimun really is selling dated, limited seats, while the competitors that rely on struck-through prices and timers are the ones regulators are now fining. The two weaknesses are thin data and the risk of empty classes. Neither price cuts nor more plan options fix them. What fixes them is filling classes: timetables that steer learners into groups already forming, cohorts planned backwards from exam dates, and recruiter contracts that fill a cohort in a single sale.

The second implication is about which learners carry the business. Hobby learners are the largest group in the surveys, but deadline buyers and the institutions paying for them will make Alimun viable. Care recruiters, Canada's French-category draws and Italy's B1 citizenship rule all create learners who must reach a level by a date, and that pressure is what makes them less sensitive to price. The first data Alimun collects should therefore be cohort churn, fill per class and conversion by segment and band. That data will settle the open questions above, which no public source currently answers.
