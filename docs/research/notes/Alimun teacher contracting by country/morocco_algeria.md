# Engaging remote language teachers in Morocco and Algeria: status, tax, payout rails and alternatives (2025-2026)

Research date: 4 Nov 2026. Not legal advice. Many sources below are secondary (consultancies, press, blogs); primary texts (DGI notes, Office des Changes IGOC PDF, Algerian Journal Officiel) could not be fully read within the tool budget. Confidence flags are given per item.

## Morocco: auto-entrepreneur status (Law 114-13), tax, the 80,000 MAD single-client rule, AMO, CPU

### Takeaway
A Moroccan teacher can invoice Alimun as an auto-entrepreneur (services cap MAD 200,000 a year, 1% of turnover for services, flat CNSS/AMO contributions paid quarterly). The 2023 rule taxing turnover above MAD 80,000 from one client by a 30% withholding is collected by the client; no source found says how (or whether) it applies when the client is a foreign company that cannot act as a Moroccan withholding agent. That is the main open question for Morocco.

### Cited Findings
- Caps under Law 114-13 (unchanged in 2026): services MAD 200,000 of collected turnover per calendar year; commerce/craft/production MAD 500,000. The services list given includes "conseil, développement, design, formation, traduction, marketing, photographie". Tax: 1% of turnover for services, 0.5% for commercial/industrial/craft. — [Beancount (Sept 2026)](https://beancount.io/fr/blog/2026/09/15/morocco-auto-entrepreneur-revenue-caps-2026-cpu-cnss-crossing-guide)
- Same caps and rates confirmed by a second guide (law 114-13 of 2015, registration through the national auto-entrepreneur portal; 1% on turnover). — [Mediaterranee (Oct 2026)](https://www.mediaterranee.com/guides/freelance-depuis-lalgerie-le-maroc-ou-la-tunisie-statut-plateformes-et-paiement-en-devises)
- Eligible activities are set by regulation; an arrêté of 3 July 2015 lists non-eligible activities; regulated professions (lawyers, doctors, engineers) are excluded. "Conseil, formation, informatique" appear among eligible services. — [Legalplace](https://www.legalplace.fr/guides/auto-entrepreneur-maroc); [Humantal](https://humantal.ma/ressources/statut-auto-entrepreneur-maroc-guide-complet); [Démarches Maroc](https://www.demarchesmaroc.com/inscription-auto-entrepreneur/)
- 80,000 MAD rule: since the 2023 Finance Law (CGI art. 73-II-G-8°), service turnover invoiced to a single client above MAD 80,000 per year is subject to a 30% withholding at source, libératoire, on the excess only; computed per client per calendar year. Example: MAD 150,000 from one client means MAD 70,000 taxed at 30% = MAD 21,000. — [Captain Legal](https://www.captain.legal/ma/blog/auto-entrepreneur-sarl-seuil-80000-dh/); [Beancount](https://beancount.io/fr/blog/2026/09/15/morocco-auto-entrepreneur-revenue-caps-2026-cpu-cnss-crossing-guide)
- Government rationale for the 80,000 rule: fight tax evasion and disguised employment. — [Africa-laws.org (2023)](https://www.africa-laws.org/Morocco's%20New%20Tax%20Regime%20for%20Auto%20Entrepreneurs%202023.php)
- Freelancers asked for the threshold to be raised to MAD 100,000 in the 2025 Finance Law; the government rejected it. — [Hespress (18 Jan 2025)](https://fr.hespress.com/406876-406876.html). A parliamentary proposal for 100,000 was also reported, with no evidence of adoption. — [Bladi.net](https://www.bladi.net/changement-statut-auto-entrepreneur-maroc,111518.html)
- Foreign client: none of the sources consulted address whether the 30% withholding applies when the payer is a non-resident company. — searches summarised from [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/retenue-a-la-source-ce-qui-va-changer-des-janvier-2023), [Africa-laws](https://www.africa-laws.org/Morocco's%20New%20Tax%20Regime%20for%20Auto%20Entrepreneurs%202023.php), [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/406876-406876.html)
- CNSS/AMO for auto-entrepreneurs: flat contribution tied to declared turnover, filed quarterly; coverage includes AMO, family allowances and minimum pension. Indicative quarterly amounts: ~MAD 600 (turnover up to 25,000), ~1,200 (25,001-50,000), ~2,400 (50,001-125,000); "may vary", check cnss.ma. — [Upsilon Consulting (2026)](https://www.upsilon-consulting.com/en/cnss-contributions-morocco-2026/)
- Conflicting description: monthly brackets for independents (AMO Tadamon/TNS style) from MAD 224/month (income up to 2,800) to MAD 1,200/month (over 12,000). — [Wafir](https://wafir.ma/fr/cnss/cnss-independants-auto-entrepreneurs). Sources disagree on the mechanism; flagged.
- CPU (Contribution Professionnelle Unique), created by the 2021 Finance Law to replace the old forfait: caps MAD 500,000 (services) and MAD 2,000,000 (commerce/industry); effective rates higher than auto-entrepreneur. — [Beancount](https://beancount.io/fr/blog/2026/09/15/morocco-auto-entrepreneur-revenue-caps-2026-cpu-cnss-crossing-guide). Exact CPU rates not found.

### Inferences
- A teacher earning €15/h for 20 h/week for 46 weeks earns about €13,800 (~MAD 150,000), under the MAD 200,000 cap, but well above the MAD 80,000 single-client threshold. If the 30% rule bites, the teacher's tax on the excess (~MAD 70,000) jumps from ~1% to 30%. Whether a foreign payer can or must withhold is unclear; the plausible reading (not verified) is that a non-resident company has no Moroccan withholding obligation, and the teacher may then have to self-declare the excess. This needs a Moroccan tax adviser or a DGI ruling.
- If Alimun relocates to Morocco (including CFC), it would become a Moroccan client and clearly a withholding agent for the 30% on the excess above MAD 80,000 per teacher.
- "Formation" is listed as an eligible service; online language tutoring billed to a foreign company very likely fits, but no official activity code was confirmed.

### Gaps
- Official DGI note circulaire on the 80,000 rule with foreign clients: not found.
- Exact current CNSS auto-entrepreneur contribution table (cnss.ma not fetched); Barid Al-Maghrib registration steps not confirmed from a primary source.
- Any Finance Law 2025/2026 changes to the auto-entrepreneur regime: none found besides the rejected 100,000 request.

## Morocco: receiving foreign currency (Office des Changes), banks, Payoneer/Wise/PayPal

### Takeaway
Service-export receipts must be repatriated within 90 days of performing the service. Residents can hold part of repatriated receipts in foreign-currency or convertible-dirham accounts (70% under circular 3/2020, per press). Holding a foreign platform account (PayPal, Payoneer balance abroad) was treated as an infraction by the Office des Changes in 2022 reporting, while 2026 guides say Payoneer withdrawals to local banks and Wise transfers in MAD work. Safest rail: bank transfer (SWIFT/SEPA to a Moroccan bank) or Wise paying out in MAD to a Moroccan bank account.

### Cited Findings
- Office des Changes (exportations de services, IGOC): full receipts must be repatriated "dans un délai de quatre-vingt-dix (90) jours à compter de la date de la réalisation des prestations" (art. 97); exporters may keep part in foreign-currency or convertible-dirham accounts (art. 98), which covers "personnes morales ou physiques inscrites au registre du commerce"; a service contract is required (art. 96); commissions deductible abroad capped at 10%. — [Office des Changes](https://www.oc.gov.ma/fr/personnes-morales/exportations-de-services)
- IGOC 2026 in force from 1 Jan 2026; the summary article mentions a 15%-of-contract foreign-market account for holders of foreign contracts but nothing about auto-entrepreneurs or retention rates. — [FNH (2 Jan 2026)](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/office-des-changes-igoc-2026); IGOC 2024 announcement — [Office des Changes](https://www.oc.gov.ma/fr/actualites/publication-de-l-instruction-generale-des-operations-de-change-2024)
- Circular n°3/2020 lets resident individuals, including those not in the commercial register, open foreign-currency/convertible-dirham accounts and keep "jusqu'à 70% de leurs revenus rapatriés au Maroc". The Office listed as infractions: not repatriating, holding assets abroad without authorisation, "opening foreign bank or platform accounts" (PayPal, Payoneer), and paying expenses abroad from export proceeds. (2022, older than 2023) — [Le360 (17 Jan 2022)](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/activites-en-ligne-generatrices-de-devises-le-rappel-a-lordre-de-loffice-des-changes-253246/)
- Press in Nov 2024: services exports by individuals are monitored by the Office des Changes in coordination with the DGI (influencers targeted); people in the commercial register can deposit up to 70% of export income in foreign-currency accounts. — [Hespress (Nov 2024)](https://fr.hespress.com/396583-396583.html); also 70% cited by [Upsilon](https://upsilon-consulting.com/export-biens-services-change-maroc/)
- 2026 guide: Payoneer allows withdrawals to a Moroccan bank; Wise allows paying/holding MAD; recommends a Moroccan bank account in MAD or foreign currency. — [Mediaterranee (Oct 2026)](https://www.mediaterranee.com/guides/freelance-depuis-lalgerie-le-maroc-ou-la-tunisie-statut-plateformes-et-paiement-en-devises)
- Wise lists Morocco (MAD) as a supported send destination. — [Wise help](https://wise.com/help/articles/2571942/what-countries-and-regions-can-i-send-to)
- Cash Plus partnered with PayPal in Sept 2025 to allow PayPal withdrawals in dirhams. — [AlgeriaTech (2026)](https://algeriatech.news/freelancing-algeria-remote-work-payment-barriers-legal-2026/)

### Inferences
- Lowest-risk design: Alimun pays each Moroccan teacher by bank transfer (or Wise in MAD) directly into a Moroccan bank account in the teacher's name, against a monthly invoice and a written service contract. That satisfies repatriation automatically and gives the bank the contract/invoice it asks for. Avoid paying into teachers' foreign accounts or platform balances held abroad.
- Retention in foreign currency is the teacher's option, not Alimun's concern.

### Gaps
- Whether IGOC 2026 changed the 70% retention or created a specific auto-entrepreneur rule: not confirmed from the IGOC text.
- Any quarterly service-export declaration obligation for individuals: one low-quality mention only (not cited).

## Morocco: employee route, EOR, subordination test, permanent establishment

### Takeaway
Employing in Morocco costs about 21% employer CNSS/AMO/training tax on top of gross (some branches capped at MAD 6,000/month), and a foreign company needs either a local entity or an EOR (~US$600/month per head at the big providers, which dwarfs a €13-20/h part-time teacher's pay). Courts decide on real subordination; a Oct 2024 Cour de cassation ruling held that a contract with an individual entrepreneur is not an employment contract absent subordination.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 employer rates: family allowances 6.40%, short-term benefits 1.05% (cap MAD 6,000/month), long-term 7.93% (cap 6,000), AMO 4.11%, vocational training tax 1.60%: total employer 21.09%. Employee 6.74%. — [Upsilon (2026)](https://www.upsilon-consulting.com/en/cnss-contributions-morocco-2026/)
- Cour de cassation (chambre sociale), arrêt n° 887/1 of 29/10/2024, affaire 2995/5/1/2024: a contract with an "entrepreneur individuel" is not an employment contract "en raison de l'absence de lien de subordination". Facts not reported. — [Hespress (18 Jan 2025)](https://fr.hespress.com/406876-406876.html)
- Practitioner guidance: decisive test is legal subordination (power to direct, instruct, supervise), Article 6 Code du travail. — [Teamed](https://www.teamed.global/contractor-hiring-guides/morocco.md)
- Older Moroccan doctrine: payment method (fixed or proportional) is not decisive; subordination is. (older source) — [Memoireonline](https://www.memoireonline.com/07/10/3683/m_Le-statut-juridique-des-travailleurs-agricoles-Maroc1.html)
- French Cour de cassation (persuasive only): work within an organised service can indicate subordination where the principal unilaterally sets the conditions of performance (Soc. 27 Sept 2023, n° 20-22.465). — [Dalloz actualité](https://www.dalloz-actualite.fr/document/soc-27-sept-2023-fs-b-n-20-22465)
- EOR: Rivermate has a Morocco EOR page (no price shown); Deel covers Morocco; Deel/Remote general EOR list prices ~US$599/month per employee (Remote also reported at $699). — [Rivermate Morocco](https://www.rivermate.com/employer-of-record/morocco); [Deel Morocco](https://www.deel.com/de/hiring/employees/morocco/); [Sovereign Magazine](https://sovereignmagazine.com/article/employer-of-record-published-pricing-459-699)

### Inferences
- At 20 h/week × €15 ≈ €1,300/month, employer CNSS adds ~€270; an EOR fee of ~€550 more than doubles the add-on. EOR only makes sense for full-time core staff.
- PE risk for a Canadian company with remote contractor teachers in Morocco looks low if teachers have no authority to conclude contracts for Alimun and no Alimun premises; but this was not researched against the Canada-Morocco treaty (gap).

### Gaps
- Moroccan case law specifically on platform or remote workers: none found.
- Canada-Morocco tax treaty PE wording; Morocco-based EOR local providers' prices.

## Algeria: auto-entrepreneur status (Law 22-23), IFU, CASNOS

### Takeaway
Algeria's auto-entrepreneur status (Law 22-23 of 18 Dec 2022; ANAE online registry live since Jan 2024) gives a 0.5% IFU on turnover (minimum DZD 10,000/year) up to DZD 5 million turnover, with CASNOS cover (sources differ: flat DZD 24,000/year option vs 15% of declared income with a 24,000 minimum). Teaching/training eligibility was not confirmed from an ANAE list.

### Cited Findings
- Law 22-23 (Dec 2022) is the founding text; ANAE, under the Ministry of Knowledge Economy, runs registration at anae.dz; platform operational from 20 January (2024); turnover must not exceed DZD 5 million (TSA: for three consecutive years). — [TSA Algérie (1 Apr 2024)](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/du-nouveau-pour-les-autoentrepreneurs-en-algerie/); [Mediaterranee](https://www.mediaterranee.com/guides/freelance-depuis-lalgerie-le-maroc-ou-la-tunisie-statut-plateformes-et-paiement-en-devises)
- Regulated liberal professions excluded. — [Mediaterranee](https://www.mediaterranee.com/guides/freelance-depuis-lalgerie-le-maroc-ou-la-tunisie-statut-plateformes-et-paiement-en-devises)
- IFU 0.5% of annual turnover, replacing income tax, professional tax and other levies; quarterly or annual declarations via anae.dz; registration in the National Register rather than the Commercial Register. — [AlgeriaTech (24 May 2026)](https://algeriatech.news/algeria-ifu-tax-regime-digital-services-freelance-gig-economy-2026/)
- IFU minimum DZD 10,000/year even with zero turnover (CIDTA art. 282 sexies, 365 bis), G12 / G12 bis declarations. — [AlgeriaTech](https://algeriatech.news/?p=21564) (secondary; verify)
- CASNOS: 15% of declared income with a minimum DZD 24,000/year — [AlgeriaTech (2026)](https://algeriatech.news/freelancing-algeria-remote-work-payment-barriers-legal-2026/); another site presents DZD 24,000/year as an optional flat contribution (citing decree 15-289 as amended by executive decree 26-257 of 15 July 2026, single source). — [AlgeriaTech](https://algeriatech.news/?p=16805). Conflict flagged.
- ANAE activity codes include a "Conseil, Expertise et formation" family. — [Upgrowth ANAE codes](https://www.upgrowth.dz/activite-anae/conseil-en-gestion-et-economie-de-011213)

### Inferences
- A teacher at ~€13,800/year (~DZD 2.1 million at the official ~151 DZD/€) stays under the DZD 5 million cap; IFU ~DZD 10,500. No single-client rule like Morocco's was found.

### Gaps
- Official ANAE list confirming an activity code for online language teaching/tutoring.
- Authoritative CASNOS amount for auto-entrepreneurs (decree 26-257 unverified).

## Algeria: receiving foreign currency, payout rails, employment, EOR, risks

### Takeaway
Legally, Algerian auto-entrepreneurs exporting services may keep 100% of their FX receipts (Banque d'Algérie note 06-2021, recalled by ANAE in 2024) and open a business account with just the ANAE card, ID and proof of address (circular 2024/01). Since Règlement 26-02 (JO 12 Aug 2026), export receipts must be repatriated within 120 days. In practice, rails are weak: Wise does not pay out DZD, PayPal does not receive in Algeria, Payoneer is partial. The lawful route is a SWIFT transfer in EUR to the teacher's Algerian foreign-currency account.

### Cited Findings
- ANAE (31 Mar 2024) reminded auto-entrepreneurs they may dispose of "100 % des revenus en devises de l'exportation de leurs services" under Banque d'Algérie note n° 06-2021 of 29 June 2021; bank account opening without commercial-register registration, on ANAE card + ID + proof of residence. — [TSA Algérie (1 Apr 2024)](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/du-nouveau-pour-les-autoentrepreneurs-en-algerie/)
- Circular n° 2024/01 of 30 Jan 2024 eases commercial bank accounts for auto-entrepreneurs. — [Mediaterranee](https://www.mediaterranee.com/guides/freelance-depuis-lalgerie-le-maroc-ou-la-tunisie-statut-plateformes-et-paiement-en-devises) (date per search snippet; verify)
- Banque d'Algérie exchange regulation: online exports of digital services, start-up services and services of non-trading professionals are exempt from bank domiciliation formalities; proceeds must still be repatriated; bank credits exporter's FX account on repatriation (art. 67). — [TSA Algérie](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/banque-dalgerie-la-nouvelle-reglementation-des-changes-promulguee/)
- July 2025 Banque d'Algérie communiqué: exporters can now freely use their FX export receipts (details not confirmed). — [Algérie Eco](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=126990); [Dzair Tube (18 Jul 2025)](https://www.dzair-tube.dz/fr/banque-dalgerie-les-exportateurs-peuvent-desormais-disposer-de-leurs-recettes-en-devises)
- Règlement n° 26-02 of 23 July 2026 (JO n° 58, 12 Aug 2026) cuts the repatriation deadline for non-hydrocarbon exports from 360 to 120 days from performance of the service; 120-180 days requires CAGEX export credit insurance; FX-account title of règlement 07-01 unchanged. — [Upgrowth](https://www.upgrowth.dz/journal-officiel/reglement-26-02-comptes-devises-rapatriement-exportations); [Observalgérie (14 Aug 2026)](https://observalgerie.com/2026/08/14/economie/algerie-ce-qui-change-pour-les-exportateurs-dans-le-rapatriement-des-devises/)
- Practical: the right to keep 100% is "underused" and bank infrastructure underdeveloped; PayPal does not support receiving in Algeria; Stripe and Wise offer no direct functionality; SWIFT wires take 5-10 business days and cost $25-50; CCP/BaridiMob cannot receive international transfers; Instruction 06-2025 (Aug 2025) PSP framework is dinar-only. Parallel euro ~280 DZD vs official 151 (July 2025). Law 25-10 (July 2025) criminalises crypto ownership/mining (2-12 months, fines up to DZD 1 million). — [AlgeriaTech (2026)](https://algeriatech.news/freelancing-algeria-remote-work-payment-barriers-legal-2026/)
- Wise: Algeria (DZD) is not a supported destination; sending to a USD/EUR/GBP-denominated recipient account may be possible. — [Wise help](https://wise.com/help/articles/2571942/what-countries-and-regions-can-i-send-to)
- Payoneer: registration possible but withdrawals to a dinar bank limited. — [Mediaterranee](https://www.mediaterranee.com/guides/freelance-depuis-lalgerie-le-maroc-ou-la-tunisie-statut-plateformes-et-paiement-en-devises); "partial", more restricted than Morocco — [AlgeriaTech](https://algeriatech.news/?p=27653)
- Cash FX exports capped at €7,500 per calendar year (Règlement 24-05 of 13 Oct 2024) — concerns physical cash, not transfers. — [Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/compte-devises-algerie-ouverture-retraits-7500-euros/)
- Employment: CNAS general rate 34.5% of gross: 25% employer, 9% employee, 0.5% social works (employer); one guide gives 26% (17% + 9%) instead. SNMG DZD 24,000/month from 1 Jan 2026. — [CLEISS](https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/algerie.html); [Upgrowth CNAS](https://www.upgrowth.dz/cnas); conflicting [AfriCarrières](https://africarrieres.com/algerie/fr/guide/employeur-entreprise/charges-employeur)
- EOR Algeria: Rivermate pages show €299, €399 and €449 per employee/month, and one page says it currently offers no services there. — [Rivermate Algeria EOR](https://www.rivermate.com/employer-of-record/algeria); [Rivermate guide](https://rivermate.com/guides/algeria)

### Inferences
- Lawful design for Algeria: require ANAE auto-entrepreneur card; contract plus monthly invoice in EUR; pay by SWIFT in EUR to the teacher's Algerian FX ("devises") account; pay within the month so repatriation within 120 days is automatic. Do not pay to third-country accounts, relatives abroad, crypto or informal channels; those expose teachers to exchange-control and (crypto) criminal risk and Alimun to reputational and AML risk. Budget the SWIFT fee ($25-50) and consider monthly batching.
- Algeria is not under comprehensive Western sanctions as far as these sources show; risk is exchange-control and AML, not sanctions. (Not specifically researched.)

### Gaps
- Full text of note 06-2021 and Règlement 26-02 not read; whether 26-02 changed auto-entrepreneur FX retention not confirmed (sources say FX-account title unchanged).
- Official Payoneer Algeria withdrawal status; Western Union business payout to Algeria limits.

## Platform features that raise reclassification risk, and lawful alternatives

### Takeaway
Morocco (and, by persuasive analogy, French-law-influenced Algeria) asks whether the principal can give orders, supervise and sanction. Features where Alimun unilaterally sets conditions and punishes refusals look like subordination; features that let teachers choose freely look like independence.

### Cited Findings
- Subordination = power to direct, instruct and supervise (Morocco, art. 6 Code du travail). — [Teamed](https://www.teamed.global/contractor-hiring-guides/morocco.md)
- Moroccan Cour de cassation 29/10/2024: no employment contract with an individual entrepreneur absent subordination. — [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/406876-406876.html)
- French test (persuasive): power to give orders, monitor and sanction; integration into an organised service where the principal unilaterally sets conditions is an indicator. — [Dalloz actualité](https://www.dalloz-actualite.fr/document/soc-27-sept-2023-fs-b-n-20-22465)
- The Moroccan 80,000 single-client rule was explicitly aimed at "disguised employment". — [Africa-laws.org](https://www.africa-laws.org/Morocco's%20New%20Tax%20Regime%20for%20Auto%20Entrepreneurs%202023.php)

### Inferences (design, derived from the tests above; not from a source specific to Alimun)
- Higher risk: "late cancellers lose priority" (a sanction); reliability scores used to rank access to work; mandatory standby with a fee (looks like on-call time); minimum-hours commitments; fixed 8-10 week groups imposed rather than accepted per offer; performance reviews, mandatory lesson scripts, monitoring of classes; exclusivity or bans on working for others; paying per hour regardless of output (looks like wages, though payment mode alone is not decisive per Moroccan doctrine).
- Lower-risk alternatives: teachers freely accept or decline each offer with no penalty for declining; frame the 8-10 week group as a fixed-term service order with a defined deliverable (a course of N sessions) priced per session; cancellation consequences as contractual terms (e.g. a cancelled order is reassigned and not paid) rather than ranking penalties, or apply objective, published allocation rules; curriculum as a quality specification of the deliverable, with teachers choosing methods; no exclusivity; teachers use their own equipment and schedules; invoices from the teacher's own status (auto-entrepreneur card/ANAE card); allow substitution by another qualified teacher in the pool.
- For Moroccan teachers, keeping individual annual billing to Alimun under MAD 80,000 avoids the 30% rule if it applies, but capping hours per teacher must not be used as a device; flag to a Moroccan adviser.

### Gaps
- No Moroccan or Algerian decision found on platform/gig workers or online teachers specifically.
