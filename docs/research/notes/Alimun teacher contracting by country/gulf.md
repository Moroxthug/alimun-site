# Engaging remote teachers in Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the UAE (contractor vs alternatives), 2025-2026

Not legal advice. Research notes compiled 4 Nov 2026 from about 25 tool calls. Most sources are law-firm, consultancy or news summaries; official portals (MoHRE, freelance.sa, Qatar MoL) were not reachable or returned no detail. Many findings came from search-result summaries and were not verified against full text; these are marked "(search summary)".

## UAE: can residents freelance for a foreign school, and what permit do they need?

### Takeaway
UAE residents can lawfully do paid side work, but only through a specific permit. The most relevant are the MoHRE private teacher (tutor) work permit, which is free, valid two years, covers online and group teaching, and needs an employer no-objection letter, and the MoHRE freelance permit or a free-zone freelance licence, which costs about AED 6,000 to 20,000 a year. The MoHRE part-time permit does not fit Alimun, because a MoHRE-registered UAE employer has to apply for it. Below AED 1m turnover there is no personal tax and no corporate tax. VAT registration is required from AED 375,000.

### Cited Findings
**Freelance permit and licences**
- The MoHRE freelance licence is valid in all seven emirates, has no free-zone tie and needs no local sponsor. Applicants must be 18 or over, with a valid passport, professional qualifications, a clean record and medical fitness. Approval takes 2 to 5 working days through TAMM (Abu Dhabi) or the DDA (Dubai). Article dated 29 Jul 2026. — [Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-freelance-licence-visa-costs-requirements-2026/)
- Approximate Year-1 costs from the same article, whose cost table is partly garbled:
  - Abu Dhabi mainland: annual licence about AED 790-1,200; total about AED 8,000-14,500.
  - Dubai premium free zones: licence about AED 7,520-9,500; total about AED 14,000-20,000.
  - Budget free zones: total about AED 5,800-8,500.
  - Source: [Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-freelance-licence-visa-costs-requirements-2026/)
- Free-zone licences usually restrict which activities are allowed. Some activities need extra approvals or a full company structure. The article does not cover teaching specifically. — [Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-freelance-licence-visa-costs-requirements-2026/)
- Residents in full-time employment, and residents on dependent visas, can freelance alongside their job with an NOC from their employer or sponsor. — [Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-freelance-licence-visa-costs-requirements-2026/)
- Commercial blogs give conflicting MoHRE permit costs: AED 6,000-10,000, about AED 7,500, or AED 6,100, with an all-in AED 7,500-20,000 a year. NOC from the current sponsor if employed (search summary). — [Property Finder](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/freelance-visa-dubai), [Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/en/blog/uae-freelance-visa-and-green-visa-2026-the-complete-guide), [Rizmona](https://www.rizmona.com/business-licenses/freelance-permit/)
- Green Visa (5-year self-sponsored residence): needs a MoHRE freelance permit, a bachelor's degree or specialised diploma, and AED 360,000 a year of freelance income over two years. — [Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-freelance-licence-visa-costs-requirements-2026/)
- Fujairah Creative City and RAKEZ freelance prices: not found (see Gaps).

**Private teacher (tutor) work permit (MoHRE / MoE)**
- Covers coaching "either online or in person, to individuals or groups". Application is free, though the good-conduct certificate and medical test carry government fees. Valid two years, renewable; processing takes 1-5 working days. Article updated 18 Sep 2025. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/uae-private-tutor-permit-who-can-apply-and-what-are-the-rules-1.500274031)
- Eligible applicants: registered school teachers (government or private), government and private-sector employees, unemployed people, school students aged 15-18 and university students. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/uae-private-tutor-permit-who-can-apply-and-what-are-the-rules-1.500274031)
- Documents: certificate, good-conduct certificate, employer no-objection letter, medical fitness and a signed Private Tuition Agreement. School teachers also need an NOC from the school, may not tutor their own school's students, and must sign a code of conduct. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/uae-private-tutor-permit-who-can-apply-and-what-are-the-rules-1.500274031)
- The Ministry of Education calls private tutoring by public-school staff a "red line" violation. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/uae-private-tutor-permit-who-can-apply-and-what-are-the-rules-1.500274031)
- Consultancy blogs claim KHDA approval is needed for structured tutoring or training businesses in Dubai. A more careful source says an individual online tutor does not automatically need a KHDA-licensed institute. A claimed AED 100,000 fine is unsourced (search summary). — [Business Dubai](https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/online-tutoring-business-setup-dubai), [Meydan FZ](https://www.meydanfz.ae/blog/start-an-online-tutoring-business-in-dubai)

**Part-time and second-employer permits**
- The MoHRE part-time permit is applied for by the secondary employer, which must be a MoHRE-registered entity. The first employer's consent is generally not needed when both jobs are part-time, but a full-time worker taking a second job needs the primary employer's permission. Reported fees are AED 100 application plus AED 500 approval, or AED 600-1,200. Validity is reported as 1 year or 6 months, so sources conflict (search summary). — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/working-part-time-in-uae-you-do-not-need-the-approval-from-your-original-employer-1.1631111286798), [RAKEZ blog](https://rakez.com/en/Blog/Details/Article/161/part-time-work-visa-and-permit-in-the-uae-a-complete-guide), [Virtuzone](https://virtuzone.com/blog/can-i-work-for-two-employers-in-the-uae-at-the-same-time/)

**Tax for individual teachers**
- Corporate tax applies to a natural person only when business turnover (not profit) exceeds AED 1m in a calendar year (Cabinet Decision 49 of 2023). Salary does not count toward the threshold. Once registered, profit is taxed at 0% up to AED 375,000 and 9% above. A person who registers and later drops below the threshold must still file nil returns (search summary). — [Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-criteria-to-determine-natural-persons-subject-to-corporate-tax/), [Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/08/13/uae-corporate-tax-freelancers-2026-guide)
- Registration deadline: 31 March of the year after crossing the threshold, with an AED 10,000 penalty for late registration. VAT registration is mandatory from AED 375,000 of turnover and voluntary from AED 187,500. — [Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-freelance-licence-visa-costs-requirements-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Fit for Alimun.** A teacher on another employer's visa has two clean routes, both needing that employer's NOC or no-objection letter:
  - the free private-teacher permit, which explicitly covers online group teaching;
  - a paid MoHRE or free-zone freelance permit.
- **Part-time permit is unusable.** It needs a MoHRE-registered UAE employer, so Alimun, as a foreign company, cannot be the applicant.
- **Open question on the tutor permit.** It is framed around private tuition of students, likely UAE-resident ones. Whether it also covers teaching adults worldwide for a foreign platform is unconfirmed.
- **No tax exposure at these rates.** At €13-20/h no teacher comes near AED 375k (VAT) or AED 1m (corporate tax), so individual tax exposure is effectively nil.
- **Practical approach.** Alimun should ask UAE teachers to warrant that they hold a valid permit (tutor permit or freelance permit) and to upload it, rather than verifying UAE law case by case.

### Gaps
- No official MoHRE page loaded; u.ae "working for two employers" returned 404. Fees and validity need checking on mohre.gov.ae.
- Freelance package prices for Fujairah Creative City and RAKEZ were not found.
- No source says whether the tutor permit covers serving a foreign company's adult learners abroad.
- No source on whether KHDA, ADEK or MoE approval applies when the school is outside the UAE and the teacher only delivers lessons.

## UAE: Alimun itself in a UAE free zone

### Takeaway
A free-zone company is unlikely to get 0% "qualifying income" on class fees paid by individual learners. Transactions with natural persons are excluded unless they relate to a listed qualifying activity, and education did not appear in the summaries of the list. A small company can instead use Small Business Relief (0% on revenue up to AED 3m a year, now extended to 31 Dec 2029), but only if it does not claim QFZP status. Costs run about AED 12,000-25,000 in year one, and bank account opening is the main practical hurdle.

### Cited Findings
- **Qualifying income test.** Income from non-free-zone persons qualifies only if it comes from a listed qualifying activity. Non-qualifying revenue must stay below the lower of 5% of revenue or AED 5m. Breaching this loses QFZP status, and sources disagree on the length: the current period plus four more, or five years (search summary). — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/uae-corporate-tax-law-what-constitutes-qualifying-income-for-free-zone-persons), [BDO](https://www.bdo.global/en-gb/insights/tax/world-wide-tax/united-arab-emirates-guidance-on-free-zone-business-issued)
- **Natural persons excluded.** Ministerial Decision 265 of 2023 lists "transactions with natural persons, except transactions in relation to the Qualifying Activities" as an excluded activity. — [MoF PDF](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/27.10.23-EN-Ministerial-Decision-No-265-of-2023-Regarding-Qualifying-Activities-and-Excluded-Activities.pdf)
- **Qualifying activities named in summaries.** Manufacturing, distribution in or from a designated zone, logistics, and HQ and treasury services to related parties (search summary). — [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/decisions-on-qualifying-free-zone-income.html)
- **Possible replacement decision.** One source says Ministerial Decision 229 of 2025 replaces Decision 265. Not verified. — [Mondaq Aug 2025](https://www.mondaq.com/tax-authorities/1680086/uae-tax-updates-august-2025)
- **Small Business Relief.** Revenue must be AED 3m or less in the current and every earlier tax period. Ministerial Decision 131 of 2026 extends the relief to tax periods ending by 31 Dec 2029. Exceeding the threshold once ends eligibility permanently. QFZPs cannot elect it, and splitting a business to stay under the threshold disqualifies it. — [Entrepreneur ME](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/business-news/uae-extends-small-business-tax-relief-through-2029), [Farahat](https://farahatco.com/blog/uae-extends-small-business-relief-until-31-december-2029-under-ministerial-decision-no-131-of-2026/), [Osome](https://osome.com/ae/blog/small-business-relief-extended-2029/)
- **Setup cost.** Budget zones (IFZA, Meydan, Dubai South) charge about AED 12-15k for a licence. A solo founder with one visa and a flexi-desk pays about AED 18-25k in year one. UAQ FTZ basic licence is about AED 4,890. Consultancy figures (search summary). — [AA Consultancy](https://www.aaconsultancy.ae/dubai/dubai-business-setup-2026-free-zone-mainland-offshore/), [BCL](https://bcl.ae/blogs/dubai-free-zone-company-formation-cost.md)
- **Banking.**
  - Account opening is "the slowest and most unpredictable" step, taking 2-12 weeks.
  - Minimum deposits range from AED 0-50k; traditional banks ask AED 25-50k.
  - KYC is strict: UBO checks, source of funds, a business plan and evidence of UAE substance.
  - Newer or cheaper zones have lower bank acceptance; Wio and Mashreq Neo are fallbacks.
  - Source (search summary): [AA Consultancy](https://www.aaconsultancy.ae/dubai/dubai-business-setup-2026-free-zone-mainland-offshore/), [The Tax Lead](https://thetaxlead.co.uk/blog/dubai-uae/uae-free-zone-setup-guide-2026.html)
- **Residence.** Having a company in a jurisdiction does not by itself make the owner personally tax resident there (search summary). — [The Tax Lead](https://thetaxlead.co.uk/blog/dubai-uae/uae-free-zone-setup-guide-2026.html)
- **EOR in the UAE.** Deel's UAE page lists EOR from $599/month, with an estimated employer cost of 0.66%-15.60% (search summary). — [Deel UAE](https://www.deel.com/de/hiring/employees/united-arab-emirates/)

### Inferences
- **Free-zone 0% is unlikely.** Selling live classes to individual consumers worldwide looks like non-qualifying income: transactions with natural persons, with education not on the list. A free-zone Alimun would probably not keep QFZP 0%.
- **SBR is the realistic 0% route.** It applies while revenue is AED 3m or less (about €700k) and is available to free-zone and mainland companies that do not elect QFZP. Above that, profits are taxed at 9% above AED 375k.
- **Residence and management drive the outcome.** A UAE company managed from Canada risks being treated as Canadian-resident through central management and control. The owner's actual residence matters more than where the company is incorporated. This is an inference; Canadian rules were not checked here.
- **UAE VAT.** It would apply to a UAE-established supplier above AED 375k. How it treats e-services to foreign consumers was not researched.

### Gaps
- Full text of Decision 265, or its possible 2025 replacement, was not read to confirm education is absent.
- Substance requirements were not detailed.
- No Fujairah Creative City or RAKEZ company prices.
- No owner-visa costs were itemised.

## Saudi Arabia: freelancing, expatriates, licensing, tax and social security

### Takeaway
The Freelance Document (freelance.sa) is reported to be for Saudi nationals only. It is free and covers more than 123 professions, but no source confirmed that teaching is one of them. Expatriates on an iqama may legally work only for their sponsor. Remote side work for a foreign company is at best a grey area and at worst a labour violation that can lead to deportation. Saudi Arabia has no personal income tax. Engaging expatriate teachers in Saudi Arabia as contractors is therefore not reliably lawful; Saudi nationals with a Freelance Document are the clean case.

### Cited Findings
**Freelance Document**
- Arabic guides say the requirements are Saudi nationality, age 18 or over, and an active Absher or Nafath account. Wafeq says expatriates cannot get the licence through freelance.sa (search summary). — [Sabbar](https://sabbar.com/blog/freelance-work-document-terms), [Wafeq](https://www.wafeq.com/en/business-hub/for-business/freelance-accounting-in-ksa)
- Jobbers.io says that as of April 2026 the Freelance Work Document is open only to Saudi citizens and that freelancing without a work permit is prohibited for expatriates (search summary; the page returned 403 on fetch). — [Jobbers](https://www.jobbers.io/?p=47417)
- Issuance, renewal and cancellation are reported to be free (search summary). — [Khaleej Calculators](https://khaleejcalculators.com/freelance/freelance-document-eligibility-fees)
- Saudipedia describes eight programmes and more than 123 approved professions. "Specialised services" need no extra permit; other categories need one from the sector authority. Translation, writing, design and consulting are mentioned; teaching and tutoring are not. Holders can register voluntarily in social insurance and open a dedicated bank account. The page is dated 2021 and cites 2023 data, so it is old. — [Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/freelancing-in-the-kingdom)

**Expatriates on an iqama**
- Foreign nationals may work only for their employer or sponsor, and the iqama names that entity (search summary). — [Expat.com forum](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/middle-east/saudi-arabia/853573-working-freelance-from-saudi-arabia.html), [Saudi Expat Guide](https://saudiexpatguide-vo6.pages.dev/salary-finance/freelance-rules-saudi)
- Online work for foreign clients is described as "a legal grey area — not explicitly permitted for most iqama holders". Saudi Arabia has no digital-nomad visa, and a June 2026 report says such work on dependent iqamas can be treated as unauthorised (search summary; secondary sources). — [Saudi Expat Guide](https://saudiexpatguide-vo6.pages.dev/salary-finance/freelance-rules-saudi), [Stamped Nomad](https://www.stampednomad.com/digital-nomad-news/saudi-arabia-deports-7989-foreign-nationals-in-june-residency-sweep)
- Fines of up to SR 50,000 and jail time have been reported for employers who engage self-employed expatriates. — [GPA](https://gpa.net/blogs/middle-east/saudi-arabia-sr50-000-fine-and-jail-time-for-employers-engaging-self-employed-expats)
- Ajeer lends workers temporarily between Saudi entities. Nothing found shows it covers remote work for a foreign company (search summary). — [SMASCO](https://www.smasco.com/en/?p=898)

**Social insurance (GOSI)**
- Non-Saudis: the employer pays 2% for occupational hazards only, with no pension or SANED.
- Saudis: the total was 21.5% before July 2024. Under the new law, new joiners' pension rates rise in steps; sources disagree on timing (for example 9.5% each from July 2025, rising to 11% each by 2028).
- Source (search summary): [Teamed](https://www.teamed.global/gulf-employment-law/saudi-gosi-and-2025-amendments), [ZenHR](https://blog.zenhr.com/en/how-to-calculate-gosi-deductions-in-saudi-arabia-complete-guide-2026), [PaySpace](https://support.payspace.com/portal/en/kb/articles/saudi-arabia-gosi-contribution-rate-increase-saudi-nationals-hired-from-3-july-2024)

**EOR cost**
- Deel's Saudi page lists EOR from $999/month, with an estimated employer cost of 2.40%-12.85% depending on nationality, insurance and family. — [Deel KSA](https://www.deel.com/fr/hiring/employees/saudi-arabia/)
- A third-party comparison lists Deel and Remote at $599. A general range of $199-699 is also reported (search summary). — [EORHQ](https://eorhq.com/compare/best-eor-for-saudi-arabia/), [Hivedesk](https://www.hivedesk.com/compliance/employer-of-record-saudi-arabia)

**Withholding tax and service PE**
- In 2023 ZATCA in effect abolished the "virtual service PE" concept (KPMG reading). — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/sa/en/home/insights/2023/05/tax-alert-updates-on-the-concept-of-service-pe.html), [EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2023-0967)
- Saudi withholding tax falls on Saudi payers paying non-residents. Alimun pays teachers from abroad, so it is not relevant to Alimun as payer (search summary). — [Cercli](https://www.cercli.com/resources/withholding-tax-saudi-arabia)

**Data transfers (PDPL)**
- The PDPL has been enforceable since 14 Sep 2024. The revised transfer regulation and the first SCCs were issued in Sep 2024; BCRs are available for countries without adequacy. — [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/data-technology/saudi-arabia-updates-data-transfer-regulations-and-introduces-the-first-set-of-standard-contractual-clauses), [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2024/09/saudi-arabia-s-personal-data-protection-law-become)

### Inferences
- **Two populations.**
  - Saudi nationals: engageable as contractors if a teaching or training activity exists under the Freelance Document. Otherwise via a commercial registration.
  - Expatriate iqama holders: should not be engaged as contractors without the sponsor's authorisation, and no clear mechanism was found for remote work for a foreign company.
- **Saudization (Nitaqat)** applies to Saudi employers (and to an EOR's own quotas), not to a foreign company engaging contractors. An EOR would have to absorb the quota. No source was found on this.
- **EOR cost is out of proportion.** At €13-20/h for perhaps 10-20 hours a week, a teacher earns about €550-1,700 a month. A $599-999 monthly EOR fee is 40-180% of pay, so EOR is not economic for part-time Gulf teachers.
- **Data protection.** Alimun would collect Saudi residents' data (teacher contractors, possibly students), so PDPL extraterritorial reach and transfer SCCs may apply.

### Gaps
- No official freelance.sa activity list; whether teaching, tutoring or training is an approved activity is unconfirmed.
- No official HRSD statement on expatriate remote side work.
- Nitaqat impact not sourced.
- PDPL extraterritorial scope for a foreign controller was not detailed.

## Qatar: freelancing rules, licensing, payment rails and EOR

### Takeaway
Qatar has no universal personal freelance permit. Expatriates need an authorised Ministry of Labour process to work for anyone other than their employer. Law No. 9 of 2026 amended the Labour Law to create a basis for part-time and freelance work, but implementing decisions were still pending as of July-August 2026. Self-employment needs a commercial licence; the Ministry of Commerce home-project scheme covers 63 activities, including translation, but tutoring was not confirmed. Expatriates carry no social insurance and there is no personal income tax. Qatar-based expatriate contractors are currently hard to engage lawfully.

### Cited Findings
- **Law No. 9 of 2026.** It amends the 2004 Labour Law to create a legal basis for part-time and freelance work, aimed partly at platform work. "Future implementing decisions" will set the rules, and some measures need regulations before they take effect. Alert dated 9 Jul 2026; no effective date given. — [Envoy Global](https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/qatar-establishes-framework-for-freelance-and-part-time-work/)
- **Conflicting reading.** One Qatar Living piece says part-time and freelance workers are expressly excluded from the Labour Law's scope, so the second reading may be that they are recognised but regulated separately (search summary). — [Qatar Living](https://www.qatarliving.com/en/article/labour-law-changes-qatar-expat-workers)
- **Qatar Living (checked 9 Aug 2026).**
  - A second job requires an authorised process; an informal verbal "NOC" is not enough.
  - The Ministry of Labour's secondment work permit lets a temporary employer request a worker registered with another establishment.
  - Family-residency holders need a work permit applied for by an establishment.
  - "No universal personal freelance permit" exists; self-employment needs a commercial or professional licence.
  - The home-project framework covers 63 activities after a 2024 expansion (translation, programming, photography and others), applied for through the MoCI Single Window.
  - Online platforms "do not remove Qatar's local rules on the right to work".
  - PayPal supports sending, receiving and withdrawing for Qatar accounts.
  - Source: [Qatar Living](https://www.qatarliving.com/en/article/side-hustles-freelancing-qatar-young-people)
- **Family residents.** A June 2025 report describes a regulated path for family-residency holders to join the labour market. — [The Peninsula](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/20/06/2025/mol-initiatives-ease-family-residency-holders-to-join-workforce)
- **Employer cost and EOR.**
  - Expatriates are not covered by GRSIA, so no social insurance is paid.
  - Gratuity is at least 3 weeks' basic pay per year of service, about 5.77% of basic.
  - Qatari nationals: 14% employer and 7% employee under Law 1 of 2022, though another source gives 10% for GCC nationals.
  - Statutory load is about 5.8% on top of gross.
  - EOR fees start at about $599/month.
  - Source (search summary; vendor sources): [Teamed](https://www.teamed.global/gulf-employment-law/qatar-end-of-service-gratuity), [RemotePass](https://remotepass.com/country/qatar/taxes), [EORHQ](https://eorhq.com/compare/best-eor-for-qatar/)

### Inferences
- **Status quo for expatriates.** Until the Law 9/2026 implementing decisions are issued, an expatriate teacher in Qatar has no clear self-serve route to invoice a foreign company. The secondment permit needs a Qatar-registered requesting establishment.
- **Qatari nationals** are not tied to a sponsor. They may still need a commercial or home-project licence to invoice as a business; no source addressed this directly.
- **Status: unresolved.** Watch for the implementing decisions.

### Gaps
- No Ministry of Labour (mol.gov.qa) or Hukoomi page was read.
- No source on Qatari nationals freelancing for foreign companies.
- Tutoring is not confirmed among the 63 home-project activities.
- No Deel Qatar price.

## Cross-cutting: permanent establishment, data protection and feasibility ranking

### Takeaway
PE risk for Alimun from independent teachers working at home is low. The OECD's November 2025 commentary treats a home office as generally not a PE where less than 50% of working time is spent there, and where remote work is for the worker's own preference rather than a commercial reason of the enterprise. Contractors who neither conclude contracts for Alimun nor work at a place at its disposal are lower risk still. Data-protection duties: Saudi PDPL is in force with SCCs. UAE PDPL executive regulations appear still unpublished as of mid-to-late 2026, so transfers rely on the statute (Articles 22-23) plus SCCs.

### Cited Findings
- **OECD 2025 Commentary on Article 5 (19 Nov 2025).**
  - A home office is not a PE if under 50% of working time is spent there over 12 months.
  - At 50% or more, the facts decide; remote work for employee preference, retention or cost saving is not a "commercial reason".
  - It can still be a PE where a founder or key decision-maker performs the main business from there.
  - Countries may interpret this differently.
  - Source (search summary): [PwC Belgium](https://www.pwc.be/en/news-publications/news/2025/will-employees-working-from-home-trigger-a-pe-new-commentaries-from-the-oecd.html), [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_ch/technical/tax-alerts/oecd-2025-update-new-rules-on-permanent-establishment-for-remote-work), [BLG](https://www.blg.com/en/insights/2026/03/permanent-establishment-and-remote-work-oecds-2025-update-and-your-organization)
- **Saudi Arabia** abolished the virtual service PE in 2023. — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/sa/en/home/insights/2023/05/tax-alert-updates-on-the-concept-of-service-pe.html)
- **UAE PDPL (Decree-Law 45/2021).**
  - Executive regulations: unpublished as of Feb 2026 (Kayrouz) and mid-2026 (Recording Law).
  - One weak source claims they were issued in 2026 with a 1 Jan 2027 compliance date.
  - Transfers rely on Articles 22-23 plus SCCs, BCRs or derogations.
  - Source: [Kayrouz & Associates](https://www.kayrouzandassociates.com/insights/cross-border-data-transfers-under-uae-law-in-2026), [Cybersigma](https://cybersigmacs.com/knowledge-center/uae-pdpl/)
- **Saudi PDPL** has been in force since Sep 2024, with transfer regulation and SCCs (Sep 2024). — [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2024/09/saudi-arabia-s-personal-data-protection-law-become), [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/data-technology/saudi-arabia-updates-data-transfer-regulations-and-introduces-the-first-set-of-standard-contractual-clauses)

### Inferences
- **PE risk for Alimun is low in all three countries,** provided teachers are genuinely independent:
  - teachers do not sign up students or conclude contracts on Alimun's behalf;
  - no office is provided;
  - teachers work for others too.
  - Neither the UAE nor Qatar taxes individuals' income. The UAE has 9% corporate tax and Qatar 10% on foreign companies' PEs (Qatar rate not sourced here; flagged).
- **Feasibility ranking for contractor engagement**, most to least:
  1. **UAE.** Clear permits exist: a free tutor permit covering online group teaching with an employer NOC, or a paid freelance permit. There is no tax burden below the thresholds. Make a valid permit a contract condition.
  2. **Saudi nationals with a Freelance Document,** if a teaching or training activity is listed (unconfirmed).
  3. **Qatar.** No clear route until the Law 9/2026 implementing decisions are issued. Qatari nationals may be possible with a commercial licence.
  4. **Saudi expatriates.** Iqama holders may work only for their sponsor; this is effectively not lawful without the sponsor's involvement.
- **EOR economics.** EOR ($599-999 a month plus statutory costs) is uneconomic for part-time hourly teachers at Alimun's rate card. The only lawful alternative for expatriate teachers in Saudi Arabia and Qatar is to avoid recruiting them, or to accept them only with documented sponsor permission.
- **Payment rails.** PayPal works in Qatar. Saudi sources advise against receiving non-employer income into a Saudi bank account. UAE rails were not researched (Wise, Payoneer and Stripe Connect availability unverified).
