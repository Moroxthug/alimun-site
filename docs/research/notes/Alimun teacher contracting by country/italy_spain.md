# Engaging remote language teachers in Italy and Spain: contractor routes, risks and alternatives (2025-2026)

Not legal advice. Research date: 4 Nov 2026. Most sources are Italian and Spanish tax and HR guide sites, plus some law-firm and vendor briefings. Very few are official (INPS, AEAT, BOE, Normattiva); where official text was not retrieved this is flagged. Search snippets were checked where possible, but several numbers below come from secondary aggregators and should be confirmed against the primary text before use.

## Italy: prestazione occasionale, partita IVA forfettario, co.co.co./art. 2 d.lgs. 81/2015, and employment cost

### Takeaway
For an Italian teacher working regularly (weekly classes for 8-10 weeks, repeatedly), the realistic lawful contractor route is a partita IVA, normally in regime forfettario. That teacher invoices the foreign company with no VAT (outside scope, art. 7-ter DPR 633/72), and Alimun withholds nothing because it is not an Italian sostituto d'imposta. Prestazione occasionale only fits genuinely sporadic work. The main legal risk is art. 2 d.lgs. 81/2015: since 2019, collaborations that are "organised by the client, including through digital platforms" get employee protections. A matching engine that assigns times, ranks teachers by reliability and sanctions late cancellations is close to the riders fact pattern. Italy is also transposing the Platform Work Directive for all platform work, with a final decree approved on 28 Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Prestazione occasionale**
- A foreign client with no Italian permanent establishment is not a sostituto d'imposta. It therefore does not apply the 20% ritenuta d'acconto, and the Italian resident teacher declares and pays the tax on the income themselves — [Fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/ricevuta-senza-partita-iva-2/); [Trend Online](https://www.trend-online.com/fisco-tasse/prestazione-occasionale-committente-estero-tasse/)
- Exception: withholding obligations can arise if the foreign client has a stable organisation in Italy or files Italian returns — [Fiscomania](https://fiscomania.com/lavoro-autonomo-occasionale-estero/)
- €5,000/year (total across all clients) is a social-security threshold. Above it the worker must register with Gestione Separata INPS, and contributions are due only on the excess. Normally 2/3 of the contribution falls on the client and 1/3 on the worker, and the client remains responsible for paying it. Sources say a foreign client "registers with INPS services as such", but no INPS source specific to foreign clients was found — [Fiscozen superamento 5.000](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/superamento-5000-euro/); [Informazione Fiscale](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/prestazione-occasionale-superamento-limite-5000-euro-cosa-fare); [Lentepubblica](https://lentepubblica.it/cittadini-e-imprese/prestazione-occasionale-cosa-succede-se-supero-i-5000-euro/)
- The €5,000 threshold is social-security only. Whether a partita IVA is needed depends on whether the activity is habitual ("abitualità"), not on the amount — [Fiscozen vantaggi/limiti](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/vantaggi-limiti-prestazione-occasionale/)
- Sources disagree on the Gestione Separata rate for occasional workers above €5,000 (33.72% vs about 33%) — same sources as above.

**Partita IVA, regime forfettario**
- 2026 revenue cap is €85,000. Between €85k and €100k the worker leaves the regime the next year; above €100k they leave immediately. The cap is prorated when the activity starts mid-year. Professionals' profitability coefficient is typically 78% (depends on the ATECO code). The 5% start-up rate lasts 5 years (no similar activity in the prior 3 years, not a continuation of a previous activity, no prevalence of a former employer). Otherwise the rate is 15%. The €35,000 limit on employment income was extended for 2026 by the Budget Law — [Ediltecnico guida forfettario 2026](https://ediltecnico.it/guida-regime-forfettario-professionisti-tecnici/); [PartitaIVA.it](https://www.partitaiva.it/page_category/regime-forfettario); [TuttoCalcolato](https://tuttocalcolato.it/blog/partita-iva-forfettaria-2026-guida/)
- Invoices to non-EU business clients carry no VAT because the service is outside Italian VAT territoriality under art. 7-ter DPR 633/72. Natura code N2.1, with wording such as "Operazione non soggetta – art. 7-ter". Sources disagree on the exact wording (one cites art. 8 "non imponibile") — [Fiscomania fattura servizi estero](https://fiscomania.com/fattura-prestazioni-servizi-estero/); [Fatture in Cloud](https://www.fattureincloud.it/guida-regime-forfettario/fattura-estera/); [QuickFisco](https://quickfisco.it/guide/fattura-estera-regime-forfettario-guida-ecommerce/)
- Gestione Separata for professionals without their own pension fund: 26.07% in 2025 (25% IVS + 0.72% + 0.35% ISCRO), per INPS circolare n. 27 of 30 Jan 2025. The 2025 ceiling is €120,607 and the minimum €18,555. The 2026 rate is reported unchanged at 26.07% (circolare n. 8 of 3 Feb 2026, not verified), with a reported 2026 ceiling of €122,741 — [INPS circ. 27/2025 PDF](https://informazionefiscale.it/IMG/pdf/15871_circolare-numero-27-del-30-01-2025.pdf); [Informazione Fiscale 2025](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/Gestione-separata-INPS-2025-aliquote-contributi); [Informazione Fiscale 2026](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/Aliquote-gestione-separata-INPS-2026-importo-contributi); [Edotto 2026](https://edotto.com/articolo/gestione-separata-inps-ecco-gli-importi-2026)

**Art. 2 d.lgs. 81/2015 (etero-organizzazione) and the riders case law**
- Art. 2 covers collaborations that are mainly personal and continuous and whose way of being carried out is organised by the client, "anche mediante piattaforme digitali" — [Ministero del Lavoro](https://lavoro.gov.it/en/node/13710)
- DL 101/2019 (L. 128/2019) removed the earlier requirement that the client predetermine times and places of work, and extended the rule expressly to organisation through platforms — [Cass. 1663/2020 commentary, dirittoantidiscriminatorio PDF](https://dirittoantidiscriminatorio.it/app/uploads/2020/12/Cassazione-1663-2020-riders.pdf); [Dottrina Lavoro](https://www.dottrinalavoro.it/notizie-c/articolo-le-collaborazioni-dopo-la-sentenza-della-cassazione-sui-riders)
- Cassazione n. 1663 of 24 Jan 2020 (Foodinho/Foodora riders) dismissed the platform's appeal. It read art. 2 as a rule that extends the protections of subordinate employment to collaborations that are not subordinate, with an anti-avoidance purpose (older than 2023, but still the leading case) — [Studio Cataldi](https://www.studiocataldi.it/articoli/37188-rider-la-sentenza-della-cassazione.asp); [Fisco e Tasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/3081-cassazione-i-riders-sono-lavoratori-etero-organizzati-non-autonomi.html)
- The labour inspectorate's guidance on enforcing the post-2019 rules is INL Circolare n. 7 of 30 Oct 2020 (older than 2023) — [Ispettorato](https://www.ispettorato.gov.it/files/2023/01/Circolare-n-7-del-30-10-2020-signed.pdf)

**Platform Work Directive (EU 2024/2831) in Italy**
- The transposition deadline is 2 Dec 2026. The directive creates a rebuttable presumption of employment where facts indicate control and direction, puts the burden of proof on the platform, and applies regardless of where the platform is established — [Ius Laboris](https://www.iuslaboris.com/insights/eu-platform-work-directive-which-countries-have-implemented/)
- A vendor tracker (checked 3 Nov 2026) reports the following:
  - DL 62/2026 has been in force since 1 May 2026 and was converted by L. 112/2026. It creates a presumption of subordinate employment for riders where algorithmic control indicators are present.
  - The full transposing decree (Atto del Governo n. 433) received preliminary approval on 23 Jul 2026 and final approval on 28 Oct 2026, but was not yet published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale.
  - Parliamentary committees gave favourable opinions on 29 Sep and 1 Oct 2026.
  - The decree covers all platform work, not just riders.

  Source: [Teamed Italy tracker](https://www.teamed.global/eu-platform-work/italy). A different secondary source dates the government approval to 24 Jul 2026 — [RemoteWorkEurope](https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/italy-platform-work-directive-transposition-draft). Not verified against the Gazzetta Ufficiale or Normattiva.

**Employment alternative (Italy)**
- CCNL Scuole private laiche (ANINSEI) was renewed on 15 Jun 2024 and runs from 1 Sep 2024 to 31 Dec 2027. Level V ("insegnante lingue") has a monthly minimum of about €1,539.64 from 1 Jan 2025, €1,589.64 from 1 Jan 2026 (€20,665.32/yr over 13 months) and €1,619.64 from 2027. There is a €20/month seniority supplement after 2 years — [Fisco e Tasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/new-rassegna-stampa/1050-ccnl-scuole-non-statali-2024-aumenti-e-altre-novita-del-rinnovo.html); [Edotto](https://edotto.com/articolo/scuole-private-laiche-aninsei-nuovi-minimi-e-salario-di-anzianita); [Leggeinchiaro](https://leggeinchiaro.it/ccnl/aninsei-scuola-privata-laica/)
- Employer social contributions are typically about 29-32% of gross. One table shows a 23.81% employer IVS share, with INAIL on top. TFR accrues at about 6.91% of pay, and most CCNLs require a 13th month — [Deel Italy employer costs](https://www.deel.com/blog/employer-costs-for-an-employee-in-italy/); [Eurodev](https://www.eurodev.com/blog/payroll-in-italy); [Teamed cost breakdown](https://www.teamed.global/country-hiring-guides/italy/cost-breakdown)
- A foreign company does not need an Italian seat to pay contributions in the state where the work is done (Reg. 987/2009 art. 21) — [Lawants](https://www.lawants.com/es/teletrabajo-en-espana-de-un-empleado-italiano-como-esta-regulado/)

### Inferences
- If Alimun stays a Canadian company with no Italian establishment, an Italian forfettario teacher bills Alimun with no VAT and no ritenuta. Alimun's only obligations are to keep the invoice and the contract. On €20,000 of fees, the teacher pays about 26.07% INPS on 78% of fees, plus 5% or 15% substitute tax on 78% net of INPS. That is a substantial cost for the teacher at €13-20/h, so rates may need to account for it.
- If Alimun moves to Italy, it becomes a sostituto d'imposta. It would then withhold 20% on payments to ordinary-regime professionals and occasional workers, but not on forfettario invoices: forfettari are exempt from ritenuta, a standard rule that was not separately sourced here, so confirm it. It would also have to handle the INPS 2/3 share for any occasional worker above €5,000 or any co.co.co.
- The prestazione occasionale is unsuitable for a teacher with recurring weekly groups. Recurring weekly work points to habitual activity (needing a partita IVA) and, when organised by the platform, to an art. 2 collaboration.
- These platform features look most like art. 2 etero-organizzazione and the PWD presumption indicators:
  - assigning fixed class times the teacher cannot reshape;
  - reliability scores and loss of priority for late cancellation (sanctions and algorithmic performance supervision);
  - mandatory curriculum delivered in a set way;
  - controlling the rate unilaterally;
  - restricting work for others;
  - keeping the teacher on a group for 8-10 weeks.

  Lower-risk designs:
  - teachers propose their own slots and class formats, and may decline freely without score penalties;
  - substitution is allowed (another qualified teacher);
  - the rate is negotiated or chosen from published bands;
  - the curriculum is offered as a resource rather than a mandated script;
  - cancellation consequences are contractual (a fee and the obligation to find a cover) rather than an algorithmic ranking demotion;
  - human review of any account restriction.

  This mapping is an inference. Neither the Italian decree text nor the indicator list was retrieved.

### Gaps
- No primary INPS or Agenzia delle Entrate guidance was found on how a foreign client with no Italian presence pays the 2/3 INPS share for occasional workers above €5,000. In practice this may be unenforceable or impractical, so it needs professional confirmation.
- The text of Atto del Governo 433 (presumption criteria, any sector scope, effective date) was not retrieved, and it was unconfirmed whether it has been published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale.
- Post-2023 Cassazione case law on art. 2 outside riders (e.g. teachers or tutors on platforms) was not found. The Foodinho/Glovo Garante fine and Tribunale Milano 2024 decisions were not checked.
- The CCNL formazione professionale hourly rates for "lettori"/language-school hourly teachers were not retrieved. ANINSEI monthly minimums are full-time figures.
- Whether teaching services by individuals could be VAT-exempt under art. 10 DPR 633/72 is irrelevant if the client is non-EU (outside scope), but it matters if Alimun moves to Italy. Not researched.

## Spain: autónomo, TRADE, falso autónomo, Ley Rider, IRPF retention, VAT, and employment cost

### Takeaway
A Spanish teacher can lawfully invoice a non-EU company as an autónomo. The invoice carries no Spanish VAT (not subject, art. 69.Uno.1º LIVA) and no 15%/7% IRPF retention, because a foreign payer without a Spanish permanent establishment has no duty to withhold. The teacher pays the income-based cuota, or the €80 tarifa plana in the first year. If Alimun provides at least 75% of a teacher's income, that teacher is a TRADE and gets extra rights. The bigger risk is falso autónomo reclassification under the Supreme Court's Glovo doctrine (STS 805/2020) and the coming Platform Work Directive presumption.

### Cited Findings
**Autónomo contributions**
- 15 net-income brackets (reduced table 1-3, general table 4-15). In 2025 monthly cuotas run from about €200 (net income ≤€670/month) to about €590 (more than €6,000/month), e.g. bracket 13 (€3,620-4,050) pays €490 — [Alicante Plaza](https://alicanteplaza.es/alicanteplaza/losautonomospagaranen2025cuotasmensualesde200a590eurosdependiendodesusingresos); [Deia](https://www.deia.eus/economia/2024/12/30/pagaran-autonomos-cuotas-mensuales-2025-9106612.html)
- Tarifa plana: €80/month for the first 12 months. It can be extended for a second year if net income is below the SMI. One source says up to 36 months, which is unverified. Users on the tarifa plana also pay the MEI share — [INEAF](https://www.ineaf.es/tribuna/nuevo-sistema-de-cotizacion-para-autonomos-o-trabajadores-por-cuenta-propia/); [TeamSystem](https://teamsystem.es/magazine/cuota-autonomos/)
- 2026: the bracket structure is unchanged. Sources disagree on the total rate (31.2%, 31.3% or 31.5% with MEI at 0.9%, citing Orden PJC/297/2026) and on the bracket-1 cuota (about €200-230). The minimum base for corporate autónomos is €1,424.40 in 2026 — [UPTA on Orden cotización 2026](https://upta.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RES-ORDEN-COTIZACION-2026-RETA.pdf); [OIE](https://www.oie.es/cuotas-de-autonomos/); [Billin](https://www.billin.net/blog/cuotas-autonomos-2023-2031/)

**IRPF retention and VAT**
- The payer is the party obliged to withhold. A foreign client that is not obliged to withhold in Spain does not apply the 15% (or the 7% for new autónomos). The rule changes if the client has a permanent establishment in Spain — [Billeo](https://www.billeo.es/blog/factura-con-retencion-de-irpf-cuando-y-cuanto-7-o-15); [GoCardless](https://gocardless.com/es/guias/consejos/factura-con-retencion-irpf); [Fiscal-Impuestos (cliente alemán)](https://www.fiscal-impuestos.com/node/3952)
- Services to a business established outside the EU are not subject to Spanish VAT (art. 69.Uno.1º Ley 37/1992). The invoice should state "Operación no sujeta – art. 69.Uno.1º", and the teacher should keep evidence of the client's foreign tax ID — [Billeo](https://www.billeo.es/blog/iva-facturas-clientes-extranjeros-autonomo-espana); [Infoautónomos](https://www.infoautonomos.com/facturas/como-facturar-clientes-extranjeros/); [Supercontable (US consulting)](https://www.supercontable.com/boletin/C/articulos/tributacion_IVA_IRPF_prestacion_servicios_consultoria_a_EEUU.html)
- The education exemption art. 20.Uno.10º LIVA covers only private lessons by individuals on subjects in official curricula. Academies fall under 20.Uno.9º, which requires public or authorised private entities — [Legálitas](https://www.legalitas.com/actualidad/formacion-iva); [Conversor CNAE](https://www.conversoriaecnae.es/iva/actividades-exentas-de-iva)

**TRADE**
- A TRADE earns at least 75% of income from one client, under art. 11 Ley 20/2007. The contract must be in writing, state TRADE status, and be registered with the SEPE (TAED register). Without registration the TRADE rights cannot be invoked. TRADEs get 18 working days of annual rest and compensation for unjustified termination — [Taxfix](https://taxfix.com/es-es/autonomos/autonomos-trade/); [PayFit](https://payfit.com/es/contenido-practico/trabajador-autonomo-economicamente-dependiente/); [Holded](https://www.holded.com/es/blog/autonomo-trade)

**Falso autónomo and the Ley Rider**
- STS 805/2020 (Pleno, 25 Sep 2020, rec. 4746/2019) held a Glovo rider to be an employee. Riders were "inserted in the employer's work organisation", lacked their own business organisation, did not negotiate prices, and Glovo owned the essential assets, i.e. the app (older than 2023, but still the leading doctrine) — [Conesa Legal (judgment PDF)](https://www.conesalegal.com/hubfs/S%20TS%2025_9_2020....pdf); [Supercontable](https://www.supercontable.com/informacion/laboral/STS_2924-2020.Sentencia_sobre_los_-riders-_de_.html); [IOE](https://industrialrelationsnews.ioe-emp.org/news/article/spain-supreme-court-decision-on-the-employment-status-of-workers-for-a-delivery-company-and-social-dialogue-process-on-a-riders-law)
- The Ley Rider (RDL 9/2021, later Ley 12/2021) presumes employment only for platforms that deliver or distribute "any consumer product or merchandise", so it does not cover teaching. Its separate right of worker representatives to information about algorithms (ET art. 64.4.d) has no sector limit. Spain still needs to transpose the PWD's general presumption, joint liability through intermediaries, algorithmic-management limits and transparency duties — [Garrigues](https://bloglaboral.garrigues.com/en/riders-law-and-platform-work-directive-challenges-ahead); [Cuatrecasas](https://cuatrecasas.com/en/spain/labor-and-employment/art/eu-regulates-digital-platform-work-iberian-perspective)
- On Spain's transposition, one source reports a public consultation in late April 2026. No Spanish draft text was confirmed — [RemoteWorkEurope](https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/platform-work-directive-enforcement-divergence-2026)

**Employment alternative and foreign employer (Spain)**
- 2026 employer contributions are about 30.65% of the contribution base: 23.60% common contingencies, 5.50% unemployment for open-ended contracts (6.70% for temporary), 0.20% FOGASA, 0.60% training and 0.75% MEI. Workplace accident cover is on top and depends on the CNAE activity code (roughly 1.5% is used as an estimate) — [Cuatrecasas cotización 2026](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/es/spain/laboral/art/seguridad-social-nuevos-topes-bases-cotizacion-2026); [Asepeyo bases y tipos 2026](https://www.asepeyo.es/wp-content/uploads/Bases-tipos-cotizacion-2026-ES.pdf); [CCOO Canarias](https://canarias.ccoo.es/579b76aad30c66b3a5d6bf9a4989fb47000069.pdf)
- A foreign company without a Spanish seat that employs someone living and working in Spain must apply Spanish social security (Reg. 987/2009 art. 21.2). It registers as a foreign employer with the TGSS through Sistema RED, gets a contribution account code and enrols the worker. It must also respect mandatory Spanish rules such as the SMI and working-time limits — [Ministerio de Trabajo note (German employer case)](https://www.mites.gob.es/mundo/consejerias/alemania/ficheros/nota_informativa-trabajo_en_remoto_empresa_en_Alemania.pdf); [Andersen](https://es.andersen.com/estudias-o-teletrabajas-implicaciones-fiscales/)
- DGT V1339-26 (2 Jun 2026): for a Portuguese employer with no Spanish permanent establishment, the employee stays taxable in Spain, but there may be no one obliged to withhold — [Fiscal-Impuestos V1339-2026](https://www.fiscal-impuestos.com/sites/fiscal-impuestos.com/files/V1339-2026.pdf); [Fiscal-Impuestos article](https://www.fiscal-impuestos.com/teletrabajo-espana-tributacion-empresa-extranjera-no-retencion-sin-establecimiento-permanente)

### Inferences
- **Cost to the teacher.** At €15/h for 80 h/month (€1,200), a new autónomo pays about €80/month on the tarifa plana. Afterwards they likely sit in the lower brackets (about €200-300/month), which is significant at these rates. Teachers working few hours may find autónomo status uneconomic. "Not registering because income is below the SMI" is not a safe harbour; the Supreme Court has required habitual activity, not an income test. That point was not sourced here.
- **TRADE status.** If Alimun is a teacher's main client (≥75%), TRADE status applies and Alimun should sign a written TRADE contract. Having TRADEs is lawful but signals dependence. Inspección de Trabajo looks at the same indicators as STS 805/2020.
- **High-risk features under the Spanish tests.** The Spanish dependence and ajenidad tests (whether the worker is integrated into the client's organisation and works for the client's account) and the coming PWD presumption put these features at highest risk:
  - Alimun setting the price unilaterally;
  - Alimun owning the "essential means" (platform, curriculum, learner relationship);
  - algorithmic allocation with reliability ranking and penalties;
  - the teacher having no business organisation of their own and no substitution right.

  Lower-risk design: teachers set or choose rates, can substitute, keep their own clients, are not ranked or penalised algorithmically, and have human review.
- **Remaining structural risk.** As an inference, a pure "Alimun teacher" with no other clients, using only Alimun materials, will always carry some reclassification risk in Spain. Payment for learner no-shows and standby fees resemble guaranteed wages. Framing them as a contractual cancellation fee is the standard B2B equivalent.

### Gaps
- The official 2025/2026 RETA bracket tables could not be retrieved from seg-social.es (the page returned no content). Figures come from secondary press and blogs, and 2026 rate figures conflict.
- No statistics on Inspección de Trabajo campaigns against falsos autónomos in 2024-2026 were found.
- The applicable convenio for an online language academy (enseñanza y formación no reglada, its salary tables and hourly minimums) was not retrieved.
- No confirmed Spanish draft PWD transposition text or date was found.
- The TRADE compensation formula and the 18-day figure were not verified against Ley 20/2007 directly.

## Non-EU company with no establishment, permanent establishment risk, and Employer of Record pricing

### Takeaway
With contractors, a Canadian company with no Italian or Spanish establishment generally has no registration, VAT or withholding duties in either country. The teacher self-assesses tax and social security and invoices outside the VAT scope. Employing directly requires registering as a foreign employer with INPS or TGSS. Employer of Record (EOR) list prices are about US$499-699 per employee per month (Deel, Remote, Oyster), which is heavy relative to part-time teachers earning €13-20/h.

### Cited Findings
- Italy: a foreign client without an establishment is not a sostituto d'imposta, so no ritenuta applies — [Fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/ricevuta-senza-partita-iva-2/)
- Spain: no IRPF retention by a foreign payer without a permanent establishment, and services to a non-EU business are not subject to Spanish VAT — [Billeo](https://www.billeo.es/blog/factura-con-retencion-de-irpf-cuando-y-cuanto-7-o-15); [Infoautónomos](https://www.infoautonomos.com/facturas/como-facturar-clientes-extranjeros/)
- Foreign employer without a seat: Spanish social security applies to residents working in Spain, with TGSS registration via RED. Italy similarly allows contributions without an Italian seat (Reg. 987/2009 art. 21) — [Ministerio de Trabajo](https://www.mites.gob.es/mundo/consejerias/alemania/ficheros/nota_informativa-trabajo_en_remoto_empresa_en_Alemania.pdf); [Lawants](https://www.lawants.com/es/teletrabajo-en-espana-de-un-empleado-italiano-como-esta-regulado/)
- EOR pricing 2026 (aggregators):
  - Deel: about $599 per employee per month (Core plan reportedly $459 annual or $499 monthly; Enterprise $899).
  - Remote: $599, or $699 month-to-month.
  - Oyster: list price about $699, or $499-599 on annual plans.
  - Payroll-only plans: about $25-29 per employee per month.

  Sources: [Costbench Deel vs Remote](https://costbench.com/compare/deel-vs-remote); [Costbench Deel vs Oyster](https://www.costbench.com/compare/deel-vs-oyster-hr/); [Gloroots Oyster](https://www.gloroots.com/blog/oysterhr-pricing); [WhichPayroll](https://whichpayroll.com/alternatives/oyster)
- The PWD applies to persons doing platform work in the EU "regardless of where the platform is established", so a Canadian company is not outside its scope — [Ius Laboris](https://www.iuslaboris.com/insights/eu-platform-work-directive-which-countries-have-implemented/)

### Inferences
- **EOR cost per teacher.** At about $599/month plus employer contributions (about 30% in both countries) plus TFR in Italy, an EOR teacher on 40 h/month at €16/h (€640 gross) would cost roughly €640 + €195 + about €550 fee, i.e. well over double the contractor cost. EOR only makes sense for near-full-time teachers. Direct foreign-employer registration with INPS/TGSS, run through a local payroll provider (about $25-29 per month platform fee plus local accountant), is much cheaper but carries full employer obligations.
- **Permanent establishment risk.** Contractor teachers delivering classes from home do not obviously create a permanent establishment, because they cannot conclude contracts on Alimun's behalf. An employee working from home habitually, or a local manager, raises the risk more (OECD home-office reasoning). This is unsourced here and needs tax advice.
- **If Alimun moves to Italy.** It becomes an Italian sostituto d'imposta (withholding on occasional and ordinary professionals) and receives EU B2B services from Spanish teachers. A Spanish autónomo would then invoice without VAT under the reverse charge (art. 69.Uno.1º, EU business client), and Alimun self-assesses Italian VAT unless the education exemption applies. Not researched in depth.

### Gaps
- Country-specific EOR quotes for Italy and Spain from vendor pricing pages were not obtained (Italy is often surcharged). The figures above are global list prices from aggregators.
- No authoritative source on permanent establishment risk from remote contractor teachers in Italy or Spain was found.
- Canada–Italy and Canada–Spain tax treaty treatment of independent personal services was not reviewed.

## Ranking of options by cost and risk (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Ranked from lowest cost to highest:
1. Partita IVA forfettario (Italy) or autónomo (Spain) contractors, with platform design that preserves autonomy. Low cost, moderate reclassification risk.
2. Prestazione occasionale. Cheapest, but only lawful for genuinely occasional work, so high misuse risk for recurring classes.
3. Contractors under the current tightly controlled matching design. Low cost now, but high and rising risk as the PWD presumption arrives in December 2026.
4. Direct employment via foreign-employer registration. About 30% contributions, plus TFR and 13th month in Italy.
5. EOR. Same employment costs plus about $500-700 per teacher per month.

### Cited Findings
- Each cost element is cited in the sections above, e.g. Gestione Separata 26.07% ([INPS circ. 27/2025](https://informazionefiscale.it/IMG/pdf/15871_circolare-numero-27-del-30-01-2025.pdf)), tarifa plana €80 ([INEAF](https://www.ineaf.es/tribuna/nuevo-sistema-de-cotizacion-para-autonomos-o-trabajadores-por-cuenta-propia/)), Spanish employer contributions about 30.65% ([Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/es/spain/laboral/art/seguridad-social-nuevos-topes-bases-cotizacion-2026)), EOR about $599 ([Costbench](https://costbench.com/compare/deel-vs-remote)), PWD deadline 2 Dec 2026 ([Ius Laboris](https://www.iuslaboris.com/insights/eu-platform-work-directive-which-countries-have-implemented/)).

### Inferences
- **Which features to change first.** The platform features most likely to trigger reclassification in both countries are: reliability ranking with loss of priority, first-to-accept dispatch tied to platform-set slots, a unilateral rate card, a mandatory curriculum and platform, an exclusive 8-10-week assignment, and guaranteed pay for no-shows or standby. These are inferences from the art. 2 / STS 805/2020 indicators and the PWD's "control and direction" test.
- **Lawful alternatives**, which reduce but do not remove risk:
  - teacher-set availability and opt-in classes with no penalty for declining;
  - substitution rights;
  - negotiated or teacher-chosen rates within bands;
  - cancellation fees and replacement duties written into the contract instead of score demotion;
  - human review of any account action;
  - encouraging teachers to have other clients;
  - written B2B contracts (TRADE contracts where 75% dependence exists in Spain).

### Gaps
- No lawyer-validated mapping of these specific features to Italian and Spanish PWD indicators was found, because the national texts were not retrieved.
