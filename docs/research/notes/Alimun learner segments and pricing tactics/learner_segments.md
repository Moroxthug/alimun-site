# Why adults pay for live online language classes: segment map (2024-2026)

Scope note: research done 26 Oct 2026, about 23 tool calls. Migration statistics and price levels were deliberately left out (covered elsewhere). Many willingness-to-pay, retention and segment-share questions have **no public data**. These are listed under Gaps and must not be filled with invented numbers. Items marked **[ESTIMATE]** are reasoned inferences, not sourced figures.

## What share of learners does each motivation account for? (cross-segment survey data)

### Takeaway
Large public surveys put travel, culture and "keeping the brain sharp" ahead of career for the *app* population, mostly in rich countries. In emerging markets, and for English and German, career and education lead. No survey we found breaks out learners who *pay for live classes* by motivation. The best public proxies are skewed toward apps and toward the US and EU.

### Cited Findings
- Duolingo Language Report 2024: education is the most common reason for studying English worldwide. English learners are mostly driven by academic and professional goals, and their motivations differ from Spanish and French learners. "Connecting with people" is the top reason for English in the US (35%) and in South Korea (about one third; education second at 23%). — [Duolingo 2024 Language Report](https://blog.duolingo.com/2024-duolingo-language-report/)
- Duolingo 2024: travel (22%) is the top reason Argentinians study Portuguese. 32% of Chinese learners of Japanese and 29% of Japanese learners of Korean study "just for fun". — [Duolingo 2024 Language Report](https://blog.duolingo.com/2024-duolingo-language-report/)
- The Duolingo report gives no motivation figures for Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, India, Turkey, Egypt, Morocco or Canada. — [Duolingo 2024 Language Report](https://blog.duolingo.com/2024-duolingo-language-report/)
- India (Duolingo/YouGov, year not clearly stated, roughly 2024): career growth 55%, travel and cultural exploration 45%, global social interactions 42%, entertainment 40%. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/71-of-indian-learners-excited-about-emerging-technology-duolingo-report-49381.htm)
- US adults (Duolingo/Talker Research, n=2,000, 19 Nov 2024): reasons for learning are cultural interests 31%, keeping the brain sharp 27% and enhancing travel 27%. 30% say they now learn "for personal fulfillment rather than necessity" and 29% say they have more time. — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/never-too-late-to-learn)
- Babbel user surveys (**OLD, 2016**): 56% of global users name communicating better while travelling as a top motivator. In a six-country survey (n>5,000), 53% said travel, 46% said interest in the language and 35% said keeping mentally fit. Career or financial reasons were highest among Italian users (18%), then German (12%) and French (10%), and lowest in the US (5%). — [Babbel 2016 press release](https://press.babbel.com/en/releases/2016-01-20-Babbel_Global_User_Survey.html); [Babbel Magazine](https://www.babbel.com/en/magazine/why-are-people-really-learning-languages-babbel-user-survey)
- Preply Language and Global Career Mobility Report (July 2026, n=1,869 in the UK, US and Canada): 93% would use employer-provided language learning. 97% say practising with a real person matters to their progress. 72% believe apps alone cannot get them to fluency. 44% lost a job or know someone who did in the past two years (63% among Gen Z). Company-sponsored. — [Workplace Journal](https://workplacejournal.co.uk/2026/07/nine-in-10-workers-would-use-employer-funded-language-learning-study-finds/)
- Goethe-Institut / German Foreign Office, "Deutsch als Fremdsprache weltweit" 2025: German is in demand worldwide "vor allem als Berufssprache" (mainly as a language for work). Demand grows especially where Germany is seen as a destination for work, vocational training (Ausbildung) or study; Egypt, India, Kenya and Colombia are named. In Cameroon, Morocco, Nepal and Uzbekistan, German is increasingly tied to professional mobility. German is losing ground as a school subject. There are about 15.5 million learners of German, and 1.1 million Goethe exams were taken in 2025. — [Goethe-Institut press release 28 Jul 2025](https://www.goethe.de/de/uun/prs/pma/26859595.html) (403 on fetch; figures taken from the search snippet); [Goethe publication page](https://www.goethe.de/de/m/uun/pub/dlz.html)
- Eurostat AES 2022: 25.3% of EU adults aged 25-64 know no foreign language, 37.6% know one and 24.7% know two. The public Statistics Explained page does not publish a breakdown of reasons for learning. — [Eurostat Foreign language skills statistics](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php/Foreign_language_skills_statistics)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** For Alimun's markets (Morocco, LatAm, India, Egypt, Turkey, Tunisia, Balkans), instrumental motives dominate paid English and German: career, migration, exams and study. Hobby and travel motives dominate Italian, Spanish and French among EU and Canadian buyers and retirees. The Babbel and Duolingo data are biased toward rich-country app users, so they overstate the hobby share for Alimun's core emerging markets.
- The 72%/97% "need a real person" figures (Preply 2026) are the strongest public argument for live classes over apps, but they come from a vendor of live tutoring.

### Gaps
- No public survey splits *paying live-class* learners by motivation, in any country.
- The Duolingo 2025 report's global motivation breakdown could not be retrieved.
- The Eurostat AES "reasons for participating in learning" tables are not language-specific in the public release.
- No Busuu or Lingoda motivation percentages from 2023-2026 were found. Lingoda's only public item is a 2021 press release with no breakdown ([presseportal](https://www.presseportal.de/en/pm/143201/4821735)).

## (a) Moving abroad to work: skilled workers, nurses and carers, Ausbildung, tech, seasonal, nomads

### Takeaway
This is the dominant motive for German worldwide, and it is concentrated in Morocco, Egypt, India, Colombia, Tunisia and the Balkans. These learners buy intensive A1-to-B1/B2 tracks against a hard deadline: visa, recognition (Anerkennung) or the employer's start date. Often an employer, a recruiter or a public voucher pays.

### Cited Findings
- German demand is driven by work and vocational training, with Egypt, India, Colombia and Morocco named. — [Goethe-Institut 2025](https://www.goethe.de/de/uun/prs/pma/26859595.html)
- In Germany, employers and social-sector providers are themselves responsible for recruiting and training foreign care staff. — [Stadt Braunschweig council document](https://ratsinfo.braunschweig.de/integration/wicket/resource/org.apache.wicket.Application/doc1735366.pdf)
- German for care professions at B2 is offered free (€0) with a Bildungsgutschein (Agentur für Arbeit / Jobcenter, AZAV-certified). Once a candidate is in Germany, publicly funded courses compete for the same learner. — [IBB/Viona course flyer S-3066-4](https://vuc.ibb.com/64e5b0b9-5130-4dbb-b5de-640688f3eb56/kursflyer-fuer-arbeitssuchende/S-3066-4.pdf)
- Courses for care staff in Austria target B1 to B2. — [BFI Salzburg 2025](https://www.bfi-sbg.at/downloads/gesundheitsberufe/produktinfo_deutsch-fuer_pflegeberufe_12_2025.pdf)
- The Federal Employment Agency publishes a fact sheet on recruiting foreign nursing staff. — [Arbeitsagentur 2024](https://arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/datei/2024-52-pfelegefachkraefte-ausland-fs_ba210717.pdf)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** What they buy: high intensity (8-20 h/week), small groups or private lessons, morning and evening slots, a 6-12 month path to B1/B2, plus exam prep (Goethe/telc/ÖSD). Retention is driven by the goal and ends at the exam or the move. They are price-sensitive when paying themselves, but lifetime value per learner is high because each level is a separate purchase.
- Strongest pairs: German × Morocco/Egypt/Tunisia/India/Colombia/Balkans/Turkey; French × Morocco/Tunisia (for Canada/Quebec, see segment c); Italian × Tunisia/Egypt for work in Italy **[ESTIMATE]**.
- Digital nomads and seasonal workers: no data found. **[ESTIMATE]** These are small niches with low intensity.

### Gaps
- No data on what share of foreign nurses or Ausbildung candidates pay for courses themselves versus having them paid by an employer or recruiter, and no figures on dropout between levels.

## (b) Career upskilling without moving: remote USD/EUR jobs, BPO, business English

### Takeaway
English is the gate to remote work paid in USD or EUR and to customer-facing BPO work. All of Alimun's English markets sit in the EF EPI "Low" band, which means a large gap between demand and supply. Employers report a skills gap and pay more for good English. These learners buy business and conversation English, often in evening slots, sustained over months.

### Cited Findings
- EF EPI 2025 (123 countries, 2.2 million test takers in 2024): Morocco 492 (#68, up from #76), Turkey 488, India 484 (#74; global average 488), Brazil 482, Colombia 480 (#76), Egypt 458/459 (#89), Mexico 440 (#103). — [Wikipedia EF EPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EF_English_Proficiency_Index); [EF fact sheet India](https://www.ef.com/assetscdn/WIBIwq6RdJvcD9bc8RMd/cefcom-epi-site/fact-sheets/2025/ef-epi-fact-sheet-india-english.pdf); [EF fact sheet Mexico](https://www.ef.com/assetscdn/WIBIwq6RdJvcD9bc8RMd/cefcom-epi-site/fact-sheets/2025/ef-epi-fact-sheet-mexico-english.pdf); [EF fact sheet Egypt](https://www.ef.com/assetscdn/WIBIwq6RdJvcD9bc8RMd/cefcom-epi-site/fact-sheets/2025/ef-epi-fact-sheet-egypt-english.pdf); [EF fact sheet Morocco](https://www.ef.com/assetscdn/WIBIwq6RdJvcD9bc8RMd/cefcom-epi-site/fact-sheets/2025/ef-epi-fact-sheet-morocco-english.pdf); [EF fact sheet Colombia](https://www.ef.com/assetscdn/WIBIwq6RdJvcD9bc8RMd/cefcom-epi-site/fact-sheets/2025/ef-epi-fact-sheet-colombia-english.pdf)
- Cambridge English / QS "English at Work" (**OLD, 2016**, about 5,300 employers in 38 countries): more than two thirds say English is important to their business. More than 40% report an English skills gap in recruitment (25% at middle and upper management). In non-English-speaking countries, 49% of job tasks need advanced English, 33% intermediate, 8% basic and 7% native-level. 57% offer a better starting package to candidates with good English. — [Cambridge executive summary](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/images/335794-english-at-work-executive-summary.pdf); [PIE News](https://thepienews.com/english-skills-provoke-workplace-premiums/)
- Revelo (**2022**): more than two thirds of LatAm tech talent have intermediate English or better, and about half have advanced English. Brazil's English is more variable outside major cities and senior roles. — [Revelo 2024 report](https://revelo.com/latin-america-tech-talent-report-2024/); [BusinessWire 2022](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220908005872/en/Revelo-Releases-First-%E2%80%9CLatin-America-Remote-Tech-Talent-Report%E2%80%9D)
- LatAm remote pay (vendor data, no stated method): software engineers earn about $45k in Mexico, $49k in Brazil, $42k in Argentina and $40k in Colombia per year, and customer support reps $22-27k. — [Pros Marketplace 2025](https://prosmarketplace.com/blog/latam-job-market/)
- Egypt BPO: French-speaking customer service pays EGP 20,120-55,000 per month and requires French at C1 and English at B1. — [TTEC Jobs](https://www.ttecjobs.com/en/french-speaking-customer-service-jobs-in-egypt-requirements-and-salary)
- Morocco is positioned as the leading nearshore BPO hub for French-speaking European buyers, with wages 60-70% below France (vendor claim). Moroccan agents earn about $17,600 a year (unsourced vendor figure). — [Stealth Agents](https://stealthagents.com/research/morocco-bpo-statistics-2026); [FusionCX](https://www.fusioncx.com/blog/locations/morocco-customer-service-outsourcing-a-strategic-advantage/)
- Preply/Leanlab (2026, n=113 native Spanish speakers aged 25-50 learning English for professional goals): the share reaching their goals rose from 27% with an app to 56% with a tutor. Sequential design, exploratory. — [Preply](https://preply.com/en/learning-research/1-on-1-tutoring-vs-self-learning-2026)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** The level required is B2+ for remote knowledge work and B1-C1 for BPO, depending on role. Learners buy 2-4 h/week of business and conversation English plus interview prep, in evening slots. Subscriptions run 6-18 months. When there is a near-term job-interview trigger, buyers are less price-sensitive.
- Strongest pairs: English × Mexico/Colombia/Brazil/Argentina/Peru/Chile/Egypt/Morocco/India/Turkey; French × Morocco/Tunisia/Egypt for BPO roles.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 survey of LatAm, Moroccan or Egyptian workers on spending for English to get remote jobs. LinkedIn, Upwork and Remote.com reports with English-level data were not found within the call budget.

## (c) Exam and citizenship/visa requirements

### Takeaway
Hard rules create deadline-driven buyers with the clearest willingness to pay. Canada's French-category Express Entry draws (NCLC 7 via TEF/TCF) and Italy's new B1 requirement for citizenship by marriage or residence are the two strongest recent triggers for Alimun's markets.

### Cited Findings
- Canada held seven French-language Express Entry draws in 2025 up to late October, including 7,500 ITAs on 21 Mar (CRS 379) and 6,000 on 29 Oct (CRS 416). Eligibility requires NCLC 7 in all four skills (TEF Canada or TCF Canada). French NCLC 7+ also earns 25-50 extra CRS points. The 2025 francophone target is 8.5% of permanent residents outside Quebec. — [immigration.ca 2025 review](https://immigration.ca/express-entry-in-2025-a-comprehensive-review/); [immigration.ca 6,000 draw](https://immigration.ca/major-canada-express-entry-draw-issues-6000-invites-to-french-speakers/); [Moving2Canada](https://moving2canada.com/news-and-features/news/immigration/express-entry-draw-365-french-draws-sept-4/)
- Italy, Law 74/2025: B1 Italian is required for citizenship by marriage and by residence, not by descent. The law is being challenged at the EU Court of Justice. A MAIE proposal would extend B1 to great-grandchildren (proposal only). — [Perfil](https://www.perfil.com/noticias/sociedad/ciudadania-italianapara-que-vias-de-tramitacion-es-obligatorio-rendir-el-examen-b1.phtml); [El Marplatense](https://www.elmarplatense.com/ciudadania-italiana-por-casamiento-ahora-el-idioma-es-un-requisito); [Italianismo](https://www.italianismo.com.br/es/falar-o-idioma-passa-a-ser-requisito-em-processos-de-naturalizacao-italiana/)
- Brazil: free Italian classes in Cocal do Sul (SC) are full, with a waiting list (single news item). — [Italianismo](https://italianismo.com.br/tag/lingua-italiana)
- Goethe-Institut: 1.1 million German exams taken in 2025. — [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/de/uun/prs/pma/26859595.html)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** This segment shows the lowest price sensitivity per hour but is short-lived: 2-6 months of intensive exam-format prep, private or small-group, with mock tests. Natural upsells: an exam bootcamp, then a maintenance subscription.
- Strongest pairs: French (TEF/TCF) × Morocco/Tunisia/Egypt/Latin America/India/Turkey toward Canada; Italian (CILS/CELI/PLIDA B1) × Argentina/Brazil (spouses, residents); German (Goethe/telc B1/B2) × all German-migration markets; English (IELTS/CELPIP) × India/Brazil/Mexico/Colombia toward Canada.

### Gaps
- No data on TEF/TCF or CILS test-taker volumes by country, or on how much candidates spend on prep. Not found within the call budget.

## (d) Hobby, travel, personal enrichment and retirees

### Takeaway
This is the largest motive among rich-country app users: travel, culture and brain health. Live-class demand exists but retains only through people and community. The segment is older, has more time, is price-aware, and leans Italian, Spanish and French.

### Cited Findings
- US adults: culture 31%, brain sharpness 27%, travel 27%. Only 8% of baby boomers actively learn a language, but 40% are interested (Nov 2024, n=2,000). — [Duolingo/Talker](https://blog.duolingo.com/never-too-late-to-learn)
- Among 2,000 Americans retired or retiring by 2029, 16% are learning a new language (Talker Research; fielding year unclear). — [Talker Research](https://talkerresearch.com/why-boomers-enjoy-learning-new-skills/)
- EF (Apr 2026, promotional): demand for language-travel programmes among adults aged 50+ rose about 50% over two years. — [EF press](https://www.ef.com/ca/about-us/press/articles/2026/scientists-confirm-power-of-language-learning-later-in-life/)
- Older learners (Poland, **2020**): their motives are travel, including visits to family abroad, socialising, new cultures and staying cognitively active. The instructor and fellow learners strongly shape their motivation. — [CEEOL](https://www.ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=1083851)
- Small qualitative study (**2022**, n=9 seniors learning Spanish): the main driver is the desire to travel. — [Universitat de Barcelona](https://diposit.ub.edu/dspace/handle/2445/200267?mode=full)
- Adult Australians learning Italian in continuing education: their motives are mainly cultural (older thesis). — [University of Sydney](https://ses.library.usyd.edu.au/handle/2123/14644?show=full)
- Babbel (**2016**): travel 53-56%, mental fitness 35%. — [Babbel](https://www.babbel.com/en/magazine/why-are-people-really-learning-languages-babbel-user-survey)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** What they buy: 1-2 h/week of small-group conversation, daytime slots for retirees, plus culture content (cinema, food, travel themes) and conversation clubs. They are more price-sensitive than goal-driven segments, but they stay longer if the group bonds. Churn spikes after a trip, and in summer and around holidays.
- Strongest pairs: Italian, Spanish and French × EU/Canada (Anglophone)/Balkans; Spanish × Brazil; English for travel × Latin America and Turkey.

### Gaps
- No data on what hobby learners spend on live classes versus apps, or on their tenure. Churn data found is app-only (e.g. app churn studies: [ICCE](https://library.apsce.net/index.php/ICCE/article/view/4148); [Cambridge Core, n=3,670 adult app users](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/85DDA2047A7A04BA7FE80541433595C6)). No comparison of live classes and apps was found.

## (e) Partner, family and heritage learners

### Takeaway
The evidence is qualitative only. Identity and talking with family are the drivers. The commercially sharp sub-segment is spouses who need B1 Italian for citizenship by marriage (see c) and spouses of migrants joining partners in Germany or Canada.

### Cited Findings
- In 175 Spanish-speaking mothers in Italy (2025), valuing Spanish as a heritage language is linked to communicative, cultural and professional reasons, and more-educated mothers value it more. — [Languages 9(4):140 via CiteDrive](https://www.citedrive.com/en/discovery/dime-con-qui%C3%A9n-andas-y-te-dir%C3%A9-qu%C3%A9-piensas-sobre-el-espa%C3%B1ol-de-los-us-language-attitudes-and-motivation-to-learn-spanish-as-a-heritage-language-through-the-lens-of-social-networks) (title as listed by the search engine; verify)
- Italian-Australians (**2017**): dialect is the family language, tied to identity and older relatives. — [pressto.amu.edu.pl](https://pressto.amu.edu.pl/index.php/stap/article/download/stap-2017-0021/28925/71427)
- Heritage and non-heritage learners differ mainly on the ethnic-heritage motivation dimension (Venice thesis, n=62). — [unitesi.unive.it](https://unitesi.unive.it/handle/20.500.14247/14703)
- Italian B1 for citizenship by marriage. — [El Marplatense](https://www.elmarplatense.com/ciudadania-italiana-por-casamiento-ahora-el-idioma-es-un-requisito)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** These learners buy private or very small groups and flexible schedules, and they are willing to pay for personalisation. They stay long when the goal is identity, and short when it is a document. Strongest pairs: Italian × Argentina/Brazil; Spanish × diaspora children in the EU and Canada; German or French for spouses of migrants from Morocco, Turkey and the Balkans.

### Gaps
- No market-size or spending data for adult heritage learners.

## (f) Students going abroad

### Takeaway
Flows are covered in earlier research. Here: education is the top Duolingo motive for English globally, and German demand is partly study-driven.

### Cited Findings
- Education is the top English-learning motive in almost all countries. — [Duolingo 2024](https://blog.duolingo.com/2024-duolingo-language-report/)
- German demand rises where Germany is a study destination (Egypt, India, Colombia named). — [Goethe-Institut 2025](https://www.goethe.de/de/uun/prs/pma/26859595.html)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** Students buy test prep (IELTS/TOEFL/DET, TestDaF/Goethe C1, DELF/DALF) in 2-6 month bursts. They are price-sensitive (often parent-funded) and seasonal around admissions deadlines.

### Gaps
- No student-specific spending or tenure data.

## (g) Companies paying for staff (B2B), and recruiters/agencies paying for candidates

### Takeaway
B2B is the largest-ticket, least price-sensitive buyer, and workers say they would use employer-funded learning. Market-size estimates vary widely. For German, recruiters and employers sometimes pay for candidates, but public funding in Germany competes once candidates arrive.

### Cited Findings
- 93% of workers would use employer-provided language learning (Preply 2026, UK/US/Canada). — [Workplace Journal](https://workplacejournal.co.uk/2026/07/nine-in-10-workers-would-use-employer-funded-language-learning-study-finds/)
- Corporate language training market estimates: $15.2bn in 2025, 6.8% CAGR, online 52.3% share (Market Intelo). Business English training $19.52bn in 2024, 8.8% CAGR (DataHorizzon). +$5.4bn growth from 2024-2029, APAC 45% of growth (Technavio). These are paid-report summaries that conflict with each other. — [Market Intelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/corporate-language-training-market/amp); [DataHorizzon](https://datahorizzonresearch.com/business-english-language-training-market-49698); [Technavio](https://technavio.com/report/business-english-language-training-market-industry-analysis)
- 57% of employers in non-English-speaking countries pay more for good English (**2016**). — [PIE News](https://thepienews.com/english-skills-provoke-workplace-premiums/)
- German employers are responsible for recruiting and training foreign care staff. — [Braunschweig](https://ratsinfo.braunschweig.de/integration/wicket/resource/org.apache.wicket.Application/doc1735366.pdf)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** B2B buys cohorts (groups of 4-8), business English or job-specific German (care, hospitality), progress reporting, and 3-12 month contracts. It has the highest lifetime value and lowest churn while the budget lasts. Recruiters (nursing, Ausbildung) buy A1-B2 bundles per candidate with pass-rate guarantees. BPOs in Morocco and Egypt are a plausible English/French B2B channel.

### Gaps
- No published data on per-employee spend or contract length for SMEs in Alimun's countries, or on recruiter spend per candidate.

## Which segments show lowest price sensitivity and highest lifetime value?

### Takeaway
No source measures this directly. Based on deadline triggers and payer type, the ranking is an inference.

### Cited Findings
- Hard requirements create deadlines: NCLC 7 for French draws, Italian B1 for marriage and residence. — [immigration.ca](https://immigration.ca/express-entry-in-2025-a-comprehensive-review/); [Perfil](https://www.perfil.com/noticias/sociedad/ciudadania-italianapara-que-vias-de-tramitacion-es-obligatorio-rendir-el-examen-b1.phtml)
- Employers pay premiums for English and say there is a skills gap (2016). — [Cambridge](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/images/335794-english-at-work-executive-summary.pdf)
- Human interaction is valued: 97% say practising with a real person matters, and 72% say apps alone are not enough. — [Workplace Journal](https://workplacejournal.co.uk/2026/07/nine-in-10-workers-would-use-employer-funded-language-learning-study-finds/)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** Lowest price sensitivity: (1) B2B and recruiter payers, (2) exam and citizenship candidates close to a deadline, (3) work migrants with a job offer. Highest lifetime value: work migrants on a multi-level German path (A1 to B2, 9-18 months), B2B contracts, and bonded hobby or retiree groups (long tenure at a lower price). Lowest lifetime value: single-exam candidates and students, who are intense but short.
- Decision triggers: job offer or recruiter contract, visa or embassy appointment, exam date, Express Entry category draws, Italian citizenship filing, an upcoming trip, retirement.

### Gaps
- No public churn, tenure or willingness-to-pay data from live-class providers (Lingoda, Preply, Berlitz, EF) by segment. Company disclosures were not found within the call budget.
