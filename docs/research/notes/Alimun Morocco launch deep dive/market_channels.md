# Morocco go-to-market fact base: segments, competitors, channels, messaging, partnerships (2025-2026)

Researcher notes, 11 Nov 2026. About 25 tool calls. FX: 1 EUR = 11 MAD. "Est." marks my own estimates. Many pages returned 403/404 (Goethe course pages) or carried no Morocco-specific numbers. Gaps are listed explicitly and not filled in.

## Segments and their deadlines (Ausbildung/nursing, university, IELTS, professionals/call centres, school-age)

### Takeaway
Migration to Germany is the most clearly sized and fastest-growing paid segment. Recognition applications from Morocco rose 45% in a year, and a nurse can go from 500-600 EUR a month at home to 1,524-1,687 EUR a month in training and about 3,550 EUR a month once qualified. The language threshold is a B1 certificate for Ausbildung and B2 for nursing and university, so learners study against a fixed exam deadline. English demand is driven by the offshoring sector (about 148,500 jobs, with a target of 270,000 by 2030) and by IELTS takers, who pay about 2,850 MAD per test.

### Cited Findings
- **Germany migration pull.** About 2,500 recognition applications for Moroccan qualifications were filed in Germany in 2025, up from about 1,700 a year earlier (+45%). Morocco is now among the top ten countries of training for applicants. Nearly half of the Moroccan applications are for unregulated trades, such as automation electronics and automotive mechatronics. — [Bladi.net, 26 Sep 2026, citing BIBB](https://www.bladi.net/allemagne-tourne-travailleurs-qualifies-formes-maroc,123995.html)
- Under the THAMM Plus programme, BIBB and GIZ prepare candidates before departure, including on language requirements. A new BIBB/THAMM Plus agreement runs from Dec 2025 to Mar 2027. In 2024, 60 ANAPEC staff were trained on German skilled-immigration rules. Germany's Chancenkarte (opportunity card) is open to Moroccans. — [Bladi.net](https://www.bladi.net/allemagne-tourne-travailleurs-qualifies-formes-maroc,123995.html)
- **Nursing economics.** A nurse in Morocco earns about 500-600 EUR a month (5,500-6,600 MAD). Moroccan trainees in Caritas care homes earn 1,524-1,687 EUR a month during training and about 3,550 EUR a month in their first year after qualifying. Caritas Rhein-Sieg is on its third Moroccan cohort, with 18 Moroccans at two homes, and works with German-language schools in Morocco. The article does not say who pays for the language course. In 2024, 13,000 health professionals arrived in Germany under the skilled-immigration law (all countries). — [Hespress FR, 10 Aug 2025](https://fr.hespress.com/436975-sante-et-soins-les-marocains-en-renfort-dans-les-maisons-de-retraite-allemandes.html)
- Morocco has about 32,000 nurses in post and reports a shortage at home. — [CCME](https://www.ccme.org.ma/professionnels-de-la-sante-au-maroc-emigrer-en-attendant-une-amelioration-des-conditions-de-travail)
- **Language thresholds.** Ausbildung needs at least B1, and university study at least B2, usually C1. — [Diakonie info brochure for Morocco, Sept 2025](https://www.diakonie.de/diakonie_de/user_upload/diakonie.de/PDFs/Publikationen/250916_diakonie_info_MAR_GER_digital_RZ.pdf)
- The visa requires a B1 certificate from Goethe, telc, ÖSD or TestDaF, and employers and chambers (IHK/HWK) often expect B2. — [GoAusbildung guide 2025, a commercial source](https://goausbildung.com/blog/ausbildung-in-germany-for-moroccan-students)
- A private recruiter (Nocare) placing nurses in Germany requires B2 German. — [bghit-nekhdem.com](https://bghit-nekhdem.com/opportunite-de-carriere-devenez-infirmier-en-allemagne-grace-a-nocare/)
- **Study time.** Goethe-Institut Marokko advertises "B1 in 9 months, B2 in 13 months" in one intensive format (brochure for 2024-25). — [Goethe kursflyer Studium](https://goethe.de/resources/files/pdf329/kursflyer__studium_final.pdf); [Goethe kursflyer Arbeit](https://goethe.de/resources/files/pdf329/kursflyer_arbeit_final.pdf)
- FIA Academy (Casablanca, health-sector German) ran 8-week levels A1 to B1 and a 12-week B2 level. Its flyers date from 2019-2021, so this is **older data**. — [FIA Marokko flyer](https://www.fia-academy.de/sites/default/files/2020-03/fia-marokko_flyer_200305_fr_web.pdf)
- **University route.** A Freshman Program (FH Aachen, a Studienkolleg-style year) runs in Rabat, Casablanca, Tangier and Fès. — [FH Aachen](https://www.fh-aachen.de/en/freshman/freshman-program-in-morocco)
- **IELTS.** The official fee is 2,850 MAD (about 259 EUR) for Academic or General Training at the British Council Tangier and Oujda centres. Third-party listings give 2,400-2,500 MAD for Marrakesh and Casablanca, which may be out of date. — [IELTS.org Tangier](https://ielts.org/test-centres/british-council-agharass-campus-tanger); [bestmytest Casablanca](https://www.bestmytest.com/ielts/test-centre/morocco/casablanca)
- **Call centres and offshoring.** The sector had about 148,500 stable jobs at end-2024 and 26.22 bn MAD of exports, with a target of 270,000 jobs by 2030. Offshoring incentives run to 31 Dec 2030, with 130,000 extra jobs planned, 50,000 of them by 2026. France's ban on cold-calling without consent from 2026 is pushing the sector to diversify. — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/services-externalises-maroc); [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/442604-442604.html)
- Postings for English-speaking roles are common. NTT Data is hiring for 210 positions, 50 of them English-speaking admin agents in Tétouan (Nov 2025). Foundever is hiring bilingual EN/FR agents for a British financial project in Casablanca, and Rabat call centres are hiring night-shift English advisors and a German/French/English trilingual agent. Entry-level advisor pay is 4,000-6,500 MAD a month. — [Dreamjob.ma NTT Data](https://www.dreamjob.ma/emploi/recrutement-massif-ntt-data-ouvre-210-emplois/); [Rekrute Foundever](https://www.rekrute.com/offre-emploi-chargeses-de-clientele-bilingues-anglais-francais-pour-un-projet-financier-british-recrutement-foundever-ex-sitel-group-casablanca-173553.html); [Rekrute Rabat night shift](https://www.rekrute.com/offre-emploi-english-speaking-customer-advisor-on-night-shift-recrutement-marketing-call-center-rabat-169897.html); [Jobsquare 2026](https://www.jobsquare.ma/blog/emploi-centre-appel-maroc-2026/)
- **School-age.** AMIDEAST runs a 10-week intensive English programme for 2nd-year Baccalaureate students preparing the national English exam. No price is shown. — [AMIDEAST online](https://amideastonline.org/course/view.php?id=535)

### Inferences
- Est.: a candidate going from A1 to a B1 certificate needs about 6-9 months of intensive study, and about 12-13 months to B2, based on Goethe's own claims. That means a 12-week level pack maps to roughly one CEFR level, and a full path to B1 means buying 3-4 packs.
- Nurses earn 5,500-6,600 MAD a month and the German payoff is 3-6x, so spending 600-830 MAD a month on courses (about 10-15% of income) is plausible for motivated candidates. Est. based on the Hespress figures.
- Call-centre agents earn 4,000-6,500 MAD a month, so they are more price-sensitive. The app (59 MAD) and open class (219-269 MAD) suit them better than exam packs. Employers are the better buyers for this segment (see Partnerships).
- The Germany pipeline runs on Moroccan trades training (OFPPT-type diplomas), so vocational schools are a natural acquisition point.

### Gaps
- No reliable count of Moroccans learning German, or of German visas issued to Moroccans in 2025-2026. Check Auswärtiges Amt or Destatis. The 23,000 work-permit figure in the brief comes from earlier research and was not re-verified here.
- Who pays: no source quantified the split between self, family, agency and employer funding. Anecdotally, employer programmes such as Caritas and Triple Win may cover courses, but this is unconfirmed for Morocco.
- No Moroccan IELTS test-volume data, and no numbers on Moroccan students heading to Germany or Italy in 2025-26.
- School-age market size was not researched (out of scope).

## Competitors 2026 (Goethe, private German schools, agencies, online/influencer teachers, English centres)

### Takeaway
Goethe-Institut Marokko is the price and quality anchor. It teaches only in Rabat and Casablanca, in groups of 12-18, at 3,000 MAD for 60 teaching units (24/25 data). Its exams cost 2,000-2,500 MAD for B1 and 2,200-2,800 MAD for B2 (2026-27). Goethe itself runs a fully funded A2 programme in Rabat, so "free" competitors include Goethe as well as Devoxa. I could not find prices for private schools outside Goethe on the web, which suggests they sell through Facebook and WhatsApp rather than published price lists.

### Cited Findings
- **Goethe course terms.** Groups are capped at 18, and a course with fewer than 12 participants may be cancelled. Registration is online within published windows, and "registration is not yet enrolment". Learners with prior German take a placement test. Family members get 25% off from the second person. — [Goethe AGB (course terms)](https://goethe.de/resources/files/pdf88/AGBDeutsch.pdf); [Goethe terms July 2024](https://goethe.de/resources/files/pdf330/geschaeftsbedingungen-kurse-juli-2024.pdf)
- Goethe teaches in Rabat and Casablanca only, in person, online or blended. — [Goethe Marokko, Deutsche Sprache](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ma/de/spr.html)
- Price: 3,000 MAD for 60 teaching units of 45 minutes, about 50 MAD per unit or 273 EUR per 60 units, in the "work" and "study" course flyers (Aug 2024 to Jul 2025). **Older; 2025-26 prices not retrieved** because the course pages returned 403. — [Goethe kursflyer Arbeit](https://goethe.de/resources/files/pdf329/kursflyer_arbeit_final.pdf)
- **Goethe exam fees 2026-2027 (MAD, internal / external):** A1 1,700 / 1,950; A2 1,800 / 2,000; B1 2,000 / 2,500 (module 1,000); B2 2,200 / 2,800 (module 1,000); C1 2,500 / 3,200; C2 2,600 / 3,300. "Internal" presumably means Goethe course participants; this is my reading, as the PDF does not define it. In EUR, B1 costs about 182-227 and B2 about 200-255. — [Goethe Marokko exam calendar 2026/2027, dated 05.2026](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf359/pruefungskalender-2026-2727.pdf)
- **Goethe exam calendar 2026-27.** B1 and B2 exams run several times a month, roughly from Sept 2026 to Jul 2027. Online registration opens about 1-2 months before, for example B1 registration on 13/07/2026 for exams on 21-23/09/2026, and 07/09/2026 for 22-23/10/2026. A1 runs roughly monthly. "Additional dates will be scheduled where possible." — [same PDF](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf359/pruefungskalender-2026-2727.pdf)
- **Goethe's free competitor programme.** The "Vorbereitet und erfolgreich nach Deutschland" programme offers fully funded A2 German courses in person at Goethe Rabat, January to March 2026, for people with A1 and work experience who cannot pay course fees. Applications closed 24 Oct 2025 and interviews were held in early November. Participants pay materials and exam fees, and no job contracts are offered. — [Goethe tender, Sept 2025](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf354/ausschreibung-sprachkurse-jan-2026.pdf)
- **Private schools.** A Studienkolleg guide estimates about 2,800-3,500 MAD per level (A1 to B2) for a standard 8-10 week group course. This is a third-party estimate. A private teacher in Casablanca advertises 200 MAD per 2-hour session (undated). — [studienkolleg.org guide](https://www.studienkolleg.org/fr/blog/studienkolleg-guide-etudiants-maroc/)
- FIA Academy Marokko (Casablanca) offers German for health professionals from A1 to B2, plus a 50 EUR credit for follow-on courses in Germany. **Flyers are from 2019-2021, so the offer may be outdated.** — [FIA flyer](https://www.fia-academy.de/sites/default/files/2020-03/fia-marokko_flyer_200305_fr_web.pdf)
- Awal Institute (Marrakech) runs German courses at several levels. The only source is a volunteer listing from 2023 (**older**). — [voluntouring.org](https://voluntouring.org/2023/09/21/volunteer-as-a-german-tutor-awal-institute-marrakesh-marocco/amp)
- A Diakonie brochure for Moroccan candidates advises checking teacher qualifications and that the school meets legal requirements. That makes teacher credentials and legitimacy a trust signal. — [Diakonie brochure](https://www.diakonie.de/diakonie_de/user_upload/diakonie.de/PDFs/Publikationen/250916_diakonie_info_MAR_FRA_digital_RZ.pdf)
- **English centres.** Wall Street English has a centre in Casablanca, but no Morocco prices are published. EDC's language centre offers Cambridge (FCE, CAE) and IELTS prep without published prices. Al Akhawayn University runs summer Business English for executives. — [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=460169); [EDC](https://www.edc.ma/?p=1594); [AUI](https://aui.ma/hubfs/Business%20English%20for%20Executives.pdf?hsLang=en)

### Inferences
- Goethe charges about 50 MAD per 45-minute unit, plus 2,000-2,800 MAD to sit the exam. Alimun's level pack at 600-830 MAD a month is competitive per month. The hidden extra in every path is the exam fee, so Alimun's exam-pack messaging should state that the exam fee is paid separately to Goethe or telc.
- Goethe's 12-18 seat groups, Rabat and Casablanca only, and in-person funded programme leave learners in Tangier, Fès, Marrakech, Agadir and smaller cities underserved. Online rooms of 8-12 are a direct answer to that gap (est.).
- Goethe's B1 sessions in 2026-27 fall almost monthly, so Alimun can anchor cohorts to a specific Goethe exam date, for example "finish B1 in time for the March 2027 session".

### Gaps
- No verified 2025-26 Goethe course prices, timetables or waiting lists (pages returned 403). No source confirms waiting lists.
- No named private German schools with prices in Casablanca, Rabat, Tangier, Fès, Marrakech or Agadir surfaced. Searches returned nothing usable. Next step: Facebook and Google Maps searches for "école allemand" plus the city name.
- Recruitment agencies' fees and course bundles: nothing reliable found. GerMarok was mentioned but unverified. Fees charged to candidates, and any fraud warnings, remain open.
- Moroccan YouTube/TikTok German or English teachers with paid courses: not found in this pass.
- British Council, AMIDEAST, ALC and Wall Street English 2025-26 online course prices were not found. The British Council and IELTS prep hourly rates in the brief come from earlier research and were not re-verified.
- Devoxa was not re-checked.

## Channels (platforms, Facebook groups, ad costs, influencers, SEO, WhatsApp)

### Takeaway
Facebook (22.8M ad reach), YouTube (21.6M), TikTok (16.7M adults, +19% a year) and Instagram (15.1M, +21% a year) all reach most Moroccan adults. TikTok and Instagram are growing fastest, and TikTok is now the top influencer platform. I found no Morocco-specific CPM or CPC benchmarks. Proxies suggest North African CPMs are well below global averages, and Ramadan raises them.

- **Ad costs (proxies only).** The global Meta CPM averaged 14.19 USD in 2025 (Triple Whale, about 35k accounts), with no Morocco breakdown. — [influee.co summary](https://influee.co/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks)
- MENA all-platform CPM blog figures: UAE 4-8 USD, KSA 3-6 USD, Egypt 2-4 USD. Morocco is not covered. Ramadan raises CPMs by 30-50%. — [adigitalboom.com](https://adigitalboom.com/glossary/cpm)
- The global TikTok CPM was about 6.16 USD in June 2025, up from 2.87 USD in January 2025. — [Gupta Media](https://guptamedia.com/insights/tiktok-ads-cost)

### Inferences
- Est.: Morocco CPMs are probably closer to Egypt's (2-4 USD, about 20-45 MAD) than to the Gulf. **This is unverified.** Run a small test in Ads Manager to get real numbers, as the Morocco market guide also advises. — [businessadsguide Morocco](https://businessadsguide.pages.dev/markets/morocco)
- The audience is male-skewed on Facebook and TikTok, while Instagram is more balanced. The nursing segment is likely female-heavy, so Instagram and Facebook should be favoured for nursing creatives (est.).
- Est.: micro creators (10k-100k) in Morocco likely cost less than the global 200-1,000 USD range. Learning and "Germany life" creators are a natural fit. Paying per lead or per sign-up may suit a market with no Creator Fund.

## Messaging and language (French/Darija/Arabic, trust, price sensitivity, payment, seasonality, time to purchase)

### Takeaway
I found no direct study on whether ads in French or Darija perform better. The literature says Moroccan advertising historically used French and Modern Standard Arabic as markers of status, and that Darija is rising in digital and influencer marketing. Seasonality is driven by the Goethe exam calendar, with roughly monthly B1 and B2 sessions from September to July, and by Ramadan, when ad costs rise.

### Cited Findings
- Moroccan advertising has historically used French and MSA, reflecting class hierarchy and formal authority. Darija is now prominent in influencer and digital marketing. — [Darija digital advertising article, artboulevard](https://artboulevard.org/items/1420925/); [AMU Poznań study of 34 ads on 2M](https://pressto.amu.edu.pl/index.php/linpo/article/view/52263)
- Vendor claims, low reliability: premium brands use French to signal sophistication, while mass-market brands favour Arabic. — [media.co.uk blog](https://media.co.uk/blogs/blog/facade-zerktouni-audience-demographics-and-advertising-insights-for-moroccan-market-success)
- A sample of Telegram ads ran about 50% in French, 45% in Arabic and 5% in Darija. This is one channel, not a consumer study. — [telegramadsarchive](https://telegramadsarchive.com/blog/morocco-telegram-ads-crypto-gambling-2026)
- Trust signals: candidates are told to check teacher qualifications and the school's legal compliance. Visa offices accept only certificates from Goethe, telc, ÖSD or TestDaF. — [Diakonie brochure](https://www.diakonie.de/diakonie_de/user_upload/diakonie.de/PDFs/Publikationen/250916_diakonie_info_MAR_FRA_digital_RZ.pdf); [GoAusbildung](https://goausbildung.com/blog/ausbildung-in-germany-for-moroccan-students)
- Price levers: Goethe offers a 25% family discount from the second family member. — [Goethe AGB](https://goethe.de/resources/files/pdf88/AGBDeutsch.pdf)
- The Goethe funded programme targets people who cannot afford course fees. — [Goethe tender](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf354/ausschreibung-sprachkurse-jan-2026.pdf)
- Seasonality: Goethe B1 and B2 exam sessions run from September to July, with registration 1-2 months before each session ([Goethe calendar 2026-27](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf359/pruefungskalender-2026-2727.pdf)). Ramadan raises CPMs by 30-50% ([adigitalboom](https://adigitalboom.com/glossary/cpm)). Goethe's funded programme follows an October application, November interview and January start cycle ([Goethe tender](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf354/ausschreibung-sprachkurse-jan-2026.pdf)).

### Inferences
- Est.: use French for the German, Ausbildung and IELTS landing pages and SEO, since the professional and visa vocabulary is French and German. Use Darija for TikTok and Instagram video hooks and WhatsApp conversations. Use MSA for formal trust content. This is consistent with the sources but **not tested**.
- The strongest messages are the outcomes this segment cares about: being ready for a named Goethe or telc session, small rooms of 8-12 against Goethe's 18, online from any city, qualified teachers, and the honest note that "the exam fee is paid to Goethe/telc".
- Est.: September to October (start of the exam year, after summer) and January are natural launch windows. Avoid heavy ad spend during Ramadan.

### Gaps
- No data on preference for monthly vs upfront payment, cash use, card penetration or CashPlus/Wafacash use for online courses.
- No data on time from first contact to purchase, refund-guarantee norms or the impact of published exam results.
- Summer and Ramadan effects on enrolment (as opposed to ad cost) are not quantified.

## Partnerships (agencies, universities and vocational schools, call centres, employers; B2B deal sizes)

### Takeaway
The visible partnership levers are German employers and welfare organisations that already recruit in Morocco and work with local language schools (Caritas), state programmes (THAMM Plus with GIZ, BIBB and ANAPEC), and offshoring employers hiring English speakers at scale (NTT Data, Foundever). I found no B2B deal sizes.

### Cited Findings
- Caritas Rhein-Sieg recruits Moroccan care trainees (third cohort, selection interviews in Morocco since early 2023) and works with German-language schools in Morocco. — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/436975-sante-et-soins-les-marocains-en-renfort-dans-les-maisons-de-retraite-allemandes.html)
- THAMM Plus (GIZ and BIBB) prepares candidates, and ANAPEC staff were trained in 2024. Germany reportedly planned migration centres in nine countries, including Morocco. — [Bladi.net](https://www.bladi.net/allemagne-tourne-travailleurs-qualifies-formes-maroc,123995.html)
- Make it in Germany lists official counselling contacts in Morocco and the "Faire Anwerbung Pflege Deutschland" (fair recruitment in nursing) framework. — [Make it in Germany: Morocco](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/fr/aide-conseil/monde-entier/maroc)
- The IMED-KOMM online course for foreign nurses is listed by Make it in Germany. Its price was not confirmed. — [Make it in Germany: nursing](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/fr/etudes-formations/formation-en-allemagne/metiers-tres-demandes/infirmier)
- Call-centre employers hiring English and trilingual staff include NTT Data (210 roles, Tétouan), Foundever (Casablanca) and several Rabat centres. Some of them also hire internal "bilingual trainers". — [Dreamjob.ma](https://www.dreamjob.ma/emploi/recrutement-massif-ntt-data-ouvre-210-emplois/); [Rekrute bilingual trainer](https://www.rekrute.com/offre-emploi-formateur-bilingue---freng-tr-bil-0425-recrutement-marketing-call-center-rabat-174119.html)
- The FH Aachen Freshman Program in Rabat, Casablanca, Tangier and Fès could be a university-route partner. — [FH Aachen](https://www.fh-aachen.de/en/freshman/freshman-program-in-morocco)

### Inferences
- The best first B2B targets are (est.) German care operators and recruiters that need a B1 or B2 pipeline outside Rabat and Casablanca, and call centres whose recruits fail English screening. Call centres already employ in-house trainers, so Alimun would be pitching as an outsourced or complementary option.
- Est.: a 12-week pack for a cohort of 10 at 600-830 MAD a month comes to about 18,000-25,000 MAD (1,600-2,300 EUR) per cohort. This is arithmetic from Alimun's own prices, not market data.

### Gaps
- No B2B contract sizes, no agency course-bundle prices, no named Moroccan recruitment agencies with verified fees, and no university or OFPPT partnership precedents found.
