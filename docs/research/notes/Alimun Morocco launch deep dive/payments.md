# Payment routes for Moroccan learners paying a foreign (Canadian) online education company, 2025-2026

Research date: 11 Nov 2026. About 24 tool calls. Not legal advice. Every claim carries its URL; anything without one is in Inferences or Gaps. Several primary pages (Médias24, TelQuel) returned HTTP 403, so some points rest on search snippets or secondary sources, and the notes say so.

## 1. The e-commerce allowance (dotation e-commerce): 2026 rules, cards, activation, declines, recurring and 3-D Secure

### Takeaway
Under the IGOC 2026 (in force 1 Jan 2026), resident individuals get 20,000 MAD per calendar year for online purchases from foreign suppliers, on a nominative "international" payment card that the issuing bank technically caps at the allowance. Online training and SaaS subscriptions are explicitly eligible (per a consultancy summary of Art. 120). The main practical route in 2026 is the banks' app-generated virtual cards (Attijariwafa, BMCE/Bank of Africa, CIH, SG, Banque Populaire), which support 3-D Secure and can be set up to be reusable for monthly subscriptions. Alimun's yearly spend per learner (about 7,200-10,000 MAD for classes) fits under the cap, but it uses up half or more of a learner's yearly allowance.

### Cited Findings
- IGOC 2026 is in force from 1 January 2026; the individual e-commerce allowance rises to 20,000 MAD a year (from 15,000) and includes MRE. Allowances for labelled start-ups go to 2 MDH. — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/reglementation-des-changes-dotation-pour-voyages-e-commerce-export-import-ce-qui-change-des-janvier_BPDQLTUSQFE5PNTNKVZO3UMNA4/); [Office des Changes, publication of IGOC 2026](https://www.oc.gov.ma/fr/actualites/publication-de-l-instruction-generale-des-operations-de-change-2026-0); [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/456745-456745.html); [Ecoactu](https://ecoactu.ma/igoc-dotations-ecommerce-plafonds-instruments-2026/)
- In the same reform the extra personal travel allowance went from 200,000 to 400,000 MAD (on top of the 100,000 MAD base, max 500,000 MAD a year), and the monthly allowance for study-abroad living costs went from 12,000 to 15,000 MAD. The service-import rules were "clarified and relaxed" (broader definition, the limited list of operations delegated to banks removed). — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/reglementation-des-changes-dotation-pour-voyages-e-commerce-export-import-ce-qui-change-des-janvier_BPDQLTUSQFE5PNTNKVZO3UMNA4/)
- Per a consultancy summary of the IGOC 2026 (Art. 120-121): the beneficiaries are resident individuals and MRE. Conditions: a valid CNIE (residence card for foreign residents) and an international payment card from a bank or licensed payment institution, used only for online purchases from foreign suppliers. The allowance renews each calendar year and does not carry over. — [Upsilon Consulting](https://upsilon-consulting.com/commerce-electronique-change-maroc/)
- The same source lists as eligible: digital services, licences, "subscriptions to SaaS platforms and professional tools" and "online training from foreign providers". Excluded: cash withdrawals abroad (travel allowance), transfers between individuals and crypto-assets. — [Upsilon Consulting](https://upsilon-consulting.com/commerce-electronique-change-maroc/)
- Same source (Art. 10, 19, 20): the card is nominative and technically blocks any payment above the allowance. One card can carry several allowances. The issuing bank reports transactions to the Office des Changes, and the cardholder keeps invoices and statements for audit. The e-commerce allowance is separate from the travel allowance. — [Upsilon Consulting](https://upsilon-consulting.com/commerce-electronique-change-maroc/). Note: this is a secondary source. I did not read the IGOC text itself; check Art. 120-131 on oc.gov.ma.
- Virtual cards from bank apps (page "updated 16 May 2026", but it mentions a 4 June 2026 launch):
  - Attijariwafa offers the Wafa Pay e-card and Simple Pay, up to 50,000 MAD, valid from 1 day to 1 year, free for its clients.
  - BMCE (Bank of Africa) offers the BMCE e-card, up to 30,000 MAD, valid for 1 day or 1 month.
  - CIH offers CIH Virtual, up to 40,000 MAD, valid from 24 h to 12 months, free. It has a "supplier card" option locked to one merchant with a monthly cap.
  - Société Générale offers SG e-shopping, up to 25,000 MAD, valid for only 1 or 7 days.
  - Banque Populaire launched La Virtuelle and L'Instant in Pocket Bank on 4 June 2026. L'Instant expires after 1 hour if unused.
  — [Wafir](https://wafir.ma/fr/guide/cartes-virtuelles-paiement-en-ligne-maroc-2026)
- Generating a virtual card takes about 30-60 s in the app, including 3-D Secure. For subscriptions the guide recommends a reusable virtual card with a fixed monthly cap. — [Wafir](https://wafir.ma/fr/guide/cartes-virtuelles-paiement-en-ligne-maroc-2026)
- Attijariwafa clients can activate or top up their international payment allowance remotely in the L'bankalik app. Attijariwafa also launched a "Carte Virtuelle" for online payments in Morocco and abroad. — [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/attijariwafa-bank-lance-une-carte-virtuelle-pour-securiser-les-e-paiements/345272); [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/attijariwafa-bank-lance-la-carte-virtuelle-pour-des-paiements-digitaux-securises-et-instantanes_FPA5DCOGXZAQLLEPSLB24WOTS4/) (activation detail from search snippet)
- 3-D Secure, by SMS code or in-app biometric approval, has been mandatory for CMI-approved Moroccan merchants since 2023. A transaction without the validation is refused. — [Wafir](https://wafir.ma/fr/guide/cartes-virtuelles-paiement-en-ligne-maroc-2026)
- Decline causes the guide names: no 3-D Secure validation; merchant anti-fraud rules rejecting virtual cards (it names "expensive training" among the affected categories); virtual cards cannot be used at terminals or ATMs. — [Wafir](https://wafir.ma/fr/guide/cartes-virtuelles-paiement-en-ligne-maroc-2026)
- Many Moroccan cards have international online spending limits. — [Kolonell (agency blog, 2026)](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/morocco-online-payment-gateway-comparison-2026)
- Apple Pay and Google Pay became available in Morocco in 2023, with CIH among the first issuers. — [Dodo Payments blog, May 2025 (promotional)](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/merchant-of-record-morocco); [Hespress on CIH Google Pay](https://fr.hespress.com/358379-358379.html)
- Older context: an undated dLocal page says only 4% of the population owns a card (Datareportal 2022) and gives payment shares of cash 60%, cards 25%, transfer 10%, wallets 5%. The figures are unsourced and the page has obvious errors. — [dLocal Morocco market page](http://www.dlocal.com/payment-processors-in-africa-and-middle-east/morocco-payment-methods-processors-ecommerce-market/). Flag: 2022 data, low reliability.

### Inferences
- Recurring charges: a merchant-initiated charge after the first 3DS-authenticated payment should work technically on a reusable card, since dLocal lists recurring as supported for Moroccan Visa and Mastercard. There are failure points, though. Single-use or short-validity virtual cards (SG 1-7 days, BP L'Instant 1 h, any card with a cap set to one charge) will fail at renewal. The annual 20,000 MAD cap may be reached mid-year by heavy online buyers. Some issuers may request 3DS on renewals (estimate, not sourced).
- Alimun should therefore show learners how to create a reusable virtual card with a monthly cap above the plan price. It should also send a pre-renewal reminder and a dunning email with a "pay with a new card" link.
- An upfront 12-week pack (1,670-2,800 MAD) avoids recurring-charge risk entirely and makes better use of a one-off card. That points to offering packs as the default for card payers.
- The 86% cash-withdrawal share (from the earlier research) and the low card penetration mean a large share of the audience cannot pay by card online at all. A cash or wallet route matters for reach, not just conversion.

### Gaps
- I did not read the IGOC 2026 article text directly. The eligibility of "online training" and the technical cap rest on Upsilon's summary.
- No official per-bank FAQ was fetched for Banque Populaire's physical cards, Crédit du Maroc, Al Barid Bank or CDM activation steps. Al Barid Bank and Crédit du Maroc virtual cards were not found.
- No published decline-rate data for Moroccan cards at foreign merchants was found.
- Whether a merchant-initiated renewal draws on the allowance cleanly or triggers issuer blocks was not documented anywhere.

## 2. Local acquiring for a foreign merchant: CMI, Moroccan PSPs, global PSPs

### Takeaway
I found no source confirming that a non-resident company can get a CMI, Payzone or YouCan Pay merchant account and settle abroad. The domestic model assumes a Moroccan entity and a MAD account. The realistic local-method route for a Canadian company is a cross-border PSP with in-country coverage: dLocal, which documents Moroccan Visa and Mastercard with recurring, plus Wafacash cash vouchers. The fallback is plain Stripe Canada, cross-border, which works for any card that has international e-commerce enabled.

### Cited Findings
- The Competition Council ended CMI's near-monopoly (over 97% share) in September 2024. The acquiring market has officially been open since 1 May 2025. — [TelQuel, Sept 2025 (via search snippet; page 403)](https://telquel.ma/2025/09/08/electronic-payments-will-the-end-of-the-cmi-monopoly-really-get-the-ball-rolling_1944604)
- Card acceptance contracts are no longer necessarily signed with CMI; several payment institutions and bank subsidiaries now offer acquiring. — [Wafir, CMI](https://wafir.ma/fr/institutions/cmi). Attijari Payment launched merchant acquiring. — [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/432204-attijari-payment-lance-officiellement-son-activite-dacquisition-commercants.html)
- CMI launched multi-currency (DCC) payment for Moroccan e-commerce sites with Global Blue. Foreign-card volume through CMI exceeded 26 bn MAD in 2024 (+36%). — [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/411997-le-paiement-multidevises-sur-les-sites-marocains-de-e-commerce-desormais-possible.html); [FNH](https://fnh.ma/articles/paiement-multi-devises-maroc-cmi)
- Payzone is CMI-approved (forum claim). An agency blog groups Payzone, Maroc Telecommerce (M2T) and YouCan Pay as Bank Al-Maghrib-approved aggregators with faster onboarding and slightly higher fees. It gives indicative commissions of 1.5-2.5% (Payzone-type) and 2-3% (another gateway), and says a foreign company implies foreign-currency flows to declare to the Office des Changes. — [Shopify community](https://community.shopify.com/t/utilisation-de-spotify-au-maroc/11644); [Kolonell 2026](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/paiement-en-ligne-pme-maroc-cmi-ou-stripe-2026). Low reliability: a vendor blog.
- Cross-border card processing works from day one but costs about 2.9-3.9% plus fixed fees (2026). The agency suggests local acquiring for Moroccan cards plus cross-border processing for the rest. — [Kolonell 2026](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/morocco-online-payment-gateway-comparison-2026)
- MAD is not freely convertible, so every cross-border flow runs under Office des Changes rules. — [WorldFirst](https://www.worldfirst.com/af/?p=49279)
- dLocal Morocco pay-ins (docs updated around March 2026):
  - Cards: Visa and Mastercard credit and debit, plus UnionPay, in MAD. Refunds, recurring, chargebacks and disputes are supported.
  - Cash: Wafacash vouchers (type TICKET), with a maximum expiry of 5 days, immediate notification and refunds.
  - A CNIE document number (5-20 characters) is required on payments.
  — [dLocal docs, Morocco](https://docs.dlocal.com/docs/morocco)
- dLocal's marketing page names CMI among accepted card brands, alongside Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay and JCB. — [dLocal Morocco market page](http://www.dlocal.com/payment-processors-in-africa-and-middle-east/morocco-payment-methods-processors-ecommerce-market/)
- dLocal is also reachable through the orchestrator Yuno, which lists Morocco connections. Smart Fit used dLocal+Yuno to accept local cards in Morocco. — [Yuno docs](https://docs.y.uno/docs/morocco-connections); [dLocal press release](https://www.dlocal.com/blog/press-releases/dlocal-and-yuno-expand-partnership-to-simplify-global-expansion-for-modern-enterprises-in-emerging-markets)
- Checkout.com, Paymob, Flutterwave: no source confirms Moroccan local acquiring. — [search result review; Checkout.com acquiring page](https://checkout.com/products/acquiring)
- Stripe does not support Moroccan merchant accounts. — [Dodo Payments blog, May 2025](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/merchant-of-record-morocco) (consistent with the earlier research)
- PayPal: Cash Plus and PayPal launched (Sept 2025) instant withdrawal of PayPal balances to a Cash Plus account in MAD. Cash Plus explains that a PayPal account counts as a foreign account, which Moroccans may not hold. — [Médias24 (snippet)](https://medias24.com/2025/09/16/cashplus-lance-via-son-application-le-retrait-des-soldes-paypal-en-mad/); [MEA Tech Watch](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/09/16/cash-plus-and-paypal-launch-moroccos-first-digital-paypal-withdrawal-service/); [Le Desk](https://ledesk.ma/encontinu/cashplus-et-paypal-digitalisent-les-retraits-et-achats-internationaux-au-maroc/). Le Desk's headline mentions "achats internationaux" (international purchases), but details could not be fetched (403). A third-party competitor puts PayPal fees at about 3-4% plus FX margin. — [Grey](https://grey.co/fr/blog/how-to-withdraw-paypal-funds-in-morocco)

### Inferences
- **dLocal** is the strongest single "local" route for a Canadian merchant: MAD pricing, local cards with recurring, and Wafacash cash in one integration. It is enterprise-oriented, though. Expect a sales process, minimum volumes and pricing on request (estimate, not sourced).
- **Stripe Canada** keeps working for learners who have a virtual or international card, with zero new integration. Fees would be the Canadian standard rate plus an international-card fee and currency conversion. The exact current numbers were not verified in this session.
- **CMI, Payzone, YouCan Pay and M2T** in practice need a Moroccan legal entity and a MAD bank account. If the owner, as a Moroccan citizen, set up a Moroccan SARL, these become options at about 1.5-3%. That brings Moroccan tax, company and repatriation obligations, which is a structural decision, not a payments one.
- **Merchant-of-record platforms** (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Dodo) handle VAT but still charge Moroccan cards cross-border, so they do not fix acceptance.

### Gaps
- No primary page confirmed CMI, Payzone or YouCan Pay terms for non-resident companies or foreign payouts. HPS (a processor and software vendor, not a merchant gateway) and Chari were not researched.
- dLocal pricing and minimums are not public.
- Stripe Canada's current international-card and FX fees were not fetched this session.
- PayPal: whether Moroccan users can now pay foreign merchants in foreign currency, and against which allowance, is unclear (the Le Desk article was not readable).

## 3. Cash and wallet options (Wafacash, Cash Plus, Barid Cash, Damane Cash; Orange Money, inwi money, etc.)

### Takeaway
Cash is the dominant payment habit. The only documented way for a foreign merchant to collect cash and settle abroad is dLocal's Wafacash voucher. No aggregator was found that lets a foreign merchant collect through Cash Plus, Barid Cash, Damane Cash or the mobile wallets and settle abroad.

### Cited Findings
- dLocal supports Wafacash cash vouchers in MAD (5-day expiry, immediate notification). Payer name, email and CNIE are required. Recurring is not listed. — [dLocal docs](https://docs.dlocal.com/docs/morocco)
- Cash Plus Mobile opens a payment account with a wallet and supports bill payment. Cash Plus has prepaid and virtual cards for e-commerce. — [FNH](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/cash-plus-possibilite-d-ouvrir-un-compte-de-paiement-via-agence-ou-mobile-gratuitement); [Wafir, Cash Plus](https://wafir.ma/fr/institutions/cash-plus); [Financial Afrik, Mar 2026](https://www.financialafrik.com/2026/03/19/maroc-cash-plus-franchit-les-13-milliards-de-dollars-et-accelere-sa-transformation-digitale/)
- Local wallets named: Jibi by Wafacash, Filahi Pay, MT Cash, CIH Mobile, Orange et moi, inwi Money. Wallet use for digital business is "still in its infancy". — [Dodo Payments blog, May 2025](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/merchant-of-record-morocco)
- Damane Cash, Yashir and CMI Net are cited as merchant payment alternatives. — [Wafir fintech ranking 2026](https://wafir.ma/fr/actu/fintech-marocaines-2026-cashplus-wafacash-payzone-yashir-classement)
- Wafacash offers bill payment at agencies. — [Wafacash](https://wafacash.ma/en/nos-solutions/je-veux-regler-mes-factures-mes-impots-mes-impayes)

### Inferences
- A Cash Plus prepaid or virtual card is a learner-side route. A learner without a bank card loads cash at an agency, then pays Stripe or dLocal by card, if that card is enabled for international e-commerce (to check).
- Wafacash vouchers suit upfront 12-week packs, not monthly subscriptions, because each payment is a one-off ticket.

### Gaps
- No source on Barid Cash or Al Barid Bank merchant collection for foreign companies, Orange Money or inwi money merchant APIs, or Cash Plus bill-pay onboarding for foreign billers.
- dLocal's Wafacash fees are not public.

## 4. App store billing in Morocco (Apple, Google Play, carrier billing)

### Takeaway
Both stores sell in Morocco and charge the user's card, so they inherit the same card limits. Apple now collects and remits Morocco's 20% VAT on digital services (from 27 Aug 2026). Google Play lists only cards for Morocco; inwi carrier billing was launched in 2019, but current availability is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Morocco applies 20% VAT to digital services from foreign providers with no local presence, operational from 11 June 2026. Apple adjusted Morocco proceeds for the 20% VAT from 27 Aug 2026 and states it collects and remits Morocco tax. Prices of apps and one-off in-app purchases changed on 14 Sept 2026 unless Morocco is the base storefront. Auto-renewable subscriptions keep their prices. — [Morocco World News, Aug 2026](https://moroccoworldnews.com/2026/08/336622/apple-applies-moroccos-20-vat-across-app-store-proceeds-and-prices); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/27/apple-announces-app-store-price-changes-in-four-countries/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/apple-developer-proceeds-drop-in-three-countries-as-morocco-adds-20-vat/)
- Local currency: a June 2024 Apple forum answer said the Moroccan storefront showed USD, not MAD. I found no 2026 confirmation of MAD pricing. — [Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/255651472) (older, may be outdated)
- Google Play payment methods for Morocco fall under "Other countries": American Express, Discover, Mastercard and Visa only. — [Google Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2651410?hl=en)
- inwi launched Google Play carrier billing (charge to prepaid balance) in 2019, the first in Morocco. — [Le360, 2019](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/une-premiere-au-maroc-google-play-store-des-centaines-dapplications-et-jeux-accessibles-chez-inwi-196624/). Flag: 2019; it is not on Google's current list, so status is unknown.

### Inferences
- In-app purchases through RevenueCat do not remove the card problem. They do take over VAT and renewal retries. Store fees are typically 15% for small developers (standard program terms, not verified here). For 59 MAD a month, the store route is still the lowest-effort route for the app subscription.
- The 20% Moroccan VAT also applies to Alimun's direct (Stripe) sales of digital services to Moroccan consumers, which may require registration or remittance. This needs a tax check: whether live teacher-led classes count as "digital services" is an open question.

### Gaps
- Current carrier billing operators for Google Play in Morocco (Maroc Telecom, Orange) are not confirmed by any source found.
- The exact MAD price points on Apple and Google Play were not found.
- Morocco's VAT registration mechanics for foreign digital-service sellers that do not use a marketplace were not researched.

## 5. Bank transfer from a Moroccan resident to a foreign company for services

### Takeaway
The IGOC 2026 relaxed and broadened "service imports" through banks, but I found no source spelling out a resident individual's procedure, documents or limits for paying a foreign online school by SWIFT transfer. Expect the bank to ask for an invoice and to treat it under service-import rules (inference). It is a slow, high-friction route, suited only to large upfront packs.

### Cited Findings
- IGOC 2026 clarified and relaxed the service-import regime: broader definition, the limited list of operations delegated to banks removed, more entities allowed (including microfinance associations). — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/reglementation-des-changes-dotation-pour-voyages-e-commerce-export-import-ce-qui-change-des-janvier_BPDQLTUSQFE5PNTNKVZO3UMNA4/)
- IGOC-24 defined service imports, listed the categories (many delivered cross-border, such as subscriptions) and named the entities allowed to carry them out. A commentator notes the risk of fictitious invoices presented to banks. — [Ecoactu](https://ecoactu.ma/igoc-operations-services/); [Ecoactu](https://ecoactu.ma/comment-doit-on-definir-les-importations-de-services/)
- Monthly study-abroad living-cost allowance: 15,000 MAD (for students abroad, not online courses). — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/reglementation-des-changes-dotation-pour-voyages-e-commerce-export-import-ce-qui-change-des-janvier_BPDQLTUSQFE5PNTNKVZO3UMNA4/)

### Inferences
- For Alimun, a bank transfer is an exception route: corporate or B2B clients, or individuals paying a large pack, against a pro-forma invoice in CAD, EUR or USD. Fees per SWIFT transfer are likely 150-400 MAD plus the correspondent fee (estimate, unsourced).

### Gaps
- No primary source on documents, per-transaction limits or whether online training is paid under the e-commerce allowance or under service imports when done by transfer.

## 6. Recommendation inputs: best acceptance for least effort

### Takeaway
1. Keep **Stripe Canada** as the web checkout and add a learner-facing guide to "pay with your bank's virtual card". Make packs the default and support monthly payments on reusable virtual cards. This takes no integration effort and uses the eligible 20,000 MAD allowance.
2. Use **App Store / Google Play through RevenueCat** for the 59 MAD app subscription. It takes over VAT (Apple, since Aug 2026) and renewal retries.
3. If volume justifies it, add **dLocal** (directly or through Yuno) for MAD-priced local card processing with recurring, plus Wafacash cash vouchers for packs. This is the only documented way a foreign company reaches cash payers and settles abroad.

A Moroccan entity with CMI, Payzone or YouCan Pay is the higher-effort alternative.

### Cited Findings
- The allowance covers online training and SaaS subscriptions from foreign providers, up to 20,000 MAD a year. — [Upsilon](https://upsilon-consulting.com/commerce-electronique-change-maroc/)
- Virtual cards are available from the five largest banks, with reusable and long-validity options and 3DS. — [Wafir](https://wafir.ma/fr/guide/cartes-virtuelles-paiement-en-ligne-maroc-2026)
- dLocal offers Moroccan cards with recurring, plus Wafacash. — [dLocal docs](https://docs.dlocal.com/docs/morocco)
- Apple collects and remits Moroccan VAT. — [Morocco World News](https://moroccoworldnews.com/2026/08/336622/apple-applies-moroccos-20-vat-across-app-store-proceeds-and-prices)
- Cross-border processing costs about 2.9-3.9% plus fixed fees, and local acquiring is cheaper. — [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/morocco-online-payment-gateway-comparison-2026)

### Inferences
- Expected success rates are estimates only; no data was found:
  - A learner with a bank app and a reusable virtual card should succeed on Stripe for one-off payments.
  - Monthly renewals will fail more often, for expired or capped virtual cards and allowance exhaustion.
  - Learners without a bank card (a large share, given 4% ownership in 2022 data and 86% of card use being withdrawals) need cash (Wafacash via dLocal) or a Cash Plus card.
- Measure, don't guess. Log Stripe decline codes for Moroccan BINs in the first months before committing to dLocal.

### Gaps
- No measured authorisation rates for Moroccan cards at foreign merchants.
- No public pricing for dLocal.
- VAT obligations on direct sales are unresolved.
