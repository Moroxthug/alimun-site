# Teacher pay gap-fill: market hourly rates by country and language (2024-2026)

Scope: fills gaps left by existing notes. It does not repeat these figures: Cambly ~$10.20/h, Lingoda ~EUR 11/h, Preply 18-33% commission, Philippines PHP 110-150/h online, Spain EUR 13.8-27.6/h, France FLE EUR 15-25/h, Brazil Wizard/CNA BRL 20-29/h, Morocco British Council MAD 12,808-14,688/month. Those are only used below to compute "+20%" figures.

Research done 26 Oct 2026, with 24 tool calls. Many hourly figures are aggregator or blog estimates (PayScale, TEFL recruiters, Cronoshare, Superprof). Primary sources such as job ads, Bundestag papers and university calls are marked. Confidence is generally LOW-MEDIUM outside Germany.

## Exchange rates used

- ECB euro reference rates, 23 Oct 2026: 1 EUR = 1.1193 USD, 55.6715 TRY, 106.2720 INR, 5.6485 BRL, 21.0226 MXN, 71.235 PHP, 18.5777 ZAR — [ECB eurofxref-daily](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml)
- The ECB does not publish these currencies, so they come from a market aggregator, 26 Oct 2026: 1 EUR = 11.0008 MAD, 62.148 EGP, 3.354 TND, 3,549.8 COP, 1,712.9 ARS, 90.74 ALL — [open.er-api.com](https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/EUR)
- Caveat: TRY and ARS have high inflation. Local-currency figures from 2024 or early 2025 are worth noticeably less in EUR today. Re-anchor them to new job ads before paying.

Most non-EU markets sit at EUR 2-11/h for school or institute teaching, before the +20%. The "+20%" rule gives very low pay in India, Egypt, Tunisia and the Philippines: EUR 2-3/h, well below the online-platform floor of about EUR 9-11/h at Cambly and Lingoda. Germany under the BAMF benchmark is around EUR 50-68/h. Online German tutor prices (about EUR 28-33/h listed) are far above online English or Italian.

### Inferences
- A literal "local market +20%" makes Alimun pay in South Asia, Egypt, Tunisia and the Philippines (EUR 2-3/h) lower than Cambly and Lingoda, which teachers there can join. The real market for an online live teacher is the online-platform rate, not the local school rate. A sensible floor is about EUR 10-12/h (Cambly/Lingoda +20%). Applying it would put India, Egypt, Tunisia, Philippines, Mexico, Colombia, South Africa and Brazil at the floor, and Turkey and Morocco close to it.
- German is a premium language everywhere: online German tutors list at about EUR 28-33/h, versus about EUR 14 for Italian on Preply, and an Indian university pays roughly four times the English institute rate for German. Per-language (not only per-country) rates are justified.
- Germany-resident German teachers are anchored by BAMF: EUR 42.23/UE ×1.2 = EUR 50.68/UE (EUR 67.57 per 60 min). That is far above EUR 20 flat, so Alimun is unlikely to attract Germany-based integration-course teachers at local-market +20% unless the online-platform benchmark (about EUR 28-33) is used instead.

### Gaps
- Albania/Kosovo: no German, Italian or English teacher pay found in English or Albanian searches.
- Online-rate data by country of the teacher (Preply/italki filtered by country) could not be extracted. Listing pages are dynamic.

## Germany: current BAMF Honorar for integration courses per Unterrichtseinheit (2025/2026)

### Takeaway
The BAMF minimum fee (Honoraruntergrenze) for freelance integration-course teachers is **EUR 42.23 per 45-minute UE**. It has not changed since 1 August 2022 and was still current in Bundestag papers of the 21st Wahlperiode and the DVV's March 2026 factsheet. About 94% of freelance teachers are paid exactly the floor.

### Cited Findings
- "Aktuell beträgt die Honoraruntergrenze für Integrationskurse 42,23 Euro und ist damit seit dem 1. August 2022 nicht mehr erhöht worden" — [Bundestag Drucksache 21/3404](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/034/2103404.pdf); also [Drucksache 21/3681](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/036/2103681.pdf)
- 93.9% of freelance teachers work at the BAMF minimum rate (EvIk evaluation 2023, cited in the same Bundestag document) — [Drucksache 21/3404](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/034/2103404.pdf)
- DVV factsheet (March 2026): the Honoraruntergrenze has been at least EUR 42.23 per course hour since 2022. The provider reimbursement rate (Kostenerstattungssatz) was cut by about 10% in October 2025 for courses with 17 or more participants, which puts pressure on providers' ability to pay above the floor — [DVV factsheet 2026-03](https://www.volkshochschule.de/themen/integration/index.php.media/51893/2026-03-Factsheet-Finanzierungsluecken-bei-Integrations-und-Berufssprachkursen.pdf). I saw this in search results only; fetching the PDF returned 404.
- GEW: a fee of EUR 54-60 per teaching hour would match pay for tariff-employed teachers. About 20,000 freelance teachers work in BAMF courses — [GEW](https://www.gew.de/aktuelles/detailseite/neuigkeiten/mehr-geld-fuer-integrationskurslehrkraefte-oder-doch-nicht/) (search snippet; the page returned 403)
- A vhs Kitzingen BAMF Honorarkraft job ad (14 Jan 2026) does not state a fee — [vhs Kitzingen PDF](https://www.vhs-kitzingen.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Dokumente/Kursleitersuche/2026_01_14_.BAMF_Honorarkraft.pdf)

### Inferences
- Market +20%: EUR 50.68 per UE, or EUR 67.57 per 60 minutes (42.23 / 45 × 60 = 56.31; × 1.2). Converting UE to 60 minutes is my arithmetic.
- The Kostenerstattungssatz cut makes a floor increase in 2026 unlikely in the short term (inference).

### Gaps
- I did not read the BAMF Trägerrundschreiben 03/25 (28 Feb 2025) directly. Berufssprachkurs (DeuFöV) fee floors were not checked.

## German teachers outside Germany

### Takeaway
Goethe-Institut posts do not publish rates. They say pay follows the "local salary scheme" of each country's institute. The only hard figure found is the University of Mumbai at INR 900-1,500/h (about EUR 8.5-14). Morocco, Egypt, Tunisia, Turkey, Colombia/Brazil and Albania/Kosovo remain gaps for German specifically.

### Cited Findings
- Goethe-Institut India posts (Kolkata, Chennai, 2025-2026) state remuneration "based on the local salary scheme of the Goethe-Instituts in India" and give no figure. Partner-school teaching needs German at B1 or above — [Goethe Kolkata careers](https://www.goethe.de/ins/in/en/sta/kol/ueb/kar.html), [Goethe Chennai](https://www.goethe.de/ins/in/de/sta/che/ueb/kar/goethe-institut.html)
- University of Mumbai German department (2025 call): INR 900 or 1,000/h for BA/MA teaching, INR 1,500/h for weekend German-as-foreign-language courses, with 30% tax deducted at source — [Univ. Göttingen-hosted Ausschreibung 2025](https://wwwt2.uni-goettingen.de/de/document/download/b5470ce2a5527a67f525d616fdb0f961.pdf/Ausschreibung%202025.pdf)
- Goethe-Institut Egypt (Alexandria): pay follows "the current valid pay scheme for the Goethe-Institut Ägypten", with no figure — [Goethe Alexandria](https://www.goethe.de/ins/eg/de/sta/alx/ueb/kar/lkv.html)
- Goethe-Institut Turkey: freelance DaF teachers are only engaged against monthly invoicing under Turkish law, and pay depends on the work assigned. No rate is published — [Goethe Türkei HLK](https://www.goethe.de/ins/tr/de/ueb/kar/hlk.html), [Ankara 2022](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf251/2022_-ausschreibung_fm_ankara.pdf)
- Benchmark only, outside scope: Goethe-Institut Tanzania pays TZS 15,000 per 45-min unit, with a minimum of 20 units per week — [Goethe Tansania](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ts/de/ueb/kar/hlk.html)
- Tunisia: a Tunis language school advertised for German teachers with a "salaire motivant selon horaire" and no amount — [Orientini](https://www.orientini.com/FR/Annonces_Orientation_Formation_Emploi/6332/Recrutement-d-enseignants-d-Allemand)
- Morocco: no German-specific hourly rate found. Private language centres in general pay MAD 8,000-14,000/month — [tefl.ie](https://tefl.ie/tefl-salaries-in-morocco/)
- Online German: Preply German pages show an average of about $37 per 60 minutes and about 2,152 tutors, with individual tutors ranging from about $7 to $45 — [Preply German tutors](https://preply.com/en/online/german-tutors?ref=hackernoon.com). Languatalk's research gives an average of $29 / EUR 28 / GBP 24.50 for German tutors, online and in person — [Languatalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/how-much-do-German-tutors-charge/)

### Inferences
- For non-German-resident German teachers, the online rate (about EUR 28-33/h listed, about EUR 22-27 net after Preply commission) is a better "market" than local school rates. A local-market rule would pay a Moroccan or Egyptian German teacher about EUR 9-11/h, while Preply suggests they can list at EUR 15-30.

### Gaps
- No German-teacher pay found for Morocco, Egypt, Tunisia, Turkey, Colombia, Brazil, Albania or Kosovo. The Goethe local salary schemes are internal. The next step would be asking Goethe partner schools (e.g. in Tirana or Casablanca) or checking LinkedIn/Bayt/Kariyer job ads directly.

## Italian teachers: Italy, Albania, Argentina, online

### Takeaway
In Italy, private lessons average about EUR 14-15/h for English. Italian-for-foreigners online teaching is advertised at EUR 15-30/h. On Preply most Italian tutors charge $11-20/h. No CPIA, scuola di lingua (CCNL), Albania or Argentina Italian-teacher pay figures were found.

### Cited Findings
- Italian-for-foreigners teachers (part-time, online tutoring company Le Tue Lezioni): EUR 15-30/h, no experience required — [Teamtailor ad](https://letuelezioni.teamtailor.com/jobs/7659514-insegnante-di-italiano-per-stranieri-part-time)
- Private English lessons in Italy: Superprof average EUR 14/h — [Superprof](https://www.superprof.it/blog/costo-ripetizioni-a-domicilio/). Money.it (updated Sept 2025): about EUR 15 for an English lesson — [Money.it](https://www.money.it/quanto-chiedere-ripetizioni-tariffe-lezioni-private). Cronoshare: average EUR 15, range EUR 10-20 — [Cronoshare](https://www.cronoshare.it/servizi/ripetizioni-inglese)
- Online Italian on Preply (undated blog count): of 1,100+ Italian tutors, 611 charge $11-20, 275 charge $21-30 and 188 charge under $10 — [ThinkInItalian](https://www.thinkinitalian.com/italian-language-classes-prices/)
- Comparison abroad: a 2025-26 post for Italian teachers in Uzbekistan offered about $1,000/month plus housing — [UniStraSi PDF](https://www.unistrasi.it/public/articoli/7339/All.%20Info%20selezione%20Speakitaly%20NYC.pdf) (search snippet)

### Inferences
- Italy "+20%": about EUR 18/h on the tutoring benchmark, or EUR 27/h on the Italian-L2 online midpoint, which is close to the current EUR 20 flat.
- Online Italian market +20% ≈ EUR 16.6/h ($15.5 midpoint).

### Gaps
- CPIA teachers are state employees (CCNL Scuola), and I found no hourly equivalent. No CCNL rate for private scuole di lingua was found. No figures for Albania or Argentina Italian teachers.
