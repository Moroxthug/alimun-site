# Teacher supply and pay: where to hire English, German, Italian, French and Spanish teachers, and what they are paid (2024-2026)

Exchange rates used throughout: ECB euro reference rates of **23 October 2026**: 1 EUR = 1.1193 USD, 0.83728 GBP, 1.5665 CAD, 71.235 PHP, 106.272 INR, 18.5777 ZAR, 21.0226 MXN, 5.6485 BRL, 55.6715 TRY, 4.3945 PLN, 5.3492 RON, 366.73 HUF, 24.327 CZK — [ECB eurofxref](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/shared/pdf/eurofxref.pdf). Conversions to EUR are my own arithmetic. They are rounded and marked "≈".

Scope warning for the report writer: the research ran on a budget of about 15 tool calls. Coverage is therefore uneven. Online English platforms, the Philippines, France, Spain, Germany (integration courses) and payout tools have sourced numbers. Most other sourcing countries named in the brief have none: South Africa, Nigeria/Kenya, India, Colombia/Brazil/Argentina, Morocco/Egypt/Tunisia/Senegal, Eastern Europe, Turkey, Ukraine, Western Balkans, Albania, Italy, Quebec and Venezuela. Those countries are listed under Gaps and were not filled from memory.

## 1. Market hourly pay for language teachers by sourcing country

### Takeaway
The figures that have sources fall into three bands:
- **Low band:** the Philippines pays about €1.5–2.1/h for online English.
- **Middle band:** global online platforms pay native-speaker English tutors about €6–12/h, and Novakid pays up to about €21/h.
- **High band:** in-person adult teaching in Western Europe pays about €15–47/h gross. Examples are Spain (€27.63/h at a university language centre), France (€23–25/h freelance FLE, €14.92/h gross salaried) and Germany (integration courses, historically €35 per 45-minute unit).

A flat €20/h is far above market for online English everywhere sourced here. It is near or below in-person market rates in Spain, France and Germany.

### Cited Findings
**English: Philippines (online)**
- PayScale gives an average of ₱124.80/h (≈ €1.75) for ESL teachers with online-education skills, with a range of about ₱36–245/h (≈ €0.51–3.44). The general ESL-teacher average is ₱140.60/h (≈ €1.97). PayScale labels these as 2026 data — [PayScale PH](https://www.payscale.com/research/PH/Job=English_as_a_Second_Language_(ESL)_Teacher/Hourly_Rate/422d53ae/Online-Education); [PayScale PH ESL](https://www.payscale.com/research/PH/Job=English_as_a_Second_Language_(ESL)_Teacher/Hourly_Rate)
- A JobStreet remote online English tutor posting offers ₱110–130/h (≈ €1.54–1.82) — [JobStreet PH](https://ph.jobstreet.com/job/92368367)
- An Indeed posting (Funtalk) offers ₱150 per teaching hour (≈ €2.11) plus a guaranteed ₱16,000/month (≈ €225) — [Indeed PH](https://ph.indeed.com/ESL-Online-Teacher-jobs-in-Philippines)
- Indeed puts Engoo pay in the Philippines at about ₱111/h for Tutor and ₱128/h for ESL Teacher (≈ €1.56–1.80). This is a small sample — [Indeed Engoo PH](https://ph.indeed.com/cmp/Engoo/salaries)
- A CazVid posting pays ₱60–70 per 30-minute class, about ₱120–140/h back to back (≈ €1.68–1.97). The source is unverified — [CazVid](https://cazvid.com/en/job/online-filipino-esl-teachers-b2f729bf)

**Spanish: Spain**
- UAB Idiomes Barcelona offers €27.63 per teaching hour for Spanish (ELE) teachers (call of December 2025, start January 2026). The 2024 call offered €27.49/h — [UAB convocatoria Dec 2025](https://www-balan.uab.cat/doc/convo-prof-espanyol-uabidiomesbcn-desembre2025); [UAB earlier call](https://uab.cat/doc/RH_CONVOCATORIA_PROFESSORAT_UAB_IDIOMES_ESPANYOL)
- Universidad de Salamanca Cursos Internacionales offered a 50% part-time contract at €10,000 gross/year over 14 payments for 723 h/year (October 2025). That is ≈ €13.83 gross per contracted hour (my arithmetic) — [USAL](https://cursosinternacionales.usal.es/sites/default/files/documentos/CONVOCATORIA_CONTRATO_RELEVO_PROFESOR.pdf)
- Spanish language-school teachers fall under the *X Convenio Colectivo de Enseñanza y Formación No Reglada 2024-2027*. UCLM's 2025-26 ELE post is a part-time grupo I contract of 795 hours under that agreement. The salary table was not retrieved — [UCLM](https://fundaciongeneraluclm.es/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/ESPACU-0032025-DOCENTE-CURSO-ANUAL-2526.pdf); [Univ. La Rioja](https://fundacion.unirioja.es/app/webroot/home/public/files/ofertas_empleo/Profesor%20titular%20de%20idiomas.pdf)

**Spanish: Latin America (online)**
- In September 2024, The Really Great Teacher Company advertised remote adult Spanish teaching at USD 5.5–5.9/h (≈ €4.91–5.27) for LATAM and US-based independent contractors. The listing is older than the 2025 window and covers several countries at once — [Remotive](https://remotive.com/remote-jobs/all-others/online-spanish-teachers-1934520)
- Argentina had about 1,400 registered Preply tutors in 2024, which makes it a major source of Spanish tutors. The article's rate figures were not retrieved — [iProfesional](https://www.iprofesional.com/negocios/422744-como-ganar-en-dolares-ensenando-online-espanol-sin-ser-profesor)

**French: France**
- A 2025 France Travail posting for a freelance (indépendant) FLE trainer pays €23–25/h by experience (Soisy-sous-Montmorency) — [France Travail 191MRDR](https://candidat.francetravail.fr/offres/recherche/detail/191MRDR)
- A salaried FLE trainer post in Vénissieux pays €14.92 gross/h under the training-organisations collective agreement — [France Travail 191GXBQ](https://candidat.francetravail.fr/offres/recherche/detail/191GXBQ)
- Université Paris-Saclay pays vacataire language teachers about €43.5 gross/h for 2025-26. That figure is quoted for the English line, not FLE — [Paris-Saclay](https://www.sciences.universite-paris-saclay.fr/recruitment-vacataire-teachers)

**French: Morocco**
- Institut français Tétouan (2024) employs 78 vacataire FLE teachers and requires a Bac+4/5 in FLE or proven experience. No pay is stated — [IF Maroc](https://if-maroc.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/TETOUAN-Enseignant-FLE-2024.pdf)

**German: Germany (integration courses, DaF/DaZ)**
- GEW reported that freelance integration-course teachers' fees would rise from €23 to €35 per teaching unit of 45 minutes (≈ €46.67 per 60 minutes). It also said the BAMF per-participant rate rose from €3.10 to €3.90. **This is an old figure**: the GEW page shows no date, and it sits alongside a 2016 Bundestag document. It should not be read as the 2025 rate. GEW also warns that many providers do not pass increases on to teachers — [GEW](https://www.gew.de/aktuelles/detailseite/mehr-geld-fuer-integrationskurslehrkraefte-oder-doch-nicht); [Bundestag 2016](https://www.mueller-gemmeke.de/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/1808476.pdf)
- BAMF sets a minimum fee per teaching unit for freelance integration teachers as a condition of multi-year provider approval. The actual fee is negotiated with the provider — [Bundestag doc](https://www.mueller-gemmeke.de/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/1808476.pdf); [GEW Ziel Festanstellung](https://www.gew.de/aktuelles/detailseite/ziel-festanstellung)
- Current postings (VHS Kitzingen, December 2025 and January 2026) ask for a BAMF teaching licence and a DTZ examiner licence and offer 20 units of 45 minutes per week. No pay is shown — [VHS Kitzingen 2025-12](https://www.vhs.kitzingen.info/fileadmin/user_upload/Dokumente/Kursleitersuche/2025_12_19.BAMF_Honorarkraft.pdf); [VHS Kitzingen 2026-01](https://www.vhs.kitzingen.info/fileadmin/user_upload/Dokumente/Kursleitersuche/2026_01_14_.BAMF_Honorarkraft.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Market +20%, computed only where a sourced market figure exists (estimates):**

| Language / country / mode | Market figure (source above) | ≈ EUR/h | Market +20% ≈ EUR/h | vs flat €20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| English, Philippines, online | ₱125/h (PayScale avg) | 1.75 | 2.10 | €20 is about 9.5× the +20% figure |
| English, global native tutors (Cambly) | USD 10.20/h | 9.11 | 10.93 | €20 is higher |
| English kids (Novakid) | USD 14–24/h | 12.5–21.4 | 15.0–25.7 | about equal |
| Spanish, LATAM online (2024) | USD 5.5–5.9/h | 4.9–5.3 | 5.9–6.3 | €20 is about 3× |
| Spanish, Spain, in person | €27.63/h (UAB) | 27.63 | 33.16 | €20 is lower |
| Spanish, Spain, salaried | ≈ €13.83/contracted h (USAL) | 13.83 | 16.60 | €20 is higher |
| French, France, freelance | €23–25/h | 24 | 28.80 | €20 is lower |
| French, France, salaried | €14.92 gross/h | 14.92 | 17.90 | €20 is higher |
| German, Germany, integration (old) | €35 per 45 min | 46.67 | 56.00 | €20 is far lower |

- A flat €20/h is generous for online teaching anywhere outside Western Europe, the US and Canada. It would be roughly 2–10× local online pay in the Philippines and Latin America. It is not "above market" for qualified freelance teachers resident in France, Spain or Germany, who earn €23–47/h in person. Germany is where it falls shortest, because integration-course DaZ work pays per 45-minute unit.
- Salaried hourly rates in Spain and France (€14–15 gross) include employer-borne social charges and paid leave. A contractor rate should not be compared one to one with them.

### Gaps
- No sourced 2024-26 figures were found for:
  - English: South Africa, Nigeria, Kenya, India, Colombia, Brazil, Argentina, Morocco, Egypt, Eastern Europe, and UK/US/Canada/Ireland in-person rates.
  - German: Austria, Turkey, Egypt, Tunisia/Morocco, Ukraine, Western Balkans, Latin America.
  - Italian: anywhere (Italy, Albania, Argentina).
  - French: Morocco, Tunisia, Senegal, Quebec (Morocco pay not stated in the IF posting).
  - Spanish: Mexico, Colombia, Argentina and Venezuela individually.
- The current (2025/2026) BAMF minimum fee per integration-course unit was not confirmed. The source found is older and should be checked on bamf.de.
- Country-level average monthly teacher salaries (employee basis) were not collected for any country.

## 2. Online platform pay benchmarks

### Takeaway
Big online platforms pay roughly USD 8–13/h (≈ €7–12) to adult English tutors. Kids' platforms pay somewhat more: Cambly Kids pays USD 12/h and Novakid USD 14–24/h. Marketplaces such as Preply keep 18–33% commission plus the full trial-lesson fee. No platform published language-specific teacher pay for German, Italian, French or Spanish that could be found.

### Cited Findings
- **Cambly:** a fixed USD 0.17/min ≈ USD 10.20/h (≈ €9.11). Cambly Kids pays USD 0.20/min ≈ USD 12/h (≈ €10.72). There is no commission and teachers cannot set their own rate. Waiting time without calls is unpaid, and new-tutor intake has been limited — [teast.co](https://teast.co/blog/preply-vs-italki-vs-cambly); [lilachbullock.com](https://www.lilachbullock.com/are-online-tutoring-jobs-legitimate-pay/); [frugalforless](https://www.frugalforless.com/best-paid-online-english-teaching-jobs/)
- **Lingoda:** blog sources disagree.
  - USD 8–13/h (≈ €7.15–11.61), the same for group and 1:1 classes — [tefl.org](https://www.tefl.org/teach-english-online/lingoda/)
  - A fixed €11/h — [teast.co Lingoda](https://teast.co/blog/teach-english-online-lingoda)
  - Group classes about USD 10–20/h and 1:1 about USD 35/h — reported in search results from the same cluster of blogs
  - Lingoda requires C2 level in the language taught, and English teachers need TEFL/TESOL plus about 2 years' experience — [tefl.org](https://www.tefl.org/teach-english-online/lingoda/); [jenontherun](https://www.jenontherun.com/lingoda-vs-preply/)
  - Lingoda student prices are higher for German and Italian (a 5-class pack costs USD 99.99 / 94.99) than for English, French and Spanish (USD 79.99) — [PCMag via search](https://tech.yahoo.com/articles/lingoda-145831490.html)
  - No official Lingoda pay page was retrieved.
- **Preply:** tutors set their own price. Most English tutors charge about USD 15–25/h. Commission runs from 33% down to 18% as hours build, and the trial lesson is unpaid (100% commission) — [teast.co](https://teast.co/blog/preply-vs-italki-vs-cambly); [fluentu](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/educator-english/esl-online-teaching-jobs/). One non-Preply blog states 31–56%, which conflicts with this — [lilachbullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/?p=116415)
- **italki** (comparison): 21% commission on single lessons, 15% on packages, 0% on trials — [teast.co](https://teast.co/blog/preply-vs-italki-vs-cambly)
- Realistic tutor earnings after commission and unbooked time are about £8–18/h (≈ €9.55–21.50) — [lilachbullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/are-online-tutoring-jobs-legitimate-pay/)
- **Novakid:**
  - USD 7–12 per 25-minute lesson (≈ USD 14–24/h, ≈ €12.5–21.4) according to a 2025 teacher interview, with outliers of USD 4–12.50 per lesson — [TEFL Academy](https://www.theteflacademy.com/blog/teaching-on-novakid-what-you-need-to-know/)
  - Another guide gives USD 15–22/h — [teast.co Novakid](https://teast.co/blog/teach-english-online-novakid)
  - A Novakid listing for non-native speakers offers up to USD 7/h (≈ €6.25) — [teast job](https://teast.co/job/online-esl-teacher-open-to-non-native-speakers-mTbYM4bk9tdMpZ6VQxeE)
- **Engoo:** about ₱111–128/h in the Philippines (≈ €1.56–1.80) — [Indeed PH](https://ph.indeed.com/cmp/Engoo/salaries)

### Inferences
- Preply net for a USD 15–25/h English tutor is about USD 10–20.5/h (≈ €9–18) after an 18–33% commission, before unpaid trials (my arithmetic, an estimate).
- Lingoda is the closest model to Alimun (live small groups, salaried-like schedule), so it is the most relevant benchmark. Its €11/h (or USD 8–13) English rate means €20/h would be roughly 1.7–2.5× Lingoda.

### Gaps
- No pay data was found for Open English.
- No platform publishes German, French, Spanish or Italian teacher pay separately. Reddit threads in r/OnlineESLTeaching and r/TEFL were not reached.
- No official Cambly, Lingoda or Preply teacher pages were fetched directly. The figures come from teacher-recruitment blogs.

## 3. Freelance vs employee: monthly salaries and "market +20%" per hour

### Takeaway
Little sourced monthly salary data was found. In Spain and France, salaried language-school teaching works out to about €14–15 gross per hour. Freelance rates in the same countries are €23–28/h. In the Philippines, a guaranteed online-teacher salary floor is about ₱16,000/month (≈ €225).

### Cited Findings
- Spain: a 50% contract pays €10,000 gross/year (14 payments, 723 h/year) — [USAL](https://cursosinternacionales.usal.es/sites/default/files/documentos/CONVOCATORIA_CONTRATO_RELEVO_PROFESOR.pdf)
- France: salaried FLE trainers earn €14.92 gross/h and freelance trainers €23–25/h — [France Travail](https://candidat.francetravail.fr/offres/recherche/detail/191GXBQ); [France Travail](https://candidat.francetravail.fr/offres/recherche/detail/191MRDR)
- Germany: integration teachers are mostly freelance (Honorarkräfte), and GEW campaigns for permanent employment ("Ziel Festanstellung") — [GEW](https://www.gew.de/aktuelles/detailseite/ziel-festanstellung)
- Philippines: Funtalk pays ₱150/h with a ₱16,000/month guarantee — [Indeed PH](https://ph.indeed.com/ESL-Online-Teacher-jobs-in-Philippines)

### Inferences
- In Spain and France, freelance hourly rates run about 1.6–1.8× salaried gross hourly rates (€23–25 vs €14.92). This is consistent with freelancers carrying their own social charges and unpaid holidays (estimate).

### Gaps
- Average monthly teacher salaries for the other sourcing countries were not collected (Glassdoor, SalaryExplorer and national statistics were not reached).

## 4. Prep time, no-shows, cancellations and non-rate factors

### Takeaway
The evidence is thin. Lingoda reportedly pays 50% for student no-shows or late cancellations. Marketplaces do not pay for trials or idle waiting time. Teachers' main complaint about platforms is unstable income, which points to guaranteed hours as a strong draw.

### Cited Findings
- Lingoda reportedly pays teachers 50% of the fee if a student does not turn up or cancels within 48 hours — [tefl.org](https://www.tefl.org/teach-english-online/lingoda/)
- Preply trial lessons are unpaid to the tutor — [teast.co](https://teast.co/blog/preply-vs-italki-vs-cambly)
- On Cambly, waiting time without calls is unpaid, and it is "great for filling spare hours, not ideal for full-time income" — [lilachbullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/are-online-tutoring-jobs-legitimate-pay/); [frugalforless](https://www.frugalforless.com/best-paid-online-english-teaching-jobs/)
- Funtalk (Philippines) uses a guaranteed monthly salary plus a per-hour rate — [Indeed PH](https://ph.indeed.com/ESL-Online-Teacher-jobs-in-Philippines)
- An anecdotal example of separating prep pay: USD 25 for one hour of instruction plus USD 25 for lesson planning — [International TEFL Academy](https://www.internationalteflacademy.com/blog/how-do-online-english-teachers-get-paid)
- German integration posts offer a fixed block of 20 units a week, which is a predictable workload — [VHS Kitzingen](https://www.vhs.kitzingen.info/fileadmin/user_upload/Dokumente/Kursleitersuche/2025_12_19.BAMF_Honorarkraft.pdf)

### Inferences
- If Alimun pays its full rate for no-shows, pays something for prep and guarantees a cohort schedule, it would stand out against Cambly, Preply and Lingoda even at the same hourly rate (inference).

### Gaps
- No survey data on what teachers value was found. Forum threads were not reached.

## 5. Payouts, contractor platforms, misclassification and tax

### Takeaway
- **Stripe Connect** cross-border payouts are self-serve only into the US, UK, EEA, Canada and Switzerland. They cost 0.25% per payout and are free within the EEA and between the UK and the EEA. Teachers in the Philippines, Morocco, Latin America and Africa therefore need another channel: Wise, Payoneer, Stripe Global Payouts via sales, or Deel/Remote/Oyster.
- **Contractor management** costs about USD 29/contractor/month on Remote and Oyster. Deel is quoted at USD 49, though one source says Deel is free.

### Cited Findings
- **Stripe cross-border payouts:**
  - 0.25% fee per cross-border payout, waived for payouts within the EEA and between the UK and the EEA — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/cross-border-payouts)
  - Self-serve only to the US, UK, EEA, Canada and Switzerland. Other countries require contacting sales — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/cross-border-payouts)
  - Requires the Full service agreement. The Recipient agreement uses "Global payouts" instead — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/cross-border-payouts)
  - The platform account must itself be in one of those regions — [WC Vendors](https://www.wcvendors.com/knowledge-base/how-to-enable-cross-border-payouts-for-international-vendors/)
- **Remote:** contractor management at USD 29/contractor/month (≈ €25.91), billed only for active contractors. "Plus" costs USD 99 (≈ €88.45) and includes indemnity up to USD 100,000 — [casrai guide](https://casrai.org/guides/remote-com-pricing); [Rivermate](https://rivermate.com/es/blog/precio-remoto-com)
- **Oyster:** USD 29/contractor/month, an optional misclassification add-on, and a reported free tier for up to 2 contractors — [eorhq](https://eorhq.com/guides/oyster-pricing/); [anywherer](https://www.anywherer.com/how-much-does-oyster-cost/)
- **Deel:** USD 49/contractor/month (≈ €43.78), as verified in September 2026 by a third-party guide. Another source says contractor management is free. The two conflict — [dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-deel-alternatives); [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/guides/best-contractor-payment-platforms)
- **Employer of record** (if teachers become employees): Remote and Oyster are about USD 599–699/employee/month — [eorhq](https://eorhq.com/guides/oyster-pricing/); [Rivermate](https://rivermate.com/es/blog/precio-remoto-com)
- **Payoneer:** available in Nigeria, Ghana, Morocco and Kenya — [WorldFirst](https://www.worldfirst.com/af/blog/business-banking-insights/payoneer-review-nigeria/)
  - Receiving fees: 0–1% for USD receipts, 2.9% + USD 0.49 for cards, and possible intermediary charges on SWIFT. The source is a competitor and its numbers conflict with others — [PhotonPay](https://www.photonpay.com/hk/blog/article/wise-vs-payoneer); [Wise blog](https://www.wise.com/gb/blog/cross-border-payouts-payoneer)
- **Wise Business:** batch transfers of up to 1,000 payments at once. Sending to Nigeria costs up to about 3% — [Wise Nigeria guide](https://wise.com/gb/blog/payoneer-nigeria); [Airwallex](https://www.airwallex.com/en-nz/blog/wise-vs-payoneer)

### Inferences
- At 20 class hours/month × €20 = €400/teacher, a USD 29 contractor platform adds about 6.5% on top of pay. Deel at USD 49 adds about 11%. Wise or Stripe at 0–3% is cheaper but leaves contracts, compliance and misclassification risk with Alimun (estimate).

### Gaps
- Contractor misclassification rules for teachers in the EU, UK (IR35 / employment status) and Canada were not researched with sources.
- The owner's VAT and withholding obligations when buying services from contractors abroad were not researched with sources (for example reverse charge on B2B services, or withholding in Morocco).
- Stripe's full Express country list and Global Payouts pricing were not retrieved.
- Official Wise and Payoneer fee pages were not fetched. The figures above come from competitor blogs.

## 6. Qualification signals and verification cost

### Takeaway
Only partial findings were made. Platforms and institutions screen for TEFL/TESOL plus experience (English), C2 level (any language), a BAMF teaching licence and DTZ examiner licence (German integration courses), and a Bac+4/5 in FLE (French in Morocco). Certificate costs and verification methods were not researched.

### Cited Findings
- Lingoda requires TEFL/TESOL plus about 2 years' experience for English, and C2 in the language taught — [tefl.org](https://www.tefl.org/teach-english-online/lingoda/); [jenontherun](https://www.jenontherun.com/lingoda-vs-preply/)
- German integration courses require a BAMF Zulassung as teacher plus a DTZ examiner licence — [VHS Kitzingen](https://www.vhs.kitzingen.info/fileadmin/user_upload/Dokumente/Kursleitersuche/2025_12_19.BAMF_Honorarkraft.pdf)
- Institut français Morocco requires a Bac+4/5 in FLE or proven FLE experience — [IF Maroc](https://if-maroc.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/TETOUAN-Enseignant-FLE-2024.pdf)

### Gaps
- No data was gathered on CELTA, DITALS, CEDILS, DELF/DALF examiner or DELE/ELE accreditation, their cost, or how to verify them (for example Cambridge certificate verification, or the Goethe-Institut and Siena/Ca' Foscari registries).
