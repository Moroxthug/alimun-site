# Other target countries for paid live online German, English and Italian classes (excluding Morocco, Canada, South America)

Research date: 26 Oct 2026. Researcher budget: about 20 tool calls, so coverage is broad but thin on local prices, wages and payment rails. Every figure carries a URL; anything without a source is in Gaps or marked as an inference or estimate.

Exchange rate used: 1 EUR = 106.54 INR (ECB reference rate, 15 Oct 2026) — [ECB INR series](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy%5Fand%5Fexchange%5Frates/euro%5Freference%5Fexchange%5Frates/html/eurofxref-graph-inr.en.html). Other rates found: 1 EUR = 70.170 PHP (ECB, 1 Jul 2026) — [ECB 20260701](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/exchange/eurofxref/shared/pdf/2026/07/20260701.pdf); 1 EUR = 51.2795 TRY (ECB, 2 Apr 2026, latest found) — [ECB TRY series](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-try.fi.html). The ECB does not publish EGP or VND reference rates.

## German demand: which countries send the most workers, nurses, trainees, students and Chancenkarte applicants?

### Takeaway
India and Turkey are the two largest and best-documented sources of German learners who need German for a move (students, Blue Card, Chancenkarte, nurse recognition), followed by the Western Balkans (Westbalkanregelung), Tunisia and the Philippines (nurses), Uzbekistan and Iran (skilled-worker visas) and fast-growing Egypt and Kenya. Official data on 2025 visas by nationality was not found; the best data points are 2024.

### Cited Findings
- The 2025 "Deutsch als Fremdsprache weltweit" survey (presented 28 Jul 2025) describes German as in demand mainly as a professional language. It names rising demand in Egypt, India, Kenya and Colombia, and links German learning to professional mobility in Cameroon, Morocco, Nepal and Uzbekistan. It publishes no global learner total in 2025 because data quality varied too much between countries — [Auswärtiges Amt](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/newsroom/deutsch-als-fremdsprache-2728478); [Goethe-Institut press release 28 Jul 2025](https://www.goethe.de/de/uun/prs/pma/26859595.html)
- The commonly quoted figure of 15.4 million German learners worldwide comes from the 2015 survey, not 2025 (OLD figure, about 90 percent of them at school) — [DAAD](https://www2.daad.de/der-daad/daad-aktuell/de/34461-die-deutsche-sprache-bleibt-gefragt/)
- 2024: 88,543 visas were issued for skilled and qualified employment migration. The top nationalities among academic skilled workers (§18b) were Turkey, India, Uzbekistan, Iran and Kosovo. Among "Berufspraktiker" (§6 BeschV) they were Turkey, Philippines, India, Iran and Bosnia and Herzegovina (no counts per country given) — [Bundestag hib 97/2025, 20 Feb 2025](https://www.bundestag.de/presse/hib/kurzmeldungen-1053332) (based on Drucksache 20/14956: [PDF](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/20/149/2014956.pdf))
- Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card, since June 2024): 11,497 visas issued by mid-2025. India led with more than 3,700, followed by China, Turkey and Great Britain. Source: a government answer to a Bundestag question, reported second-hand — [VISAGUARD.Berlin, 16 Jul 2025](https://www.visaguard.berlin/en/post/opportunity-map-first-official-figures-show-great-interest-over-11-000-visas-since-launch). An earlier count (about 2,500 cards) had India at 780, then China, Turkey and Pakistan — [The African Courier](https://www.theafricancourier.de/germany-issues-2500-opportunity-cards-top-recipients-by-nationality)
- In June 2025, about 420,000 employees paying social insurance held an employment-based residence permit, up from just over 200,000 in 2020. More than half held an EU Blue Card. A good quarter come from the six Western Balkan states, whose annual Westbalkanregelung quota was doubled to 50,000 in mid-2024 — [Bundesagentur für Arbeit, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/presse/2026-07-fuenf-jahre-fachkraefteeinwanderungsgesetz)
- At the end of 2025, about 471,000 people held a temporary residence permit for employment; the Blue Card was the most common title — [Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Im-Fokus/Fachkraefte/Zuwanderung/_inhalt.html)
- Recognition of foreign qualifications in 2024: more than 79,000 approved (+20 percent). Nursing accounted for 32,500 (41 percent). By country of training, Turkey had about 9,200, Tunisia 5,300, India 4,900 and Syria 4,300 (Destatis data reported second-hand) — [tuenews, Jul 2026](https://tuenews.de/en/more-foreign-professional-qualifications-recognized/). BIBB counts applications instead (about 71,000 in 2024, 22,425 of them from nurses, with India, Tunisia, Turkey and the Philippines prominent) — [VISAGUARD.Berlin](https://www.visaguard.berlin/en/post/recognition-of-foreign-professional-qualifications-reaches-new-high)
- Philippines: 15,711 positive recognition outcomes for Filipino applicants between 2013 and 2022 (14.8 percent of all). OLDER data — [HAW Hamburg study 2024](https://reposit.haw-hamburg.de/handle/20.500.12738/17091?locale=en). The Triple Win programme has recruited more than 4,300 nurses from Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Philippines and Tunisia since 2013 — [CGD Global Skill Partnerships](https://gsp.cgdev.org/legalpathway/sustainable-recruitment-of-nurses-triple-win/)
- Students: about 402,000 international students in winter semester 2024/25, with a record 116,600 first-year students. India is the largest sender with just under 59,000 (+20 percent) and China second with about 38,600 — [DAAD press release, Wissenschaft weltoffen 2025](https://www.daad.de/en/press-releases/erneut-hohe-zahl-an-internationalen-studierenden-in-deutschland/); [ICEF Monitor Dec 2025](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/12/there-are-now-more-than-400000-international-students-in-germany/)
- Capacity bottleneck at Goethe: Goethe-Institut Pune runs an exam waiting list for 2025 sessions (A1 to B1 included). Goethe Addis Ababa puts all exam enrolments on a waiting list "due to very high demand". In Lahore, a Pakistani exam partner books by appointment only because slots are limited — [Goethe exam PDF 2025](https://goethe.de/resources/files/pdf340/website_exam-v2025.pdf); [Goethe Ethiopia](https://www.goethe.de/ins/et/en/m/spr/prf/anm.html); [Schimmel Lahore](https://www.schimmel.com.pk/examination-dates-fee-structure/)

### Inferences
- The strongest German markets for a prep course (A1 to B2 to visa level, not a certificate) are India, Turkey, Western Balkans (Kosovo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Albania, North Macedonia, Serbia), Tunisia, the Philippines, Uzbekistan and Egypt. Iran is large on paper but cannot be served with Stripe (sanctions), so it is excluded.
- A full Goethe waiting list is a selling point: "start A1 now online, sit the exam when a seat opens".

### Gaps
- Official 2025 visa counts by nationality (Fachkräfte, Ausbildung §16a, nurses §16d) were not found; the BAMF migration report for 2025 should be checked.
- Country figures for Turkey, Iran and Syria in Wissenschaft weltoffen 2025 were not retrieved.
- Size of the waiting lists at Goethe in Egypt, Vietnam and India: no numbers found.
- Austria and Switzerland: no data gathered.

## Italian demand: decreto flussi, students, citizenship B1, heritage learners

### Takeaway
Italy's 2026–2028 decreto flussi (about 497,550 entries across three years) reserves non-seasonal quotas for named countries including Albania, Bangladesh, Egypt, India, the Philippines and others. Iran, China, Turkey, India, Pakistan and Albania are the largest student sources. The B1 citizenship rule creates steady exam-prep demand among residents and spouses (mostly people already in Italy or married to Italians). Ancestry claims need no language level, and Law 74/2025 narrowed them.

### Cited Findings
- DPCM of 2 Oct 2025 (GU no. 240, 15 Oct 2025): 164,850 entries in 2026, 165,850 in 2027 and 166,850 in 2028, a total of 497,550. Non-seasonal work has a reserved quota of 25,000 a year for listed countries, including Albania, Algeria, Bangladesh, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Republic of Korea, Côte d'Ivoire, Ecuador, Egypt, El Salvador, Ethiopia, the Philippines, Gambia, Georgia, Ghana, Japan, Jordan, Guatemala, India and others. There is a separate quota for countries with migration agreements: 18,000 (2026), 26,000 (2027) and 34,000 (2028). The 2026 click day for non-seasonal work was 16 Feb 2026, and the employer applies, not the worker — [Infoimmigrazione guide](https://www.infoimmigrazione.com/decreto-flussi-2026-2028/); [Ascom Bologna, circolare 8047 of 16 Oct 2025 (PDF)](https://ascom.bo.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CIRCO-120-25_Nuovo-decreto-flussi-ingressi-2026-2028-e-circolare-interministeriale-8047-del-16-ottobre-2025.pdf); [QuiFinanza](https://quifinanza.it/lavoro/decreto-flussi-click-day-febbraio-2026/958046/)
- Study permits issued in 2024: Iran 3,083, China 2,557, Turkey 1,722, India 1,031 and Pakistan 833. ISTAT reports study visas fell 26.7 percent after three years of growth — [Orizzonte Scuola (ISTAT)](https://www.orizzontescuola.it/istat-litalia-non-attira-piu-gli-studenti-dopo-tre-anni-di-crescita-i-visti-per-studio-crollano-del-267/)
- University enrolment by citizenship (year not clearly stated): Iran 9,837, China 5,687, Turkey 4,939, India 4,066, Albania 2,971 — [Il Sole 24 Ore / Caritas-Migrantes](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/caritas-migrantes-over-121000-foreigners-enrolled-in-italian-universities-AG9LGla). OLDER (2021/22): 109,681 international students; top senders were Romania, Albania, China, Iran (7,224) and India (5,260) — same source family; also [Business Today India, Sep 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/nri/study/story/study-in-italy-expanding-english-taught-courses-attract-100000-international-students-495325-2025-09-23) (about 100,000 international students in 2024, many in English-taught courses)
- Citizenship by marriage (art. 5) or residence (art. 9) of Law 91/1992 requires Italian at level B1 or above, a rule in force since 4 Dec 2018. CILS B1 and CELI 2 are the main accepted certificates — [IIC New Delhi](https://iicnewdelhi.esteri.it/en/lingua-e-cultura/certificazioni/); [The Local Italy](https://www.thelocal.it/20241014/how-to-organise-your-language-test-for-italian-citizenship); [Europass Italian](https://www.europassitalian.com/language-exams/b1-for-citizenship)
- Citizenship by descent (iure sanguinis) has no language requirement. Law 74/2025 (the converted Tajani decree) limits automatic recognition to children and grandchildren of Italians born in Italy — [Universidade do Intercâmbio](https://www.universidadedointercambio.com/cidadania-italiana-nivel-de-italiano-exigido/); [Giambrone Law](https://www.giambronelaw.com/site/advice/gaining-citizenship/italian-citizenship/italian-laws/italian-passport-language-test/)

### Inferences
- Italian is a smaller, more niche product. The best fits are Albania (flussi list plus a large student flow, and Italian is widely understood), India and Pakistan (students; English-taught degrees still need everyday Italian), Egypt and Bangladesh (flussi plus diaspora), and the B1 citizenship exam market (spouses and residents in Italy plus spouses abroad). That last market is not country-bound: it is worldwide and mostly paid in EUR.
- Heritage learners in the USA and Australia: Law 74/2025 cuts ancestry-based passport demand, which removes one driver of "learn Italian before my citizenship trip". Treat it as cultural or hobby demand. No data was gathered.
- Iran is the largest Italian student source but cannot be served with Stripe.

### Gaps
- Official MUR (Ministry of University) 2024/25 enrolment by citizenship was not retrieved. Bangladesh student numbers were not found.
- Whether Law 74/2025 changed the B1 rule: no source confirms either way.
- Exact per-country flussi allocations (if any) and the seasonal list were not verified.
- Numbers of care workers (badanti) by origin: not researched.

## English demand: adults paying for English for migration and remote work

### Takeaway
UK study-visa data confirms India, China, Pakistan and Nigeria as the largest English-for-migration pools, but these markets are crowded with cheap local IELTS coaching and tutors from about $3 an hour. Data on English for remote work, the Gulf, East Asia and European adults was not gathered within this budget.

### Cited Findings
- UK study visas, year ending June 2025: China 99,919 (24 percent, down 7 percent), India 98,014 (24 percent, down 11 percent), Pakistan 37,013 (9 percent, up 9 percent). Nigeria had the steepest fall (down 25 percent), linked to the January 2024 ban on dependants. Work visas fell 48 percent year on year — [GOV.UK Immigration statistics YE June 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/immigration-system-statistics-year-ending-june-2025/why-do-people-come-to-the-uk-study); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/immigration/uk-student-visas-for-indians-drop-11-as-work-visas-fall-48-in-2025-125082200461_1.html); [ICEF Monitor Aug 2025](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/08/uk-visa-grants-strengthening-in-first-half-of-2025/). Conflicting figures: India 95,231 and Pakistan 30,781 — [BusinessDay NG](https://businessday.ng/news/article/uk-study-visas-for-nigerians-rise-to-30204-remain-among-top-four-sources/)
- Preply's German tutor filter runs from $3 to $40+ per lesson. Its intensive-German category pages show an average of about $37 per session (Toronto, London) — [Preply German tutors](https://preply.com/en/online/german-tutors?ref=hackernoon.com); [Preply Toronto](https://preply.com/en/Toronto-Canada/tutors-intensive-german). Average German tutor rate is about $29/h (Germany $29, Austria $30, Switzerland $33), according to a third-party estimate — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/how-much-do-German-tutors-charge/)

### Inferences
- English should not be the lead product in South Asia, the Philippines or Nigeria, where supply is very cheap. English is most promising where a European-run, small-group live format beats local options and incomes are higher: Gulf states, Turkey, Poland, and adults in Spain, Italy and France (all existing locales). This is unverified within this research.

### Gaps
- IRCC and Australian DHA statistics, IELTS test-taker volumes, and English course prices in Egypt, Turkey, Vietnam, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Poland, Spain, Italy and France were not gathered.
- Preply and italki English and Italian price ranges were not found.

## Per-country fact base (prices, wages, PPP, payments, competition)

### Takeaway
Only India has a verified local benchmark price: Goethe/Max Mueller Bhavan intensive courses cost INR 28,500 (about EUR 267), which works out to roughly EUR 1.5–1.8 per 45-minute unit. That is a very low ceiling for a premium online offer, so India must be positioned on speed and availability, not price. Egypt's card restrictions on foreign payments eased in 2024–2025. Indian recurring card payments are capped at INR 15,000 per charge without extra authentication.

### Cited Findings
- India, Goethe/Max Mueller Bhavan prices: Bangalore intensive (11–12 weeks × 15 units) and super-intensive (6 weeks × 25 units, A1–B1) are both INR 28,500. Mumbai super-intensive A1–B2 (about 7 weeks) is INR 28,500; Thane evening intensive (12 weeks) is INR 26,000. Pune is from INR 28,500. Pages are undated, and Delhi accepts card or RuPay payment in INR — [Goethe Bangalore](https://www.goethe.de/ins/in/en/sta/ban/kur/all.html); [Goethe Mumbai](https://goethe.de/ins/in/en/sta/mum/kur/all.html); [Goethe Pune](https://goethe.de/ins/in/en/sta/pun/kur/all.html)
  - ESTIMATE: INR 28,500 ≈ EUR 267.5 at 106.54. Bangalore super-intensive has 150 units of 45 minutes (about 112.5 hours), so about EUR 2.4 per 60-minute hour of group class. Intensive has about 165–180 units, so about EUR 2.0–2.2 per hour.
- India, payments: card e-mandates for recurring payments run without extra authentication up to INR 15,000 per transaction. Issuers must send a pre-debit notification 24 hours before each charge. International merchants were slow to adapt; more than 3,400 were registered — [Business Standard, 2022](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/finance/new-e-mandate-guidelines-rbi-enhances-limit-for-e-mandates-on-credit-debit-cards-to-rs-15-000-122060800417_1.html); [RBI notification](https://website.rbi.org.in/web/rbi/-/notifications/processing-of-e-mandates-for-recurring-transactions-12341) (2022 data; current status not re-verified)
- Egypt, payments: banks capped card spending abroad at USD 50–100 for regular customers in 2023. In Sep 2024, NBE and Banque Misr raised credit-card foreign-currency limits to USD 5,000. In 2025, AAIB raised its cap to USD 10,000, and NBE, CIB and Banque Misr cut FX markups from 5 percent to 3 percent (Aug 2025) — [Egypt Today](https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/141676/Travelers-gain-easier-access-to-foreign-currency-as-banks-relax); [CairoScene 2023](https://cairoscene.com/Buzz/Egypt-s-Banks-Tighten-Limits-for-Foreign-Currency-Credit-Card-Spending); [Mada Masr](https://www.madamasr.com/en/?p=354383)

### Inferences
- Pricing implication (INFERENCE): in India, Turkey, Egypt, the Philippines and Tunisia, the local institutional benchmark is EUR 2–4 per group hour. A EUR-priced subscription that is "cheaper than competitors" there would need country-level (PPP) pricing. Otherwise Alimun competes on immediate start, small groups, evening slots and nurse/Ausbildung-specific content rather than price.
- Iran (sanctions) cannot be served, and Syria is not practical for payments.

### Gaps (not gathered within budget — needs a follow-up pass)
- Local course prices and online tutor rates for Turkey, Egypt, Tunisia, the Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia, Uzbekistan, Kosovo/Albania/Bosnia, Pakistan, Kenya, Nigeria and the Gulf.
- Teacher pay per hour in each country; median wages (ILO/national statistics); World Bank PPP conversion factors.
- Stripe card acceptance and local wallets per country (Stripe can charge cards from most countries; local methods such as UPI, GCash, Fawry, iyzico and M-Pesa were not checked).
- Competition intensity (local Goethe partners, Sprachschulen, apps) by country.
- EUR conversion for EGP and VND (no ECB rate available).

## Ranked shortlist (synthesis — INFERENCE, based on the findings above)

1. **India** (German first, then Italian and English). Largest German student pool (about 59,000), top Chancenkarte nationality (more than 3,700), top-5 in academic skilled visas and nurse recognitions (4,900), Goethe waiting lists, and on the Italian flussi list. Risks: very low local prices (Goethe about EUR 2–2.5 per hour) and the RBI e-mandate rules for subscriptions.
2. **Turkey** (German). Number 1 for academic skilled visas, Berufspraktiker and recognitions (about 9,200 in 2024), number 3 for Chancenkarte, number 3 for Italian students. Turkish is not an existing site locale. TRY volatility (EUR = 51.28 TRY, Apr 2026).
3. **Western Balkans: Kosovo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Albania, North Macedonia, Serbia** (German; Italian for Albania). A quarter of employment-permit holders in Germany, a 50,000 a year quota, Kosovo top-5 for academic visas, Bosnia top-5 for Berufspraktiker and Triple Win nurses. Albania is on the Italian flussi list and number 5 for students in Italy.
4. **Egypt** (German, English, Italian). Named as a growth market in the 2025 DaF survey, on the Italian flussi list, and Arabic is an existing site locale. Card limits eased in 2024–25.
5. **Tunisia** (German for nurses, plus Italian). Number 2 for recognitions in 2024 (5,300), Triple Win country, and French/Arabic locales already exist. Italian flussi status was not verified.
6. **Philippines** (German for nurses and care, plus Italian care). Number 2 for Berufspraktiker visas, Triple Win, 15,711 recognitions 2013–22, on the Italian flussi list. English is already strong, so it is not an English market.
7. **Uzbekistan** (German). Top-5 for academic skilled visas and a named DaF mobility market. Payment rails were not checked.
8. **Pakistan** (German, Italian, English). Early Chancenkarte top-4, number 5 for Italian study permits (833), number 3 for UK study visas (37,013).
9. **Kenya** (German). Named DaF growth market; no hard numbers found.
10. **High-income diaspora and adults** (not ranked by data): Gulf states (South Asian, Egyptian and Filipino professionals preparing for Germany or Italy, with strong card acceptance), and EU adults learning German for work. No data gathered — a gap.

Excluded: Iran (largest Italian student source and a top-5 German visa source, but sanctions block Stripe), Syria, China (large, but payments and platform access are a separate problem and it is not in the brief).
