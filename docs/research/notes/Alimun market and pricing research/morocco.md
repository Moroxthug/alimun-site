# Morocco: market for live online English, German and Italian classes (2024-2026)

Conversion convention: **1 EUR = 11.0 MAD**. This is an approximation of the Bank Al-Maghrib reference rate in October 2026. BAM's reference was 10.998 MAD on 30 Sep 2026 and 11.162 MAD on 7 Oct 2026, and the dirham then gained 1.39% against the euro in the week of 8-14 Oct ([Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/dirham-decroche-euros-valent-desormais-dirhams,124265.html); [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/492911-change-le-dirham-sapprecie-face-au-dollar-et-a-leuro-9.html)). All EUR figures below are my conversions at 11.0, and all per-hour figures are my own arithmetic (price / hours) unless a source states them.

## 1. Demand drivers by language (emigration, study, jobs)

### Takeaway
German has the clearest paid, deadline-driven demand. It rests on a defined certificate requirement (B1 for Ausbildung and most skilled routes, B2 for nursing), a fast-growing German skilled-visa pipeline, and the 23,000 Moroccans already holding German work permits. Italian demand is real but smaller and less documented (decreto flussi plus around 1,500 student visas a year). English demand is broad (IELTS, jobs, studies), but Morocco's offshoring sector is about 80% French-facing.

### Cited Findings
**Germany**
- About 23,000 Moroccan nationals held a German residence title for employment or skilled work as of 30 April 2026. That puts Morocco in the top ten non-EU nationalities under this regime, level with Russia and Iran. The figure excludes other Moroccan residents. — [Le Brief, 19 Jun 2026, citing German federal data of 18 Jun 2026](https://www.lebrief.ma/immigration-professionnelle-23-000-marocains-actifs-en-allemagne-100156547/)
- Germany issued about 133,000 work and skilled-employment titles to all non-EU nationals in 2023, 157,000 in 2024 and 205,000 in 2025. The data is not broken down by nationality. — [Le Brief](https://www.lebrief.ma/immigration-professionnelle-23-000-marocains-actifs-en-allemagne-100156547/)
- In the 2025 microcensus, 37,000 Moroccans living in Germany said education was their main migration motive. That makes Morocco the 5th-largest source country, after India (98k), China (77k), Türkiye (52k) and Iran (43k). This is a stock figure, not an annual flow. — [Morocco World News, 20 Aug 2026, citing Destatis WISTA](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/08/335475/37000-study-migrants-rank-morocco-5th-among-germanys-source-countries/)
- Moroccan students at German universities: 8,261 in 2024/25 per official German statistics, the 3rd-largest African origin after Egypt and Tunisia; [Studying-in-Germany.org](https://www.studying-in-germany.org/germany-international-student-statistics/) gives 7,398 for WS 2024/25. The earlier figure was 7,045 in 2022/23. — [Africa Briefing](https://africabriefing.com/germany-draws-49328-african-students/); [Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2023/12/26202/munich-is-top-destination-for-moroccan-students-in-germany/). The sources conflict, so treat 7,000-8,300 as the range.
- Ausbildung visa requirements: German at B1 as a rule, and training pay of at least EUR 822 net per month (2026, per Make it in Germany). — [ijob.ma](https://ijob.ma/immigration/ausbildung-allemagne-2026-formation-professionnelle-visa-marocains-b1-remuneration/)
- Nursing typically requires B2 German; technical trades require about B1. This comes from commercial guides, not official sources. — [GoGermany](https://www.gogermany.ma/en/articles/64)
- Caritas recruited Moroccan nursing trainees through German language schools in Morocco, interviewing there in early 2023; the first cohort arrived in 2023. No headcount is given. Recruitment is expanding into hospitality and health care. — [Hespress EN](https://en.hespress.com/117867-germany-looks-to-morocco-to-help-fill-nursing-shortage.html)
- BIBB runs a German-Moroccan project on fair migration through recognition of qualifications. — [BIBB](https://www.bibb.de/en/218897.php)
- Goethe-Institut Marokko runs a dedicated "Vivre et travailler en Allemagne" pre-integration page, which signals institutional focus on the migrant learner. — [Goethe Marokko](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ma/fr/spr/led.html)

**Italy**
- The decreto flussi DPCM of 2 Oct 2025 (Gazzetta Ufficiale, 15 Oct 2025) sets 497,550 entries for 2026-2028: 164,850 in 2026, 165,850 in 2027 and 166,850 in 2028. Non-seasonal work gets 76,200 a year; seasonal work gets 88,000, 89,000 and 90,000. — [infoimmigrazione](https://www.infoimmigrazione.com/decreto-flussi-2026-2028/); [dequo](https://www.dequo.it/articoli/decreto-flussi)
- Part of the quota is reserved for countries with migration-cooperation agreements, and Morocco is among them. One source cites 43,000 reserved places in 2026 and 51,000 in 2027 for such countries combined, with no per-country breakdown. — [avvocatofrancescolombardini](https://www.avvocatofrancescolombardini.it/decreto-flussi/) (law-firm source, not the DPCM text)
- The joint circular of 16 Oct 2025 puts nulla osta for workers from Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka and Morocco under extra checks: issuance waits on a favourable opinion from the Ispettorato Nazionale del Lavoro. This is friction for Moroccan applicants. — [Ministero del Lavoro, Circolare 8047](https://www.lavoro.gov.it/documenti-e-norme/normativa/Circolare-8047-del-16102025)
- Over 1,500 Italian student visas were issued to Moroccans in 2024, per Italy's ambassador. An older (about 5-year-old) figure was around 2,200 Moroccans enrolling in Italian higher education each year. — reported via [InfoMigrants](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/67369/20-italian-academies-open-their-doors-to-moroccan-students) and search summary. The second figure is OLD, so flag it.

**English**
- Morocco's offshoring sector had about 148,500 stable jobs at end-2024, with a target of 270,000 by 2030. About 80% of call-centre business is tied to the French market. France's telemarketing ban, in force from 11 Aug 2026, puts an estimated 40,000-50,000 jobs at risk. — [Le360 EN](https://en.le360.ma/economy/50000-jobs-at-risk-as-frances-telemarketing-ban-hits-moroccan-call-centers_SOVLIMMTGFBEZARM74W323MAS4/); [Morocco World News, Sep 2026](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/09/336970/call-center-union-urges-moroccos-government-to-revise-offshoring-offer-protect-jobs/)
- The British Council is the only IELTS provider in Morocco (IDP does not run IELTS there), and all tests are now on computer. — [BandLadder, read 13 Oct 2026](https://bandladder.com/guides/ielts-exam-fee-in-morocco)

### Inferences
- The French telemarketing ban could push call-centre workers to reskill toward English or German markets, or toward emigration. This is speculative.
- German is the strongest paid-demand language: learners need a certificate level by a deadline (visa, Ausbildung contract, nurse recognition), so willingness to pay is driven by necessity. Italian is narrower. English demand is widest but faces the most cheap competition (tutors, apps, the British Council's 100 MAD/h).

### Gaps
- No official 2024 or 2025 count of German visas (national D-visas) issued in Rabat by category, nor annual Moroccan Ausbildung or nurse counts. The Auswärtiges Amt and BA statistics were not reached.
- No per-country decreto flussi allocation for Morocco, and no actual issuance numbers.
- No reliable count of Moroccans moving yearly to the UK, US or Canada, and no English-specific BPO hiring data for 2025.

## 2. Prices of competing courses (MAD and EUR, per hour)

### Takeaway
Institutional group courses in Morocco cost about **50-105 MAD per hour (EUR 4.5-9.5)**: Goethe is about 50-67 MAD/h, the British Council about 83-103 MAD/h, and IELTS prep about 117-133 MAD/h. Private tutors charge **100-300 MAD/h (EUR 9-27)**, and exam prep tutors charge up to 250-700 MAD/h. Goethe groups go up to 18 learners.

### Inferences
- Goethe's 50-67 MAD/h for groups of up to 18 is the price floor to beat for German. An online small group at, say, 40-55 MAD/h per learner (EUR 3.6-5) would undercut it. This is an estimate for modelling, not a market observation.
- An A1→B2 German path is about 4 levels × 60 h = 240 h. At Goethe that is about 12,000 MAD (EUR 1,090) in tuition before exams (my calc). Devoxa's "several tens of thousands of dirhams" claim does not match the Goethe price list.

### Gaps
- No price lists found for Dante Alighieri Morocco, AMIDEAST Morocco, ALC (American Language Center) or named private centres in Marrakech, Fès or Tangier. No telc, ÖSD or CILS/CELI prep course prices. No Avito tutor ads were sampled. Preply and italki listings for Morocco-based tutors were not sampled.
- IIC Rabat course hours are not found, so its per-hour price is unknown.

## 3. Exam fees (willingness-to-pay context)

### Takeaway
The certificate a learner needs costs about EUR 180-300. In other words, one exam sitting costs roughly as much as 2-4 months of Goethe tuition, and close to a month's minimum wage.

### Cited Findings
- Goethe-Zertifikat B1 (Morocco, prices from 1 Jan 2024): 2,500 MAD external (EUR 227) or 2,000 MAD for Goethe course participants (EUR 182); a single B1 module costs 1,000 MAD; rebooking costs 500 MAD. Fees can only be paid from Morocco. — [Goethe Marokko FAQ/prices](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ma/de/spr/prf/ogf.html); [Goethe B1](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ma/de/spr/prf/gzb1.cfm)
- Goethe B2: 2,800 MAD external (EUR 255) or 2,200 MAD for course participants (EUR 200); a module costs 1,000 MAD. C1 costs 3,200/2,500 and C2 costs 3,300/2,600 MAD. — [Goethe Beratung](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ma/de/spr/prf/anm.html)
- IELTS Academic or General on computer: 3,080 MAD (EUR 280); IELTS for UKVI 3,310 MAD (EUR 301); UKVI Life Skills 2,200 MAD (EUR 200), as of 13 Oct 2026. — [BandLadder](https://bandladder.com/guides/ielts-exam-fee-in-morocco)

### Gaps
- No Morocco-specific telc B1/B2, ÖSD, CILS or CELI fees were found.

## 4. Teacher pay in Morocco

### Takeaway
Hourly pay for language teachers at private centres is not published. The best anchors are a British Council Rabat teacher contract at 12,808-14,688 MAD gross per month, entry-level private and language-school teachers at 3,500-5,500 MAD net per month, and a 150 MAD/h private tutoring job ad. Public-sector pay averages about 10,100 MAD net per month (2026).

### Cited Findings
- British Council Rabat English teacher (1-year fixed-term contract): 12,808 MAD gross per month (EUR 1,164) or 14,688 MAD (EUR 1,335), depending on the number of monthly payments. — [British Council jobs](https://www.britishcouncil.ma/about/jobs/professeur-danglais-0)
- Beginner teachers in private schools and language schools earn 3,500-5,500 MAD net per month (EUR 318-500). — [Jobsquare blog](https://www.jobsquare.ma/blog/enseignant-anglais-maroc-salaire-carriere/) (blog source)
- An Indeed tutoring ad (not a language-centre post) pays 150 MAD/h (EUR 13.6). — [Indeed Maroc](https://ma.indeed.com/Maroc-emplois-Professeur-Priv%C3%A9-Salaire)
- Glassdoor "Teacher" Morocco: 16,500 MAD per year / 8 MAD per hour. This looks unreliable. — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.fr/Salaires/maroc-teacher-salaire-SRCH_IL.0,5_IN162_KO6,13.htm)
- Public teachers received a 1,500 MAD/month net raise in two tranches of 750 (April 2024, January 2025), covering about 330,000 Ministry of Education staff. — [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/407431-le-ministere-de-leducation-acte-la-seconde-tranche-daugmentation-pour-les-enseignants.html); [H24info](https://h24info.ma/maroc/education/education-le-gouvernement-et-les-syndicats-saccordent-sur-une-augmentation-generale-des-salaires-de-1500-dh/)
- The average net public-sector salary was set to reach 10,100 MAD per month (EUR 918) in early 2026. — [TelQuel, May 2025](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2025/05/01/le-salaire-net-moyen-dans-le-secteur-public-passera-a-10-100-dirhams-debut-2026_1930012/)

### Inferences
- A full-time teacher (about 80-100 teaching hours a month) earning 4,000-14,700 MAD a month implies about 40-180 MAD per teaching hour; private-centre staff are likely at the lower end. This is my estimate only. Paying Moroccan teachers 150-200 MAD/h (EUR 14-18) for online classes would likely be above local market. This is not verified by a pay survey.

### Gaps
- No published per-hour rates at Moroccan private language centres, Goethe or IIC freelancers. No German- or Italian-specific teacher pay. No starting salary for public teachers (échelle 10).

## 5. Purchasing power

### Takeaway
The minimum wage is about 3,300-3,400 MAD gross per month (EUR 300-310). Median pay is around 3,400 MAD (unverified), and the CNSS-declared private-sector average is about 5,900 MAD gross. A 500 MAD/month subscription (EUR 45) is about 15% of a minimum-wage income.

### Cited Findings
- SMIG was 17.10 MAD gross per hour from 1 Jan 2025 (decree 2.24.1122), about 3,266 MAD gross per month on 191 hours, or about 3,046 MAD net. — [smic-horaire-maroc.com](https://smic-horaire-maroc.com/)
- SMIG 2026 is 17.92 MAD per hour; SMAG is 97.44 MAD per day. — [DreamJob.ma](https://www.dreamjob.ma/droit-du-travail/smig-maroc/); [Humantal](https://humantal.ma/blog/smig-2026-maroc). My calculation: 17.92 × 191 = 3,423 MAD gross per month (EUR 311).
- Median salary is about 3,400 MAD and the all-sector average (formal and informal) about 4,193 MAD (attributed to HCP 2024). The CNSS private-sector average is about 5,871 MAD gross per month (2024). A 9,500 MAD "average" is also circulating. These come from aggregators and conflict with each other. — [clicpaie.ma](https://clicpaie.ma/blogs/salaire-moyen-maroc/); [tourdum.fr](https://www.tourdum.fr/salaire-moyen-maroc/)
- PPP conversion factor (GDP): 3.94 MAD per international $ (2020); private consumption 4.05 (2021). These figures are OLD. — [IndexMundi](https://www.indexmundi.com/facts/morocco/ppp-conversion-factor); [World Bank PA.NUS.PPP](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/PA.NUS.PPP?t=0)
- Nursing pay in Morocco is described as low by Moroccan trainees going to Germany. — [Hespress EN](https://en.hespress.com/117867-germany-looks-to-morocco-to-help-fill-nursing-shortage.html)

### Inferences
- With USD/MAD around 9.5-10, the PPP factor of about 4 implies that prices are roughly 40% of US levels. A EUR price therefore feels about 2.3-2.5× heavier to a Moroccan than to a German. This estimate uses old PPP data.

### Gaps
- No verified HCP 2024-2026 median income, and no current World Bank PPP value was retrieved. No budget survey for students, nurses or job seekers.

## 6. Payment realities

### Takeaway
Cards are widespread (22.6M in circulation), but 86% of card use is cash withdrawal. Online card payments are growing (44.8M operations in 2025). The key constraint for paying a foreign merchant is the e-commerce foreign-currency allowance: 15,000 MAD a year, raised to 20,000 MAD a year in 2026. Institutes still collect by bank transfer.

### Cited Findings
- There were 22.6 million bank cards in circulation in 2024 (+12%). Card operations totalled 192.5M (63 bn MAD), and point-of-sale payments 153.3M (52.2 bn MAD). Cash withdrawals still made up 86% of card transactions (88% the year before). — [EcoActu](https://ecoactu.ma/226-millions-de-cartes-bancaires-en-circulation/); [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/paiements-par-carte-1925-millions-doperations-17-en-un-an_XLZA5ZKHLVCMZOIZYRMYPIYAFE/); [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/paiements-cartes-bancaires-2024)
- Médias24's summary of the same 2024 report gives 252M approved transactions worth 110.7 bn MAD, a different scope. — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2025/11/25/chatbot-medias24-rapport-2024-bank-al-maghrib-marches-financiers-1583473/)
- Online card payments in 2025: 44.8M operations (+16%) worth 12.1 bn MAD (+10%). — [Le Brief](https://www.lebrief.ma/le-bilan-de-bank-al-maghrib-pour-2025-100170705/). Another count gives e-commerce at 16.3M (2024) → about 29M (2025), under a different definition. — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/articles/paiements-par-carte-au-maroc-operations-en-2025)
- The e-commerce foreign-currency allowance for resident individuals was raised from 15,000 to 20,000 MAD per calendar year by the IGOC 2026. Banks issue international cards against this allowance. — [Office des changes IGOC 2026 PDF](https://www.oc.gov.ma/sites/default/files/reglementation/pdf/2026-01/IGOC%202026.pdf); [Médias24, 5 Jan 2026](https://medias24.com/2026/01/05/operations-de-change-ce-qui-change-dans-la-nouvelle-instruction-1606301/); [Attijariwafa](https://www.attijariwafabank.com/fr/espace-media/actualites/votre-dotation-e-com-internationale)
- Goethe exam fees "can only be transferred from Morocco", and IIC fees are paid at a Crédit du Maroc branch. Local institutes collect locally by bank transfer. — [Goethe](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ma/de/spr/prf/gzb1.cfm); [IIC](https://iicrabat.esteri.it/it/lingua-e-cultura/i-corsi-di-lingua/)

### Inferences
- A foreign Stripe subscription charged in EUR draws on the learner's 20,000 MAD yearly e-commerce allowance. About 500 MAD a month (6,000 a year) fits, but the learner needs an internationally enabled card, and activation fees vary by bank. A local acquirer (CMI) or local transfer option would likely lift conversion. This is an inference, not measured.

### Gaps
- No data on CMI merchant volumes, mobile money (e.g. wallets) adoption, the share of adults with internationally enabled cards, or the subscription churn and willingness to pay of Moroccan consumers.

## 7. Which language has the strongest paying demand

### Takeaway
No direct measure (search volumes, enrolment counts) was found. Indirect evidence points to **German first** for paid, outcome-driven demand, **English** for broad volume but heavy price competition, and **Italian** as a niche.

### Cited Findings
- Germany's skilled permits to non-EU nationals rose from 133k (2023) to 205k (2025), and Moroccans hold about 23k work titles. — [Le Brief](https://www.lebrief.ma/immigration-professionnelle-23-000-marocains-actifs-en-allemagne-100156547/)
- A visible ecosystem of Moroccan German-prep sellers and agencies exists (Devoxa, GoGermany, goausbildung). — [GoGermany](https://www.gogermany.ma/en/visa/ausbildung); [goausbildung](https://goausbildung.com/blog/moroccos-hidden-gateway-why-german-companies-recruit-ausbildung-students-from-casablanca)
- Italian: decreto flussi places shared with other countries, Morocco under extra nulla osta checks, and about 1,500 student visas in 2024. — [Ministero del Lavoro](https://www.lavoro.gov.it/documenti-e-norme/normativa/Circolare-8047-del-16102025)

### Inferences
- German exam-prep tracks (B1 for Ausbildung, B2 for nurses) are likely the highest-value Morocco product. English IELTS prep is second (the British Council charges 117-133 MAD/h).

### Gaps
- No Ahrefs or Google search-volume comparison, no enrolment numbers per language at Goethe, the British Council or IIC, and no Avito ad counts.
