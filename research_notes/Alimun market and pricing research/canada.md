# Canada: market for live online French, Spanish and English classes (2025-2026)

Research date: 26 Oct 2026. **Currency convention:** EUR figures use 1 CAD = 0.622 EUR (OFX, 15 Jul 2026; reverse rate 1 EUR = 1.61 CAD on 20 Aug 2026). I found no October 2026 rate, so re-check with the Bank of Canada before modelling. Sources: [OFX CAD-EUR](https://www.ofx.com/en-au/exchange-rates/cad-to-eur/), [OFX EUR-CAD](https://www.ofx.com/en-us/exchange-rates/eur-to-cad/). EUR values are computed by me and rounded.

Method note: the fetch tool returned empty pages for several official sites (alliance-francaise.ca, quebec.ca). Some figures therefore come from search-engine extracts of those pages, or from third-party aggregators. Each one is marked "(third-party)" or "(unverified)" where that applies.

## 1. Demand drivers and size

### Takeaway
French for immigration is the strongest and fastest-growing paid driver. In 2025 and 2026 French-language Express Entry draws were the largest category, with about 42,000 invitations in 2025 and about 40,500 to 50,500 by autumn 2026. Outside Quebec, the federal target is about 30,000 francophone PR admissions a year, rising toward 35,000. English demand comes mainly from 380,000 PR admissions a year plus temporary residents who need CELPIP or IELTS. Temporary-resident inflows are being cut sharply, from 673,650 in 2025 to 385,000 in 2026. Spanish is a hobby and travel segment, and I found no hard size data for it.

### Cited Findings

- 2025 French-category draws reported: 19 Feb, 6,500 ITAs (CRS 428); 6 Mar, 4,500 (CRS 410); 21 Mar, 7,500 (CRS 379); 6 Oct, 4,500 (CRS 432); 28 Nov, 6,000 (CRS 408) — [Careerbrick](https://careerbrick.com/blog/ircc-issues-6-500-itas-in-first-french-language-express-entry-draw-why-choosing-this-category-is-the-smartest-route-to-pr-in-canada); [Canadim #339](https://www.canadim.com/news/express-entry-draw-339-4500-francophone-candidates-invited/); [Canadim #341](https://www.canadim.com/news/express-entry-draw-341-7500-francophone-candidates-invited/); [GA Immigration](https://gaimmigrationlawyers.com/express-entry-french-language-proficiency-draw-october-2025/); [Moving2Canada](https://moving2canada.com/news-and-features/news/immigration/latest-french-draw-november-28-2025/)
- French draws led all Express Entry categories in 2025 with about 42,000 ITAs. ### Inferences
- French draws give about 40,000 to 50,000 ITAs a year to people who already scored NCLC 7. The pool of people *trying* to reach that level is a multiple of this, so the TEF/TCF prep segment probably numbers in the tens of thousands to low hundreds of thousands, inside and outside Canada. This is an estimate with no source.
- Cuts to temporary residents shrink the inflow of students and workers, the core paying segment for English and Quebec French, from 2026. But people already in Canada on permits, trying to convert to PR, have a stronger reason than ever to get points from French.
- Because the PEQ closed or is in flux and the PSTQ scores French, Quebec temporary residents have a strong reason to improve their French. Many can use free Francisation Québec, though (section 2).

### Gaps
- No official count of TEF Canada, TCF Canada, CELPIP or IELTS test takers in Canada was found.
- The CRS French bonus (points by NCLC level) was not verified from IRCC.
- No data was found on the size of the Spanish adult-learner market in Canada (hobby, travel or heritage).
- No official number of federal positions designated bilingual, and no figure for annual spending on language training.

## 2. Free and subsidised competition, and who pays

### Takeaway
Free options (LINC/CLIC federally, Francisation Québec in Quebec) cover PRs and refugees across Canada and almost everyone living in Quebec, including many temporary residents. The paying segment is therefore: (a) temporary residents and citizens outside Quebec, who are not eligible for LINC; (b) people abroad preparing for Express Entry or Quebec; (c) anyone needing targeted exam prep (TEF/TCF/CELPIP) or flexible evening and online hours that free programmes don't offer; (d) hobby and heritage Spanish learners and SLE learners.

### Cited Findings
- LINC/CLIC (IRCC-funded) is aimed at adult permanent residents and protected persons. Citizens and temporary residents (students, visitors, work-permit holders) are generally excluded. People approved for PR who have not yet received their card can sometimes start — [Welcomeaide guide](https://welcomeaide.com/es/blog/linc-language-classes-newcomers-guide) (third-party; confirm on IRCC)
- Ukrainian temporary residents lost access to IRCC-funded LINC on 1 Apr 2025. Temporary residents affected by the Gaza crisis are eligible until 31 Mar 2027 — [Mohawk College](https://www.mohawkcollege.ca/node/37)
- Francisation Québec is free for anyone aged 16 or over living in Quebec or planning to settle there, including PRs, citizens, temporary workers, international students and asylum seekers. Some temporary-permit holders have conditional access — [vieauqc.com](https://vieauqc.com/en/guides/provincial/free-french-francisation-quebec) (third-party)
- Full-time Francisation students can receive an allowance, reported as $230 a week (attributed to Québec.ca, which I could not open); other guides say $185-200 a week — [vieauqc.com](https://vieauqc.com/en/guides/provincial/mifi-francisation); [Québec.ca news](https://www.quebec.ca/en/news/actualites/detail/updates-financial-assistance-programme-quebecois-apprentissage-francais-58040)
- More than 90,000 people used Francisation Québec services between 1 Apr 2024 and 31 Mar 2025, about 25% more than the year before. The waiting list fell from about 47,000 (Mar 2024) to 25,000 (Mar 2025) — [Québec.ca](https://www.quebec.ca/nouvelles/actualites/details/francisation-quebec-nombre-record-de-personnes-francisees-en-2024-2025-62491)
- On 28 Feb 2026 just over 6,000 people were waiting, against more than 33,000 a year earlier — [Québec.ca](https://www.quebec.ca/nouvelles/actualites/details/troisieme-annee-doperation-francisation-quebec-trouve-son-rythme-de-croisiere-69703)
- School-network funding: $104.4M (2023-24), $114.4M (2024-25), $119.4M (2025-26, 63 school service centres). In autumn 2024 there were course cuts and suspended enrolments, followed by an extra $10M in Dec 2024 for 5,000 more students — [Québec.ca $10M](https://www.quebec.ca/nouvelles/actualites/details/francisation-quebec-10-m-pour-soutenir-les-efforts-de-francisation-59979); [QCNA Oct 2024](https://qcna.qc.ca/learning-centres-suspend-new-francisation-enrolments/); [FAE brief](https://www.finances.gouv.qc.ca/ministere/outils_services/consultations_publiques/consultations_prebudgetaires/2025-2026/memoires/federation_autonome_de_lenseignement.pdf)
- Small employers (fewer than 100 staff) can get wage compensation for workplace francization — [hellodarwin](https://hellodarwin.com/business-aid/programs/financial-assistance-workplace-french-course)
- College continuing education is subsidised and cheap (see section 3: George Brown $342.81 for 45 h).

### Inferences
- Within Quebec, general French for residents competes with a free, now short-queue programme that even pays an allowance. Alimun's Quebec French offer should focus on people abroad preparing to move, exam-specific TEF/TCF/TEFaQ prep, and schedules the free system doesn't serve.
- Outside Quebec, temporary residents (about 385,000 new arrivals in 2026 plus the existing stock) and citizens cannot use LINC for French or English. This is the core paying segment in Canada.
- Express Entry hopefuls abroad are not eligible for any Canadian subsidy and are the clearest buyers of TEF/TCF prep.

### Gaps
- I could not open the official IRCC LINC eligibility page or the Québec.ca allowance page directly.
- No enrolment figures for LINC/CLIC (national learners per year) were found in this search.
- Francisation Québec's exact rules for temporary workers (which permit types) were not confirmed.

## 5. Teacher pay in Canada

### Takeaway
Job Bank median for a "language school instructor" is about **C$42/h** nationally (range C$21-62). LINC instructors in Ontario are posted at **C$36-42/h**, and college ESL has a median of about C$45/h. Marketplace tutors earn far less. The benchmark for "above market" in Canada is therefore around C$40-45/h for qualified instructors. That is far above the per-hour revenue of a C$10-15 group seat unless groups have 4 or more students.

### Cited Findings
- Job Bank, Language School Instructor (Canada): low C$21.00, median **C$42.56**, high C$62.37 (Nov 2023 update). A Dec 2024 update gives C$23.00 / **42.12** / 65.42 — [Job Bank Ontario page](https://ns.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/22921/ON); [Job Bank Alberta regional page](https://ab.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/22921/geo25350)

Regions: Toronto C$40.00, Ottawa C$46.00 — [Job Bank Ontario](https://ns.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/22921/ON); [Job Bank Toronto](https://qc.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/22921/geo9219); [Job Bank Vancouver](https://nb.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/22921/39070)

## 6. Purchasing power

### Takeaway
Median after-tax income is about C$75,500 per household unit (2024). Minimum wages range from C$15.00 (Alberta) to C$18.25 (BC). A typical newcomer on minimum wage earns about C$16-18/h gross, so one hour of a C$12-15 class costs close to one hour of work.

### Cited Findings
- Canadian Income Survey 2024 (released 29 Apr 2026): median after-tax income C$75,500 for economic families and unattached individuals; families C$108,900 (+1.8%); unattached individuals C$41,000; poverty rate 11.0% (about 4.5M people) — [StatCan Daily](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260429/dq260429a-eng.htm); [table](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260429/t001a-eng.htm)
- Minimum wages 2026: BC C$18.25 (from 1 Jun 2026); Ontario C$17.60 (from 1 Oct 2025), reportedly C$17.95 from 1 Oct 2026 (unverified); Quebec C$16.60 (from 1 May 2026, up from C$16.10); Alberta C$15.00 (unchanged since 2018); Manitoba C$16.40 from 1 Oct 2026; Nunavut C$19.75 (highest) — [Wealthnorth](https://wealthnorth.ca/income/average-income/minimum-wage/); [Retail Council](https://www.retailcouncil.org/quickfacts/minimum-wage-by-province); [CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/business/article/five-canadian-provinces-boost-their-minimum-wage-alberta-now-lowest/); [HR Reporter](https://www.hrreporter.com/focus-areas/payroll/which-5-provinces-are-increasing-minimum-wage-rates-in-october/394893)

### Inferences
- A monthly budget of about C$100-200 (about €62-124) for language classes seems realistic for working newcomers. For example, Lingoda's 12-class plan is about €119 (C$192) every 4 weeks. This is an estimate with no direct survey source.
- Quebec has lower minimum wages and free francisation, so price sensitivity for general French there is highest.

### Gaps

## 7. Quebec vs rest of Canada (summary)

### Takeaway
Quebec: free and now mostly unqueued Francisation Québec, open even to many temporary residents, plus the PSTQ (and a possibly revived PEQ) that rewards French. The paid market there is exam prep, people abroad and niche schedules. Rest of Canada: no free French for temporary residents or citizens, a strong Express Entry French incentive, and higher wages. This is the stronger paid market for French; English (CELPIP) and Spanish (hobby) are secondary.

### Cited Findings
- Francisation Québec served more than 90,000 people in 2024-25, with about 6,000 waiting in Feb 2026 — [Québec.ca](https://www.quebec.ca/nouvelles/actualites/details/troisieme-annee-doperation-francisation-quebec-trouve-son-rythme-de-croisiere-69703)
- French-speaking PR target outside Quebec: 30,267 (2026) — [IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/transparency/committees/cimm-nov-18-2025/francophone-immigration.html)
- Job Bank college-ESL median: Quebec C$48.61 vs BC C$41.03 — [Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/22996/ca)
- Minimum wage: Quebec C$16.60 vs Ontario C$17.60-17.95 and BC C$18.25 — [Wealthnorth](https://wealthnorth.ca/income/average-income/minimum-wage/)

### Inferences
- Target English-Canada temporary residents and abroad Express Entry candidates first for French. In Quebec, sell TEF/TCF/TEFaQ exam prep rather than general French.

### Gaps
- No Quebec-specific private-school price data was found (e.g. Montreal language schools), apart from McGill and Dawson prep.
