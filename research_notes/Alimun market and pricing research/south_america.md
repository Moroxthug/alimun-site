# South America: market for live online English and German classes (2025-2026)

Research date: 26 Oct 2026. Scope: South American countries with high emigration or work/study-abroad needs, for Alimun (live small-group and private online classes, monthly subscription, human teachers).

**Currency note (read first).** I could not retrieve a verified October 2026 exchange rate in this session (search returned only stale 2022-2023 BRL/EUR pages, e.g. [O Liberal](https://www.oliberal.com/economia/euro-hoje-qual-o-valor-do-euro-para-real-hoje-08-de-fevereiro-1.642870)). EUR conversions below therefore use **assumed indicative rates, NOT verified**, and are marked "(est.)": EUR 1 = BRL 6.2, COP 4,600, PEN 4.0, CLP 1,080, USD 1.16, ARS ~1,550 (ARS is especially volatile). The report writer should replace these with ECB / central-bank rates on the publication date.

**Research budget note.** This pass used ~18 tool calls; several key questions (competitor price pages, teacher pay outside Brazil, remote-work data, country-level migration flows other than Venezuela) remain thin and are listed under Gaps.

## 1. Emigration and demand: which countries, which destinations, which language

### Takeaway
Venezuela's diaspora (about 7.9 million abroad) is by far the largest emigrant population, but most of it now lives in Colombia, Peru, Brazil, Chile and Ecuador, so "Venezuela" as a target is really a diaspora spread across those host countries. Germany has a formal Bundesagentur für Arbeit (BA) programme recruiting nurses from Brazil (since 2019) and Colombia (since 2022), with language prep in the home country, making Brazil and Colombia the clearest German-language targets; English demand is broad region-wide, with lowest proficiency (largest need) in Brazil, Colombia and Ecuador.

### Cited Findings
**Venezuelan diaspora**
- An estimated 7.9 million Venezuelans were living abroad as of November 2025 — [EUAA](https://www.euaa.europa.eu/print/pdf/node/32013); R4V regional update — [ReliefWeb / R4V, Nov 2025](https://reliefweb.int/report/colombia/venezuelan-refugees-and-migrants-region-november-2025)
- 6,947,649 Venezuelans lived in Latin America and the Caribbean as of Feb 2026; Colombia hosts the most, followed by Peru, Brazil, Chile and Ecuador; outside the region the US and Spain host the largest populations — [ReliefWeb / R4V](https://reliefweb.int/report/colombia/venezuelan-refugees-and-migrants-region-november-2025)
- Caveat: R4V figures are host-government figures updated at irregular intervals — same source.
- Earlier figures: 7,891,241 (Dec 2024), 7.77 million (May 2024) — [NTN24](https://www.ntn24.com/noticias-actualidad/crisis-humanitaria-en-venezuela-ha-desplazado-a-7-1-millones-de-personas-segun-plataforma-r4v-401628); [UNHCR Situation Overview, Nov 2024](https://reporting.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2024-11/Venezuela%20-%20Situation%20Overview.pdf)

**Germany's recruitment of South American workers**
- BA programme "Pflegekräfte Lateinamerika": recruits nursing professionals from Mexico since 2018, **Brazil since 2019, Colombia since 2022**, based on agreements with official partner organisations; university-trained nurses are recruited with German employers, **given language preparation in the home country**, and accompanied through migration; recognition of the qualification follows in Germany — [BA brochure (search snippet; PDF now 404)](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/datei/ag-broschuerepflegefachkraeftelateinamerika_ba178277.pdf), reported also by [Nordkurier](https://www.nordkurier.de/politik/deutschland-wirbt-um-pflegekraefte-aus-lateinamerika-1763393)
- BA signed an arrangement with Brazil's nursing council **Cofen** covering selection, placement, language acquisition and recognition — [Nordkurier, 2023](https://www.nordkurier.de/politik/baerbock-und-heil-werben-in-brasilien-um-pflegekraefte-1665859)
- In 2023 (Minister Heil) fewer than 200 Brazilian nurses worked in Germany; BA judged recruiting **up to 700 per year** from Brazil feasible — same source (2023, older).
- A government answer cited by press: 236,000 foreign nursing professionals employed in Germany end-2021; in 2021, 2,109 nurses came from Brazil and 652 from Mexico (2021, older; the Brazil figure conflicts with Heil's "<200 working" and may count different things) — [Berliner Zeitung](https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/article/355643); see also [Bundestag Drucksache 20/8423](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/20/084/2008423.pdf)
- **Triple Win (GIZ/BA)** does NOT currently list Brazil or Colombia; its partner countries are Bosnia-Herzegovina, Philippines, Tunisia, Indonesia, Kerala (India), Jordan, plus Vietnam (training track) — [GIZ](https://www.giz.de/en/worldwide/41533.html); [deutschland.de](https://deutschland.de/en/topic/business/international-skilled-professional-programmes-labour-market-entry)
- Colombia (with Mexico) is covered by a doctors' programme "Specialized!" supporting recognition in Germany — [deutschland.de](https://deutschland.de/en/topic/business/international-skilled-professional-programmes-labour-market-entry)
- **Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)**: allows non-EU nationals with a degree or recognised vocational qualification to stay 12 months to search for work, with part-time work up to 20 h/week — [German Embassy Wellington](https://wellington.diplo.de/nz-en/service/-/2659152); Colombian business press covered it for Colombian readers — [Portafolio](https://www.portafolio.co/internacional/alemania-abre-la-puerta-a-trabajadores-sin-contrato-asi-puede-solicitar-la-opportunity-card-y-buscar-empleo-499238)

**English proficiency (proxy for need)**
- EF EPI 2025: Argentina ranks 26th of 123 with 575 points, best in Latin America — [Mercado](https://mercado.com.ar/noticias/argentina-alcanza-el-puesto-26-en-el-indice-de-proficiencia-en-ingles-de-ef-education-first); [Diario de Cuyo](https://www.diariodecuyo.com.ar/argentina/argentina-es-el-pais-con-mejor-nivel-de-ingles-de-la-region-1802634.html)
- Uruguay scored 542 — [El Salvador in English](https://elsalvadorinenglish.com/2025/11/27/2025-ef-index-el-salvador-strengthens-its-position-as-a-regional-leader-in-english-proficiency/)
- Costa Rica (516) is above only Panama, **Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador** and Mexico in the region, so Brazil, Colombia and Ecuador score below 516 — [Delfino.cr](https://delfino.cr/2025/11/ef-epi-2025-evidencia-caida-del-ingles-en-costa-rica-estudiantes-con-el-nivel-mas-bajo-del-pais)

### Inferences
- **German:** Brazil and Colombia are the only South American countries with an active BA nursing pipeline; candidates must reach German before departure (the BA does home-country prep). Typical German nursing recognition requires B1-B2 (commonly B2 for recognition) — I did not retrieve a 2025 primary source confirming the level, see Gaps. This creates paying demand for B1/B2 prep among nurses and for Opportunity Card applicants (A1/B1 German earns points).
- **English:** Brazil, Colombia and Ecuador combine low proficiency with large populations; Argentina has high proficiency (a market for advanced/exam-prep and remote-work English, not beginners).
- **Venezuela:** target the diaspora where it lives (Colombia, Peru, Chile, Ecuador, Brazil, Spain, US), not Venezuela itself (payments and currency make in-country selling hard; see section 4).
- Ranking by language pair (estimate, judgement from the above): (1) Brazil-English, (2) Colombia-English, (3) Brazil-German, (4) Colombia-German, (5) Peru-English (incl. Venezuelans in Peru), (6) Chile-English, (7) Argentina-English (advanced/remote work) and Argentina-German (heritage/EU passport demand, unverified).

### Gaps
- Official 2024-2025 emigration flows for Brazil, Colombia, Peru, Ecuador, Argentina (UN DESA / OECD / national data) were not retrieved.
- No nationality breakdown of Chancenkarte issuances found ([Fintiba](https://www.fintiba.com/es/opportunity-card-germany) and similar are guides only). BAMF/Auswärtiges Amt would have it.
- No 2025 count of Brazilian/Colombian nurses placed by BA; the brochure PDF returned 404.
- Required German level for nurse recognition and Ausbildung not confirmed with a primary 2025 source.
- No data retrieved on USD-paid remote work, call-centre English demand (Colombia, Peru) or student visas to US/Canada/UK/Australia.
- Brazil and Colombia exact EF EPI 2025 scores not retrieved (EF page loads them dynamically).

## 2. Competitor prices (local currency and EUR, per hour where possible)

### Takeaway
Hard, current price data was scarce in this pass. The only usable figures are Wizard Brazil (~BRL 2,160 per semester, undated) and Preply's regional range (USD 12-25/h for Latin American tutors, USD 10-40/h for English generally). Goethe-Institut São Paulo runs 8-16 student groups and charges with no enrolment fee and instalments, but its price table was not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- **Wizard (Brazil):** a blog cites English course at **BRL 2,160 per semester**, materials extra; date unclear — [auditorioibirapuera.com.br](https://www.auditorioibirapuera.com.br/quanto-e-a-hora-aula-na-wizard/). ≈ BRL 360/month ≈ EUR 58/month (est., assuming 6-month semester). Hours per week not given.
- **Goethe-Institut São Paulo:** formats are extensive 16 weeks (1-2x/week), intensive 8 weeks (2x/week), super-intensive 4 weeks (5x/week); **groups of 8-16 students**; no enrolment fee; instalments allowed, last instalment due by final class day; bank fees borne by student; adults 15+ — [Goethe-Institut Brasil, 2026 general conditions](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf359/condicoes-gerais-para-cursos-2026_2107262026.pdf); [Goethe SP course page](https://www.goethe.de/ins/br/pt/sta/sap/dku/sta.html). Enrolment open for 1st semester 2026 online and in person — [goethe.de/ins/br](https://www.goethe.de/ins/br/pt/index.html)
- **Preply:** English averages roughly USD 25/h for native speakers; English generally USD 10-40/h — [learnenglish.life review, updated 2026](https://learnenglish.life/reviews/preply/); [Preply blog](https://preply.com/en/blog/english-tutor-rates/). "Eastern Europe / Latin America: USD 12-25 per hour" — [onlinecourseing.com](https://onlinecourseing.com/preply-cost/) (affiliate site). Preply Brazil English page shows price filter USD 3-40+ per lesson and sample tutors at USD 35-38 per 50-min — [Preply Brazil](https://preply.com/en/Brazil-IN/english-tutors). Preply bills subscriptions every 28 days — same review sources.
  - USD 12-25/h ≈ EUR 10-22/h (est.).
- **Open English:** monthly-subscription model with automatic renewal confirmed (2016 complaint; one charge of USD 72.88) — [Las2orillas](https://www.las2orillas.co/?p=177623). No current price found.

### Inferences
- Wizard at ~EUR 58/month for a group course (likely 1-2 sessions/week, so roughly 8-12 class hours/month) implies ~EUR 5-7 per group class hour (estimate). This is the bar Alimun must beat on per-hour price in Brazil.
- Preply private lessons at EUR 10-22/h set the ceiling for private classes; Alimun's small-group hour should sit well below that.

### Gaps
- No current prices retrieved for Open English, English Live (EF), CNA, Fisk, Berlitz, Cultura Inglesa, Colombo Americano, ICPNA, Goethe Bogotá/Lima/Santiago/Buenos Aires, Cambly, italki. These need direct price-page checks (many show prices only after a sales call).
- Exam-prep course prices (without exam fee) not found.

## 3. Teacher pay

### Takeaway
Only Brazil data was found: English teachers at franchise schools earn roughly BRL 20-29 per class hour (≈ EUR 3.2-4.7, est.), with monthly pay around BRL 2,300-3,800 and job ads up to BRL 3,500. Paying "above market" in Brazil is therefore cheap in EUR terms.

### Cited Findings
- Wizard average hourly pay ≈ **BRL 20-28**; CNA ≈ **BRL 22-29** (salary-aggregator data) — [auditorioibirapuera.com.br (CNA)](https://www.auditorioibirapuera.com.br/quanto-o-cna-paga-por-hora-aula/); [(Wizard)](https://www.auditorioibirapuera.com.br/quanto-ganha-um-professor-do-wizard/). ≈ EUR 3.2-4.7/h (est.).
- Monthly: CNA English teacher average BRL 3,768 (range 1,195-6,300); Wizard average BRL 2,320 (range 870-10,295) — same aggregator sources; wide ranges indicate weak samples.
- Wizard job ads in Minas Gerais (Feb 2025) up to **BRL 3,500/month** — [Click Petróleo e Gás](https://en.clickpetroleoegas.com.br/wizard-anuncia-vagas-de-emprego-com-salario-de-ate-r-3-500-para-professor-de-ingles-sima00/)
- Language-school pay is tied to weekly hours, up to ~BRL 6,000 in hot markets such as Rio — [Guia da Carreira](https://www.guiadacarreira.com.br/blog/quanto-ganha-um-professor-de-ingles)
- Wizard São Paulo ads are for "horista" (hourly) instructors — [Jooble](https://br.jooble.org/jdp/-9188952672059492263)

### Inferences
- An Alimun rate of, say, BRL 40-50/h (≈ EUR 6.5-8, est.) would be ~1.5-2x Brazilian franchise-school pay. German teachers are scarcer and likely higher-paid (unverified).

### Gaps
- No teacher-pay data for Colombia, Peru, Chile, Argentina, Ecuador, nor for German teachers anywhere in the region; no Goethe local-teacher pay.

## 4. Purchasing power, payments and Stripe

### Takeaway
2026 minimum wages range from about USD 250 (Argentina) to about USD 600 (Chile). Stripe officially supports only **Brazil** (and Mexico) as merchant countries in Latin America; Alimun as a European Stripe account can still charge cards from anywhere, but local methods matter: in Brazil, Pix took 42% of e-commerce value in 2025 and credit cards 40%, driven by instalments (parcelamento).

### Cited Findings
**Minimum wages 2026**
- Brazil: **BRL 1,621/month** from 1 Jan 2026 (Decree 12.797/2025), +6.79% vs BRL 1,518 in 2025; BRL 7.37/hour — [SBT News](https://sbtnews.sbt.com.br/noticia/brasil/lula-assina-decreto-que-estabelece-salario-minimo-em-r-1-621-a-partir-de-1-de-janeiro-de-2026); [Band](https://www.band.com.br/economia/noticias/valor-salario-minimo-2026-reajuste-r-1621-202512101209). ≈ EUR 261 (est.)
- Colombia: **COP 1,750,905/month** from 1 Jan 2026 plus transport allowance COP 249,095; another source gives COP 1,746,882 + 253,118 (same total COP 2,000,000), +23.7% — [Rivermate](https://rivermate.com/es/blog/salario-minimo-en-america-latina-2026); [El Universal](https://www.eluniversal.com.co/colombia/2025/12/30/ranking-del-salario-minimo-en-latinoamerica-en-2026-en-que-puesto-quedo-colombia/). ≈ EUR 380 (est.)
- Peru: **PEN 1,130/month**, unchanged since 2025 (single source) — [Rivermate](https://rivermate.com/es/blog/salario-minimo-en-america-latina-2026). ≈ EUR 283 (est.)
- Chile: **CLP 539,000/month** (18-65) from 1 Jan 2026 — [Bloomberg Línea](https://bloomberglinea.com/economia/salario-minimo-2026-listado-de-los-mas-fuertes-y-debiles-de-latinoamerica/). ≈ EUR 499 (est.)
- Ecuador (dollarised): **USD 482/month** (SBU 2026), +USD 12 — [Finanzas Digital](https://finanzasdigital.com/salario-minimo-2026-america-latina/). ≈ EUR 416 (est.)
- Argentina: stepped, **ARS 341,000 rising to ARS 376,600 by 1 Aug 2026**; USD estimates USD 233-247 depending on rate — [Rivermate](https://rivermate.com/es/blog/salario-minimo-en-america-latina-2026)
- USD ranking: Chile USD 594, Colombia USD 539.6, Ecuador 482, Peru 332, Argentina 247 — [El Universal](https://www.eluniversal.com.co/colombia/2025/12/30/ranking-del-salario-minimo-en-latinoamerica-en-2026-en-que-puesto-quedo-colombia/)

**Payments**
- Stripe's official global page lists **Brazil and Mexico** as the only supported Latin American business countries; Colombia, Peru, Chile, Argentina, Ecuador, Uruguay are not listed — [Stripe Global](https://stripe.com/global) (fetched 26 Oct 2026)
- Brazil 2025: **Pix 42%** of e-commerce value, credit cards 40% (supported by parcelamento), Pix projected 44% by 2030 — [Poder360, citing Global Payments Report 2026](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-economia/pix-chega-a-42-dos-pagamentos-on-line-no-brasil/); a different measure gives 38% of transactions — [Letsmoney](https://www.letsmoney.com.br/pagamentos/pix-ecommerce-38-global-payments-report/)
- Brazilian merchants commonly offer interest-free instalments of 3-12x — [Antom Brazil market page](https://www.antom.com/markets/brazil)
- Goethe-Institut Brazil itself sells courses in instalments — [Goethe conditions 2026](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf359/condicoes-gerais-para-cursos-2026_2107262026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Alimun's Stripe account (EU) can accept international cards from all these countries, but buyers pay FX/IOF and cross-border fees and many cards in Argentina/Venezuela are restricted (unverified). Stripe offers Pix in some configurations (not confirmed here); otherwise Mercado Pago/dLocal/EBANX-type local acquirers are needed for Pix, PSE/Nequi (Colombia) and Yape (Peru).
- A monthly subscription priced at ~5-10% of the minimum wage in Brazil (BRL 80-160) or Colombia (COP 90,000-175,000) is a plausible mass-market band (estimate, not data).
- Ecuador's dollarisation and Venezuelans abroad earning in host currencies reduce FX risk; Argentina carries the highest currency risk.

### Gaps
- Average/median wages, PPP conversion factors (World Bank), inflation 2025-2026 and card penetration by country not retrieved.
- Stripe Pix / local-method availability for an EU-based account not confirmed; PSE/Nequi/Mercado Pago data not retrieved.
- Verified EUR exchange rates for Oct 2026 not retrieved (see currency note).
